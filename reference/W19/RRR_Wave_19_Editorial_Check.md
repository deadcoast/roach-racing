# RRR — Wave v0.19
## Editorial Integrity and Evidence Boundary

**Edition:** 0.19.0 · **Date:** 12 September 2026.

This is a researched compendium extension, not a prototype release. The checks below concern document navigation, structured-reference agreement, explicit relationships and preservation. No race simulation, breeding trial, economic model, player session or retention experiment was run.

## Current definition review

The reference contains 115 addressable records: six settlements, four proposed civic factions, nine charter entries, seven meeting templates, seven city areas, eight route connections, three new craft roles, three dated case histories plus their time-frame definition, three scope papers, ten physical-object briefs, twelve amendments, eighteen editorial cases, twenty existing-source route entries and the shared introductory/service definitions.

The eighteen cases are written situations with required interpretations. They are not presented as executed gameplay tests. In particular, the review separates the qualified lanista, the host's history, the inspected build and the faction delegation. It retains optional buying, catching, breeding, display and local racing; it does not add faction power bonuses or an exclusive-species system.

## Changes made before delivery

The four-entry series uses four races, not three, so each entrant can occupy each of the four starting positions once. The reference still requires review of layout-position interactions rather than treating rotation as proof of perfect fairness. Single-final start assignment and unresolved finish ties are separately defined. A series qualification needs actual valid finishes. A final that began with a competitive field can have one valid finisher; merely being alive is not a finish.

A Factional roster requires enough distinct Regional qualifiers. Repeated wins cannot clone one lanista into several places. Sponsored journeys require real booked support; a cancelled carrier does not turn an earned berth into a compulsory unrelated quest. Ordinary city visitors need no world-grid entry.

Absolute-scale recommendations and recovery alternatives carry `proposal_not_adopted` and `adoption_required: true` in the structured records. The proposed civic factions and newly dated histories are also explicitly labeled. The preserved D01/D02 decisions and past world records are not rewritten.

## Limits that remain substantive

The four factions are a bounded authored reference region, not a count of every faction on the planet. The atlas is topological, not a measured geographical map. M4 is a proposed programme, not a demonstrated balance solution. No prices, travel times, support budgets or staffing ratios are represented as economically calibrated. Biosphere recovery, global life-course policy and absolute scale remain separate author decisions.

Human comprehension, meaningful regional identity, enjoyable racing, affordable repeated travel, faction appeal, and voluntary return require later evaluation. The MIVIM hard quality requirement is not replaced by record counts, link counts or an archive hash.
