## A separate supporting organism

[W18.SUPPORT.BANKSEED](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-support-bankseed) is a short authored developmental-host supply profile. It is not counted as an additional complete racing-donor monograph, a new race part, or a substitute for the required compatible stage. Its role is to make Caustic production intelligible while ready Boiler buying stays accessible.

## Three useful reading paths

**I want the faster racer:** begin with the relevant component function, inspect the actual current limitation and choose bought, caught, bred or refitted anatomy accordingly. Stronger source tissue need not solve a clearance or control failure.

**I want a productive line:** read the reproductive unit, useful stage, ordinary products and bounded replacement plan before pricing output. A living founder, harvested component and future cohort are separate assets.

**I want the finished project:** choose a complete kit whose contents and ordinary support cover the intended first use. The supplier’s complete life cycle is reference depth, not a task list every entrant must perform.
