# RRR — Wave v0.19
## Named Interpretation Amendments

**Status:** researched, revisable compendium definitions. Author premises govern; W15 participation principles and the established anatomical meanings remain current except for explicitly named refinements. New factions, geographic links, histories, formats and scale proposals are authored fiction. No application or historical result is modified.

### Qualifications, hosts and builds

`W19.AMEND.ENTRY_RIGHT`

**Earlier ambiguity.** Older ladder descriptions could attach advancement ambiguously to a person, a host or a component list.

**Current proposal.** The responsible lanista/team owns the earned campaign entry; the animal retains its own historical results; each heat freezes a build. Sale transfers the animal, not the previous lanista’s berth.

**Related reference:** [W19.CHARTER.ENTRY](RRR_Wave_19_Sporting_Charter.md#w19-charter-entry)

<a id="w19-amend-affiliation"></a>

### Representation without a profession or gene lock

`W19.AMEND.AFFILIATION`

**Earlier ambiguity.** Faction representation could become a prerequisite to ordinary racing or to acquiring the desired insect.

**Current proposal.** Ordinary local Open remains available. Campaign affiliation is declared for representation; it changes support and identity, not species access or component physics.

**Related reference:** [W19.CHARTER.AFFILIATION](RRR_Wave_19_Sporting_Charter.md#w19-charter-affiliation)

<a id="w19-amend-travel"></a>

### A supported earned invitation

`W19.AMEND.TRAVEL`

**Earlier ambiguity.** An earned world seat could leave a winner with no achievable route unless they complete unrelated work.

**Current proposal.** Advertise and secure a practical support floor before the qualifier. Optional extra work and premium stock remain separate choices.

**Related reference:** [W19.CHARTER.SUPPORT](RRR_Wave_19_Sporting_Charter.md#w19-charter-support)

<a id="w19-amend-city"></a>

### City access versus final-grid access

`W19.AMEND.CITY`

**Earlier ambiguity.** New World City could be treated as entirely inaccessible to nonchampions.

**Current proposal.** Public trade, work, display and local clubs permit nonqualified visitors; the Derby grid requires the earned faction seat.

**Related reference:** [W19.REGION.CITY](RRR_Wave_19_Regional_Atlas.md#w19-region-city)

<a id="w19-amend-capability"></a>

### One class label is not the whole event

`W19.AMEND.CAPABILITY`

**Earlier ambiguity.** Open, Flyer, regional and veteran have sometimes stood next to one another as if the same dimension.

**Current proposal.** Separate locomotion, equipment policy, competitive tier, format and participation conditions. Ground and aerial results cannot be added into one unexplained global time.

**Related reference:** [W19.CHARTER.CLASSES](RRR_Wave_19_Sporting_Charter.md#w19-charter-classes)

<a id="w19-amend-format"></a>

### A solver is not a sporting civilization

`W19.AMEND.FORMAT`

**Earlier ambiguity.** A historical two-roach solver or three sponsored cards could harden into the only world format.

**Current proposal.** Define intended pack racing and edition-based schedules independently; preserve historical probes without claiming new executable physics.

**Related reference:** [W19.CHARTER.FORMAT](RRR_Wave_19_Sporting_Charter.md#w19-charter-format)

<a id="w19-amend-faction"></a>

### Civic factions are not renamed vendors

`W19.AMEND.FACTION`

**Earlier ambiguity.** Local organizations could become all-powerful regional governments through an atlas label.

**Current proposal.** Two Nets, Cinderhouse Broodworks, the Saltglass Counter, Foundry and the Case Runners retain separate assets and roles under the proposed civic connections.

**Related reference:** [W19.LAYERS](RRR_Wave_19_Foundations.md#w19-layers)

<a id="w19-amend-home"></a>

### Local familiarity is not an invisible multiplier

`W19.AMEND.HOME`

**Earlier ambiguity.** A regional kit or resident could gain generic home-course power.

**Current proposal.** Local knowledge and available preparation can matter through actual information and resources. The same fitted anatomy obeys the same mechanism away from home.

**Related reference:** [W19.SCOPE.CULTURE](RRR_Wave_19_Scope_Proposals.md#w19-scope-culture)

<a id="w19-amend-display"></a>

### Display awards have a complete independent role

`W19.AMEND.DISPLAY`

**Earlier ambiguity.** Appearance recognition might be treated as racing qualification or obliged to pay cash.

**Current proposal.** Display work has a real object, criteria and audience. Its result stays separate; any prize or collector transaction requires actual funding.

**Related reference:** [W19.CITY.DISPLAY](RRR_Wave_19_Sporting_Charter.md#w19-city-display)

<a id="w19-amend-history"></a>

### A new history is authored, not recovered evidence

`W19.AMEND.HISTORY`

**Earlier ambiguity.** A dated case or original guide might be mistaken for previously verified in-world events.

**Current proposal.** H-year case histories are explicit new fiction. Original objects, later interfaces, lineages and replicas are separately identified; no prototype winner is reassigned.

**Related reference:** [W19.HISTORY.FRAME](RRR_Wave_19_Supply_and_Heritage.md#w19-history-frame)

<a id="w19-amend-map"></a>

### A route graph is not a completed global map

`W19.AMEND.MAP`

**Earlier ambiguity.** Adjacency could be mistaken for miles, directions, borders or exclusive ecological ownership.

**Current proposal.** This atlas states useful connections and service nodes. Distances and specific global geography follow an adopted scale and map study.

**Related reference:** [W19.LAYERS](RRR_Wave_19_Foundations.md#w19-layers)

<a id="w19-amend-priority"></a>

### Later specificity is not blanket overwrite

`W19.AMEND.PRIORITY`

**Earlier ambiguity.** More recent compendium prose could silently overrule W15 participation or author class definitions.

**Current proposal.** Named W19 refinements apply only to their subjects. Explicit author premises, W15 optional depth and the W14–W18 anatomy remain current elsewhere.

**Related reference:** [W19.PREMISE](RRR_Wave_19_Foundations.md#w19-premise)
