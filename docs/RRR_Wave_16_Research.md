# RRR — Wave v0.16

## Research register and inference limits

**Method:** source-hobby documentation establishes functions; original studies and attributed life-history references supply narrow biological anchors. The adjacent RRR mechanism is an authored translation. Search-rendered text is labeled where direct retrieval failed. Subscription methods, videos and numerical re-analyses are not claimed inspected.

No source validates universal insect graft compatibility, prepared-root survival, RRR anatomy, economic coefficients, exact breeding rates or player retention. The author’s mutation premise permits the fictional world; the research prevents avoidable anatomical and life-history conflations.

## The evidence boundary in practice

A nymphal gear source supports making Clockleaf’s useful stage distinct from adulthood. It does not prove that a removed real gear can live in a roach. A honey-bee developmental study supports treating rearing and caregivers as relevant. It does not justify an inherited +speed bonus for buying a better tank. A Mini 4WD kit supports an attainable coordinated assembly project. It does not prescribe RRR inventory, prices or compulsory services.
