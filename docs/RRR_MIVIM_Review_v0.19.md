# MIVIM — Wave v0.19 Contribution Review

**Objective:** Give the living kit-racing hobby a coherent regional, civic and competitive setting whose source work, ordinary participation and personal builds remain useful at every level.

**Comparator:** Four palette-swapped factions granting stat bonuses; a world-city lock barring nonracers; qualification transferred by buying a winning roach; an earned invitation that secretly requires unrelated work; or an administrative expansion without concrete local kits, sources and venues.

**Hard admission:** quality at the stated compendium scope AND purposeful novel-development adherence. No authorized numeric weights, score bands or expanded MIVIM acronym are invented.

| Criterion | Current contribution | Evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | Named institutions, source relationships, entry-right distinctions, real support commitments, coherent meeting formats and physically legible objects are authored. | Human comprehension, complete game implementation, affordability and final world cohesion. |
| Novel-development adherence | Civic identity changes support and lived hobby culture while the same tissue remains the same performance object. Representation connects to actual growers, makers, carriers and recipients without a faction stat layer. | No global prior-art or player-perceived uniqueness claim. |
| Effectiveness | The reference answers who qualifies, what is credited to the animal, how a delegate travels and why others visit the city. | Played qualification/travel sequences and user feedback; no runtime produced. |
| Creativity | Four distinct public cultures, complementary material trade, a parallel display scene and particular history objects extend the existing insect hobby. | Whether people find the factions memorable rather than bureaucratic. |
| Scaling potential | Stable IDs, a flat twenty-source route index and separate region/faction/event records allow more communities without cloning biology. | Large-world retrieval, autonomous markets, schedule performance and human engagement remain unmeasured. |

## Corrections made before closing

The series example was changed from three races to four so four entrants can each occupy the four start positions once. Alternating layouts still requires review for course-position interactions; rotation is not proof of perfect fairness. Enough distinct Regional winners are explicitly required before a Factional roster is formed. Multiple schedule and body commitments cannot duplicate a person, host or loaned part. Source-route hubs are receiving connections, not newly exclusive natural habitats.

## Not upgraded into facts

The four factions and new adjacencies are authored proposals; the scale-study recommendation and recovery alternatives are not adopted canon. Dated H-year histories are new fiction, not old simulated events. No numerical races, biological experiments, economic sustainability studies or retention tests were run. Structural checks support navigation and preservation only.
