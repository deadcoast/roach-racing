## Developed foundation statements

### The same specimen becomes the racing part

**Origin:** source_hobby_translation. **Refines:** W03.TRACE.02, W03.PERFORMANCE.08.

**W04.BIND.01** — Compile a racer from one host, actual installed component identities, native anatomy, preparation and a saved setup.
Decision: Fit parts, then enter that configuration. Consequence: Purchase alone does not change racing performance.

**W04.BIND.02** — An acquired part only changes a build after installation; acquisition history grants no hidden multiplier.
Decision: Buy, catch or breed a useful donor. Consequence: Equivalent finished anatomy performs equivalently under equal conditions.

**W04.BIND.03** — A drive contributes cadence and useful force; leverage changes their delivery rather than adding a second motor.
Decision: Choose drive stock and hip geometry separately. Consequence: Acceleration and sustained pace change through the named variables.

**W04.BIND.04** — A hopper contributes inherited return, phase and settling; preload and alignment remain builder choices.
Decision: Breed a better pair or remount the existing pair. Consequence: The comparison identifies which layer changed.

**W04.BIND.05** — A multi-role graft contributes mass, tissue and capability once.
Decision: Keep a whole package or separate its subcomponents. Consequence: A hopper can serve jump and suspension roles without two free assemblies.

**W04.BIND.06** — Race entry captures an immutable build snapshot and model/rules/course versions.
Decision: Commit the intended setup before the start. Consequence: Later edits do not alter an old win, trophy or provenance claim.

**W04.BIND.07** — Normalized values remain local test profiles; absolute creature scale is not established by the solver.
Decision: Calibrate scale separately. Consequence: No normalized time is advertised as real seconds or a retail product guarantee.

### Club benches, borrowing and personal assembly

**Origin:** source_hobby_translation. **Refines:** W03.CUSTODY.07, W03.POLISH.08.

**W04.BENCH.01** — A club bench supplies tools, fixtures, space and advice, not an exclusive right to modify a roach.
Decision: Use home facilities, a club bay or a paid fitting service. Consequence: Casual access and expert ownership coexist.

**W04.BENCH.02** — A test loan changes custody of a particular component for an agreed session.
Decision: Try a motor-equivalent before buying. Consequence: One component cannot be on the shop display and two test racers simultaneously.

**W04.BENCH.03** — A comparison ticket identifies fixed conditions, changed components and intended measurements.
Decision: Compare a drive replacement or a preload change. Consequence: An observed result is attributable to a stated experiment, not a universal best-build score.

**W04.BENCH.04** — Guided kit assembly shows the actual parts, placement and incorporation; advanced adjustment remains on the same objects.
Decision: Assemble the tin or modify its recipe. Consequence: The beginner still builds their own racer.

**W04.BENCH.05** — A failed fitting previews blocked motion, inadequate support or an event conflict separately.
Decision: Move the graft, alter support or select an eligible event. Consequence: No universal one-brow-slot rule returns.

**W04.BENCH.06** — A fitting commission states desired outcome, supplied stock, labor, testing and revision terms.
Decision: Earn money through assembly expertise. Consequence: Professional work can improve execution without secretly improving donor genetics.

**W04.BENCH.07** — A loan return or service completion records actual condition; new loss rules cannot be introduced retroactively.
Decision: Accept known terms before handing over valued stock. Consequence: Service income does not depend on surprise confiscation or mandatory permadeath.

### Modular courses that support a modular hobby

**Origin:** source_hobby_translation. **Refines:** W03.REVISION.05.

**W04.TRACK.01** — A section defines inlet and outlet pose, width, surface, usable paths, supports and active clearance.
Decision: Assemble valid connected pieces. Consequence: A track is both geometry and ordered travel, not a bag of section names.

**W04.TRACK.02** — An installed course uses owned or borrowed section instances and a separate layout recipe.
Decision: Pool, hire, buy or rearrange track pieces. Consequence: A plan does not spawn the physical course.

**W04.TRACK.03** — Passing space is authored as track geometry; guided and shared sections are both valid formats.
Decision: Build a technical channel or a broad pack circuit. Consequence: Contact opportunities follow the actual layout.

**W04.TRACK.04** — Alternative routes declare their entry, exit, required gates and locomotion conditions.
Decision: Choose a legitimate jump or climbing route. Consequence: Route freedom does not silently skip mandatory course progress.

**W04.TRACK.05** — Inside and outside paths have different physical distances when geometry makes them different.
Decision: Take an inside line or a clear outside pass. Consequence: Lateral position affects both distance and maneuvering; no artificial equal-distance assumption.

**W04.TRACK.06** — A repeated qualifying format can rotate start assignments; a single experimental grid is not proof of competitive fairness.
Decision: Choose published heat and seeding procedures. Consequence: Grid advantage is assessed instead of hidden.

**W04.TRACK.07** — Surface wear and cleaning change declared contact conditions only when that system is present.
Decision: Maintain a venue or prepare suitable feet. Consequence: The course does not receive arbitrary invisible difficulty multipliers.

**W04.TRACK.08** — A layout revision creates a new record category while retaining historic times under their original layout.
Decision: Modify a famous circuit and set new records. Consequence: An old champion is not retroactively disqualified by a later rebuild.

### Pace, passing, contact and the finish

**Origin:** source_hobby_translation_and_rrr_extension. **Refines:** W03.PERFORMANCE.01, W03.PERFORMANCE.05, W03.PERFORMANCE.08.

**W04.RACE.01** — Running output uses loaded drive, energy delivery, traction, body mass and leverage.
Decision: Improve the desired mechanical contribution. Consequence: Pace emerges from motion integration rather than a hidden race-power score.

**W04.RACE.02** — A passing maneuver identifies available space, commits to a side and recenters after clearance.
Decision: Attempt a pass rather than repeatedly steering toward and away from the same rival. Consequence: The racer follows through on an observable overtaking attempt.

**W04.RACE.03** — Hopper loading and locomotion share current reserve and delivery; released energy is not spent twice.
Decision: Choose preload and timing. Consequence: An active maneuver has a real resource cost before its benefit.

**W04.RACE.04** — A jump uses inherited tissue and setup to determine useful energy, trajectory and recovery.
Decision: Change the donor, preload, alignment or route. Consequence: A better spring is not a guaranteed pass at every speed or clearance.

**W04.RACE.05** — A horn attack requires reachable aligned contact after wind-up; a miss consumes the declared commitment.
Decision: Build and use a useful ram geometry. Consequence: No remote damage is awarded from an attack rating.

**W04.RACE.06** — A grasping response has closure delay, reach, hold and recovery; holding can impede both racers.
Decision: Intercept a committed rival or release to resume racing. Consequence: Claws create a racing decision rather than a permanent disable.

**W04.RACE.07** — Native contact and dedicated weapons are different capabilities; Speedsters permits the former and excludes the latter.
Decision: Select the intended division. Consequence: Weapon legality is not decided by cosmetic naming.

**W04.RACE.08** — Recovery states preserve course location and elapsed time.
Decision: Regain motion through existing anatomy or supported intervention. Consequence: Falling behind is a racing consequence; the game does not silently reset position.

**W04.RACE.09** — Finish classification requires the full ordered course; ties and nonfinishers have declared handling.
Decision: Complete the race. Consequence: Neither damage count nor a bench result grants victory.

**W04.RACE.10** — The simulation reads inherited internal properties; public advice and commercial claims read only acquired evidence.
Decision: Observe, test and disclose selectively. Consequence: Mechanical correctness does not create an omniscient shopping interface.

### The event is a funded operation

**Origin:** source_hobby_translation. **Refines:** W03.CASH.09, W02.RACE.08.

**W04.MEET.01** — A meeting names venue, layout, locomotion, equipment policy, format, entry conditions and qualification consequences independently.
Decision: Enter an appropriate local race or qualifier. Consequence: Open is build freedom; local participation is not automatic World City admission.

**W04.MEET.02** — A practice run can award a local record or rival acknowledgement without creating a cash prize.
Decision: Practice freely or enter a funded event. Consequence: Replay and practice do not print tournament money.

**W04.MEET.03** — Prize commitments name entry receipts, sponsor funds, club funds or other declared backing.
Decision: Host an event at a sustainable size. Consequence: Prize money is transferred once from an identifiable budget.

**W04.MEET.04** — Tied placements share the affected prize allocation under published terms.
Decision: Resolve a dead heat. Consequence: An identifier or iteration order does not select the richer winner.

**W04.MEET.05** — Operational payments fund actual hosting activities separately from race prizes.
Decision: Provide timing, track setup, staffing or facility services. Consequence: Trackmakers and hosts can earn a keep without winning races.

**W04.MEET.06** — A sanctioned result stores the named host, build snapshot, finish and course.
Decision: Keep a trophy, publish a record or enter the next qualifier. Consequence: A racer retains a career across equipment changes.

**W04.MEET.07** — Replays, photographs and subsequent comparisons reference the original result without recommitting its transactions.
Decision: Revisit a race or share its build. Consequence: Watching a win twice does not pay twice.

**W04.MEET.08** — A race winner is not automatically a genetically certified breeding line.
Decision: Celebrate the win and offer an appropriately qualified claim. Consequence: Prestige can generate attention without fabricating inherited guarantees.

### Race attention becomes specific hobby demand

**Origin:** rrr_native_world_integration. **Refines:** W03.CASH.01, W03.BREED.07.

**W04.DEMAND.01** — A viewer notices a visible performance or appearance; an inquiry precedes a purchase unless a standing agreement already exists.
Decision: Demonstrate, advertise or decline to disclose. Consequence: Publicity is an opportunity, not instant sale revenue.

**W04.DEMAND.02** — A trial order states the useful component, observable acceptance criteria and funded quantity.
Decision: Sell a small credible batch before promising a major run. Consequence: The buyer purchases a specified assembly, not a promise that every child wins.

**W04.DEMAND.03** — A buyer pays for performance, fit, consistency, availability, appearance or provenance according to its purpose.
Decision: Choose the appropriate market for retained and surplus stock. Consequence: Different customers can value the same cohort differently.

**W04.DEMAND.04** — Funded inquiries compete for actual supply and production capacity; growing young remain future inventory.
Decision: Allocate stock among personal builds, founders, ordinary sales and commissions. Consequence: Commercial success creates meaningful allocation choices.

**W04.DEMAND.05** — Consignment retains the consignor identity until a real sale; fees and custody are explicit.
Decision: Use a shop to reach customers without selling outright. Consequence: Shelf price and shop possession do not become realized seller income.

**W04.DEMAND.06** — Recurring demand depends on customer activity and budgets outside the breeding tank.
Decision: Serve kit buyers, collectors, racers, clubs and traders. Consequence: No rule assumes that fresh animals are needed only because every race destroys them.

**W04.DEMAND.07** — Ordinary paid services and common-stock buyers remain accessible recovery routes.
Decision: Earn modest income after a failed ambitious project. Consequence: A major race loss need not force a second career or wipe the workshop.

**W04.DEMAND.08** — A broader economy may aggregate ordinary household spending, but its replenishment must be declared and bounded.
Decision: Scale the world without simulating every citizen at full fidelity. Consequence: Aggregate customers still have accountable expenditure rather than infinite wallets.

### Performance questions lead back into the wildlands

**Origin:** rrr_native_world_integration. **Refines:** W03.INTAKE.02, W02.FIELD.14.

**W04.FIELD.01** — Race symptoms identify candidate explanations, not unique hidden genetic diagnoses.
Decision: Check fuel, condition, setup and comparable assays before replacing a lineage. Consequence: Late fading does not automatically mean poor inherited endurance.

**W04.FIELD.02** — A targeted run links the missing contribution to a donor, stage, habitat and inspectable sign.
Decision: Pursue loaded cadence, release timing, wing integrity or a particular appearance. Consequence: The trip has a stated use without guaranteed rarity.

**W04.FIELD.03** — A currently useful founder can be an ordinary performer that supplies the missing inherited complement.
Decision: Retain synchronized hoppers for a high-return line. Consequence: Field selection uses the current project, not only the finished-product cutoff.

**W04.FIELD.04** — The same route can supply several disjoint customer needs and personal interests.
Decision: Return with common pads, selected arms or attractive moths. Consequence: Unwanted for one racer does not mean worthless to every customer.

**W04.FIELD.05** — Published demand changes which traits a catcher seeks, not which genes already captured animals possess.
Decision: Revisit known habitats for previously ignored characteristics. Consequence: A new race trend cannot reroll existing specimens.

**W04.FIELD.06** — Population recovery, stage changes and travel use world time; entering a screen is not a new cohort.
Decision: Choose another route, breed, trade or wait under the declared clock. Consequence: Rare searching remains an activity rather than a reload exploit.

**W04.PEOPLE.07** — Advice, sale reputation, competition standing and lineage evidence are independent records.
