# MIVIM — Wave v0.18 Contribution Review

**Objective.** Complete the special-source catalogue and connect distinct wing/root/control constructions to meaningful aerial routes, selective stock, material handling and accessible hobby participation.

**Comparator.** A passive wing renamed as flight, a generic recovery reset, a cooldown-only chemical attack, or elevated ground rails without flight-specific decisions.

**Admission.** Required quality at the declared scope AND purposeful novel-development adherence; neither is compensated by volume, speed, or mechanical checks.

| Criterion | Finding | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | Full fictional donor and source limits, explicit complete bills of function, fit/action/course examples, distinct receiving rules and consistent references are authored. | No game-quality, physical, biological or human usability certificate follows from the manuscript. |
| Novel-development adherence | The design is specific to RRR: fore/hind flight arrays, role-explicit yokes, source-dependent reproductive supply and optional services reach the same personal kit hobby. | No global originality or exhaustive prior-art claim; complexity and entry counts are not creativity scores. |
| Effectiveness | The definition answers what to seek, breed, buy, prepare, fit, display and race for each special source and aerial construction. | The claim is reference usability at desk-review scope; advertised motion and enjoyment still need later implementation and observation. |
| Creativity | Source-hobby work/delivery/contact concepts become distinct living structures and three-dimensional course decisions rather than new generic stat auras. | Player-perceived identity and pleasure are not measured here. |
| Scaling potential | Shared donor, material, assembly and course vocabulary supports further content; a complete supplier industry need not become a mandatory task list for each player. | No runtime-scale, world economy, population or retrieval benchmark has been performed. |

**Grading.** No authorized numeric scoring weights, grade bands or expansion of the MIVIM acronym were recovered; none are invented.

## Critical repairs

Longwing surfaces are differentiated rather than counted vaguely; coordination is not maximum synchrony; relative airflow replaces a ground-speed-only aero claim; fold and working envelopes are distinct; recovery needs actual support and drive; discharge readiness does not replenish material; Caustic development needs actual support-stage stock; aerial gate completion and safe retry are independently defined. None of these edits changes a historical race or grants a new genetic bonus.

## Unperformed evaluation

The six [comparison briefs](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#comparison-briefs) state later controlled questions about arrays, flight/control, recovery, offense, entry and supply. Editorial cases are expected interpretations, not experiments. Biological transferability, numerical balance, participant comprehension and voluntary return remain open.
