### W20.S10 — O’Neill & Taylor (2020; online 2019) — Repair of microdamage caused by cyclic loading in insect cuticle

`https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31647615/`

**Checked:** 2026-09-12. **Read scope:** PubMed primary abstract; DOI 10.1002/jez.2329.

**Finding used:** The abstract reports a loss of measured stiffness after cyclic loading and recovery of that measure at later observations.

## Changed research interpretation

The repair studies answer different questions. Partial recovery of incised cuticle, age-dependent measured outcomes and recovery of a stiffness measure after loading are not interchangeable. No experimental percentage or duration becomes an RRR coefficient. The findings justify asking what recovered and under what conditions, not prescribing clinical treatment or claiming viable transplanted tissue.
