# MIVIM — Wave v0.16 contribution review

**Objective:** make source-organism diversity, useful-stage choices, prepared-material life, and earned work precise enough for downstream game authors while preserving complete optional hobby loops.

**Comparator:** a universal two-parent template, generic rarities, one storage timer for every part, interchangeable tank upgrades, and profitable-looking receipts that omit the stock or labor used to earn them.

**Hard admission:** quality at the declared reference scope AND purposeful novel-development adherence. No authorized numerical MIVIM formula is asserted.

## Budget findings kept visible

The fitter’s base case leaves +26 after paid labor, while a rework case leaves −10. Two complete tins leave +50 under stated supplier quotes, but a retail fallback leaves −20 and an unsold tin leaves −52 cash for the period. The breeder’s base case leaves +48 before the separate 96-credit initial capital purchase; a low-yield/no-pair case leaves −40. These are internally specified possibilities, not market predictions. Two adverse cases also need additional upfront liquidity or staged receipt timing.

## Supporting assurance

ID/reference checks, arithmetic, local navigation and byte preservation support the book’s reliability. They are not gameplay tests or an innovation score. The original prototypes were not modified or rerun. The final external editorial report identifies exactly which checks were completed.
