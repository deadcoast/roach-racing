# MIVIM — Wave v0.17 contribution review

**Objective.** Give ground components distinct source biology and preserve their useful function through selection, preparation, carrying, receiving, fitting and repeated livelihoods.

**Comparator.** Seven differently named generic breeding profiles, parts whose arrival only changes a health bar, and disconnected profitable-looking one-period ledgers.

**Hard admission.** Required quality at declared compendium scope AND purposeful novel-development adherence. No authorized numerical weights, grade bands or acronym expansion are invented.

| Criterion | Current contribution | Not established |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | Seven detailed source profiles, twelve physical product sheets, six worked receiving decisions, explicit amendments and linked budgets are authored. | Biological feasibility, final game balance, human reading or playtest acceptance. |
| Novel-development adherence | Source-function translation extends into anatomy-specific supply and service rather than unrelated features: mixed-source matching, conditional Counterbrace actuation, face/backing guide handling and useful ready stock. | Worldwide uniqueness or exhaustive prior-art search. |
| Effectiveness | The written definitions resolve named source/controller, material, custody, allocation and cost questions. Documentary links and budget arithmetic are checked separately. | No game runtime, actual carrier outcome, population model or customer demand is exercised. |
| Creativity | Distinct source stages, morphology, working preparations and material artifacts make the same parts recognizable in habitats, breeding cases, bench work and races. | Player-perceived originality or compelling retention. |
| Scaling potential | Structured donor, material, service and customer records let later authors extend a common language without copying one universal organism template. | Large-agent retrieval quality, world-scale simulation or repeated economic equilibrium. |

## Review repairs

- Observed strike versus arm response: Prevents hereditary arm ratings from absorbing the entire donor’s behavior.
- Horn geometry versus unconditional penalty: Keeps the original exciting horn builds meaningful without lazy longer-is-better or bigger-is-always-worse rules.
- Counterbrace actuation and material class: No deleted attack is mistaken for free deployment power. This refines rather than discards the old material classes.
- Source pair versus working pair: Creates real rematching work and correct provenance without species or family bans.
- Moth covering versus brake material: Prevents an attractive biological analogy from masquerading as evidence.
- Stage and adult feeding by donor: Maintains actual source diversity.
- Load face and condition are product properties: Makes surface selection and carrying requirements purposeful.
- Interface lists must state complete anatomy: Avoids both missing material obligations and double product yields.
- Working condition does not erase a useful object: Supports recovery, collection and honest trade without universal salvage buyers.
- Future budgets retain unsold stock: Extends rather than recalculates W16 or the historical W12 loss.
- Arrival is a new observation, not a second genetic draw: Preserves both useful uncertainty and stable identity.
- Price the useful service, not every confirmation: The deeper source and transport system must not obstruct the initial build-and-race pleasure.

Editorial checks support the work; their count is not an innovation grade. Conditional positive budgets are not evidence that a real game economy will support the quoted terms. Negative branches and unsold stock remain visible.
