## 1. The current foundation: one hobby, several complete lives

`W20.CH.FOUNDATION`

**Rad Roach Racing is a build-and-race hobby in a living wasteland.** People want fast racers, extraordinary insects, beautiful constructions, useful breeding lines, money from good work, and races worth winning. The roach is not a metaphor for a car and the donor is not a colored loot container. Their anatomy, condition, identity and ownership create the differences that make this sport its own.

**The original mutation remains the foundation:** rad roaches incorporate other insects’ anatomy. Prepared exchange makes rebuilding predictable; it does not grant a proprietary permission to assimilate. The original horn, grasping arms and jumping legs remain recognizable everyday ambitions, not rewards withheld until the hobby ceases to resemble its premise.

**A complete activity can end successfully before the whole world is mastered.** A novice opens a tin, builds, names, finishes and races. A catcher returns with one desirable animal. A breeder recognizes a useful contribution in a mixed brood. A fitter returns a customer’s improved arrangement. A collector completes a display. A race is still a race when its owner does not manufacture or sell anything afterward.

### What the milestone actually edits

| Previous accumulation | Current replacement |
|---|---|
| A reader follows many wave documents to decide which meaning is current. | This desk edition gives the current relationship, scoped amendments and direct source/part routes; the historical archive remains separate. |
| Quality, health, current output and preparedness can collapse into one rating. | The useful contribution, current material, fitted construction, present supply and evidence are distinct. |
| A setback ends at retirement, an invoice, or an unresolved condition label. | The reference follows it through observation, supported care, a real choice, performed work and return, changed use or explicit loss. |
| Anatomical completeness risks making every owner manage specialist procedures. | The maker and service provider supply a defined complete project and routine support; expert operations remain optional. |
| A recipe is either exact-individual copying or so broad that it accepts unsuitable stock. | A product promise describes measured function, operating ranges, included anatomy, service responsibility and allowed substitutions. |
| A racer can have many registered predecessors but little memorable continuity. | Current host, historic configuration, retained core, replacement, descendant and replica have separate meanings. |

### Four eras of the same reference

**0–5:** the personally built racer and the distinct component functions. **6–13:** particular source animals, reproduction, real stock, courses, makers and competition. **14–19:** biological and institutional definitions, optional depth, complete supplier lives and the road to New World City. **20:** a single current reading route and a developed account of how that living work remains useful through change.

This is not a reset of the atlas. All twenty source monographs, earlier material descriptions, proposed regions and recorded experiments remain retrievable. No old animal, genome, money transfer, race result or prototype coefficient is changed by editing a book.

### Authority without an endless approval queue

Author-established premises outrank design proposals. A named later amendment replaces only its named scope. A current working definition can be developed decisively without pretending that it was personally dictated by the author. Research is evidence about its own subject, not new fictional canon. Old trial values are historical inputs, not automatically the world’s units or laws.

The current reading separates **author premise**, **working world definition**, **proposed alternative requiring a choice**, **external observation**, and **historical experiment**. These distinctions are in the companion resolution register as well as the prose. No developer must read three incompatible rules and silently choose the newest paragraph.

Global host mortality, rare-material regeneration, absolute scale and the origin of post-winter life remain author-level choices. This milestone supplies operational definitions that work without secretly choosing immortality, scheduled death or endless reproduction of paid parts. The provisional branches and their consequences are stated in Chapter 13.

## 2. The translation contract: preserve the choice, define the anatomy

`W20.CH.TRANSLATION`

**PCR is the direct component-and-rivalry reference. Physical Mini 4WD is a separate reference for how components, kits and courses work. RRR supplies the living execution.** A biological claim cannot be established by a car-part name; a real insect observation cannot reveal a videogame’s hidden item coefficients.

SEGA’s official image shows eight performance headings, body selection in the listed build, appearance controls and whole-build readings. It shows one build, not a complete item database. Its cost of 40 is a source-game example, not a revived universal RRR Graft Point budget. [W20.S01](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s01)

| Source function | RRR object | Useful selection/fitting question | What must not be silently imported |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body/vehicle | Persistent host and native body plan | What proportions, native mass, articulation and root-bearing anatomy suit the project? | A different skin with identical anatomy, or a borrowed car dimension as an insect law. |
| Frame | Native support and prepared bracing | Where does working load travel, and does support permit movement? | Armor across the entire animal merely because the frame is broad. |
| Motor | Drive musculature | What useful work, force, cadence and efficiency can be delivered? | A universal speed gene independent of limbs, load and supply. |
| Gears | Working linkages; coordination where actually included | What force/travel relationship and phase are required? | More mechanical advantage or more simultaneous motion always being better. |
| Tires | Specific running contact anatomy | How does it plant, carry load, release and tolerate the surface? | Fixed-wheel steering behavior applied literally to walking feet. |
| Battery | Reserve body, contents and delivery interface | How much is carried; how much can be delivered; what remains? | An unfilled large organ providing fuel, or recharge regenerating failed tissue. |
| Stabilizer | Sensing, bracing, lean stops or guides | What disturbance is detected and what physical response exists? | Four differently named components stacking the same invisible stability effect. |
| Suspension | Receiving joints and supported compliance | What landing demand can be received and how does running resume? | The same joint counted again inside complete Hoppers. |
| Front aero | Passive vanes, foils and trim | What air-relative loading occurs at this shape and orientation? | A powered wing system hidden inside a passive-aero slot. |
| Appearance | Natural morphology, applied finish, edition and provenance | What does the owner want to recognize, collect or show? | A rarity color granting force, fertility or automatic price. |
| Bearings/rollers/brakes/dampers: physical-hobby additions | Sleeves, Railshoes, contact brakes, supported Keels | Which actual contact, loss or movement is changed? | Claiming every item occurs in the same PCR game menu. |
| RRR-native anatomy | Horns, Catchclaws, Hoppers, Boiler, flight arrays, Kickback | How does the action use the body and change the race? | Inventing a false one-to-one car equivalent for every extension. |

The physical guide distinguishes motor selection, ratio adjustment and smooth moving interfaces. Those observations inform the separation above; the full insect behavior is authored. [W20.S02](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s02)

### A component contribution has four layers

**Function:** the job, such as receiving a landing. **Product promise:** the observed or specified performance under named conditions. **Current ability:** what this actual preparation can presently do. **Use in a construction:** whether this host and setting can employ that ability.

A new specimen can improve several useful properties at unchanged burden. Keep that improvement. An operating alternative can be valuable without belonging above or below another on a universal ladder. A specialist mechanism needs recurring opportunities, not a mandatory obstacle that only its item name unlocks.

For every new sect, record: source anatomy; prepared assembly; traits worth selecting; field clue and its limit; fitting contribution; required supply/control/support; observable action; non-enabling situation; ordinary customer; service or replacement consequence. The unit is a physically intelligible object. A stat entry alone is not enough.

### Retained whole-build readings

Speed, acceleration, contact, cornering, balance, hold-down and mass remain useful summaries where applicable. Their labels do not become independent ingredients. A poor corner can involve contact, a constrained joint, traffic, support or setup. A comparison must identify which changed. A Flyer uses contacts at reached surfaces; a ground stat does not need a fictional airborne effect to stay familiar.

Exact unavailable PCR coefficients are not invented. Named source examples and verified component purposes are the baseline. A later complete item-level PCR census is a distinct research deliverable; this milestone does not claim one.

## 3. Useful, ready, damaged and valuable are different questions

`W20.CH.STATE`

<a id="w20-state-01"></a>

**W20.STATE.01 — Replace the single all-purpose condition score with a compact, causal account of the actual object.** The developer may summarize it in a small interface, but the underlying meaning cannot collapse into one percentage that changes genes, money, physics and permission at once.

| Layer | Example | What changes it |
|---|---|---|
| Source potential and inherited contribution | A line’s tendency toward quick contact release. | Reproduction and the declared inheritance model; not cleaning or a sale. |
| Developed anatomy | Adult root spacing, a completed wing, an immature coupling. | The source’s actual development; not an item upgrade label. |
| Current material | A clean pad, a cracked lever, a damaged interface. | Use, incident, environmental exposure, supported service or repair. |
| Current supply/readiness | Reserve contents, fatigue, prepared support. | Actual expenditure, supported recovery, refilling and preparation. |
| Fitted arrangement | Orientation, stance, preload, shared supply and clearance. | Real assembly and adjustment. |
| Evidence | Field observation, old assay, new receiving result. | What has actually been observed, by whom, under which conditions. |
| Purpose and demand | Racer, breeder, product source, display or historical original. | A person’s goal and funded demand; not an intrinsic universal price. |

<a id="w20-state-02"></a>

**W20.STATE.02 — Readiness is relative to a proposed use.** A wing damaged for flight may remain suitable for display. A recovered support may serve a lighter configuration without meeting the original heavy-load promise. That narrower use is not an excuse to sell it as unchanged.

### Six practical dispositions

**Ready for the named use.** Actual required functions are supported; ordinary uncertainty about race outcomes remains.

**Routine service required.** A known reversible issue has an ordinary supported remedy: fouling, fill, alignment, or a specified conditioning need.

**Use limited pending reassessment.** Some functioning anatomy remains, but the previous promise is not established. A safe proposed alternative can be assessed.

**Repair required.** A real material or interface change requires work, inputs and an appropriate post-work check.

**Function lost.** The stated capability cannot be restored by the ordinary procedure. Replacement, a different documented preparation, display or other actual disposition is considered.

**Assessment incomplete.** The answer is not yet known. This is not a hidden failure, a clean bill of health or an instruction to buy the most expensive test.

These dispositions can differ across regions of one racer. An empty intact reservoir is not a dead host. A lost wing root is not a whole-body zero-value label. A worn foot does not invalidate the reproductive ancestry of the donor whose tissue supplied it.

### Observations must not become a diagnosis oracle

A new limp, late release or drifting turn is evidence. It may justify checking a named mechanism. It does not uniquely identify damaged genes or guarantee that one part caused a lost race. Reports show the relevant observation and the first useful comparison, with competing causes retained where genuine.

The ordinary owner sees a short verdict and the next meaningful choice. Specialist tests appear when their additional information can change that choice. Repeatedly opening a card does not refresh the genotype, improve condition, consume care or produce another independent sample.

<a id="w20-state-03"></a>

**W20.STATE.03 — Historical assays retain their date, condition and scope.** A valuable old measurement can guide selection but cannot certify a newly fractured part. A new result can demonstrate restored function without rewriting what happened before.

## 4. The Return Bench: complete the consequence loop

`W20.CH.RETURN`

The Return Bench is an extension of an ordinary Foundry session, home workshop or Wrangloid service. It is not a new profession, a required building tier or a second inventory.

**Its job:** let the owner understand what happened and choose what to do with the same racer and material. A healthy return normally resolves through the existing session allowance. Extraordinary work is quoted before it is performed.

### The five-step service route

**1. Record the outcome.** Show the finish, placing or retirement first. Keep the race entry and actual incidents separate from current bench edits. A result does not change because a component later recovers.

**2. Secure what returned.** Account for the host, attached parts and recovered loose anatomy. A part lost outside the reachable course is not back in the tray simply because it was once equipped. A retrieved part is not transferred to an opponent by contact.

**3. Identify a changed use or condition.** Compare the named observation against the last relevant reference. Healthy retained anatomy does not need compulsory recertification after every uneventful run. The first assessment can be bundled into attendance or normal bench service.

**4. Choose a real route.** Keep the working build; perform included service; approve a repair; use a real alternate part; commission an ordinary replacement; preserve a historic object; or pause this project while care continues. Where insufficient evidence remains, choose a useful test rather than a fabricated verdict.

**5. Close the work.** Return the actual objects, identify new material, record the achieved function and settle performed service. A readiness demonstration is not a promised race win. The owner may race again, breed eligible stock, display the project, sell a described object or stop.

### An exchange plan is not mandatory consumption

A stocked plan identifies optional compatible alternatives, ordinary supplies and the service included during the booked visit. It is a planning object, not another stat module. A single healthy spare can serve several sequential races; it cannot be installed on several hosts simultaneously. A borrowed spare has an owner and return terms.

A complete beginner tin includes a viable first-use arrangement. It does not need to include a second rare donor in case the game destroys the first one. Ordinary service access, a quoted replacement route and the actual rarity of required material must be coherent before entry prices or damage incidence are calibrated.

### Four choices that preserve different value

**Maintain:** keep the existing object working under its declared profile. **Restore:** perform work that re-establishes a named useful capability. **Rebuild:** change the configuration or replace an actual subassembly. **Retain:** preserve an animal or object for another purpose, including breeding where eligible, display, history or a later project.

No choice is automatically cheaper, better or safer. A cleaned pad can genuinely recover its earlier behavior. A repaired structural crack can remain unsuitable for its former load. A newly supplied ordinary joint can make the present racer useful while the prized original waits for specialist work.

### A practical recovery without copying a phenotype

A Hopper pair has good inherited timing but one receiving surface is damaged. The bench distinguishes that surface from the shared coordination, drive and roots. If the preparation permits a separate receiving element, a real compatible replacement is fitted and the pair is reassessed. If not, the owner chooses a complete replacement or another actual path. The book does not force a new premium brood for a replaceable foot, and it does not assume every compound assembly can be disassembled infinitely.

The desirable result is not merely fewer fees. It is continuity: the owner can understand, keep using, modify or preserve the work they cared about.

## 5. Repair, biological recovery, replacement and regeneration

`W20.CH.RECOVERY`

**Use does not automatically equal damage.** A normal completed heat is not a mandatory subtraction from every fitted component. Any modeled accumulation must follow the component’s real duty, materials and condition; a new inspection does not itself age the animal.

The new research supports a sharper distinction than either “insects cannot repair” or “living tissue restores everything.” One locust study observed targeted internal cuticle deposition restoring part of the injured structure’s mechanical strength. Another found different restoration outcomes across the studied ages. A third examined recovery of measured stiffness after cyclic loading. Those are different experimental questions, not one universal regeneration percentage. [W20.S08](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s08) [W20.S09](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s09) [W20.S10](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s10)

### The six operations

| Operation | Actual subject | Completion evidence | What it never supplies by name alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cleaning / ordinary adjustment | Fouling or fit without absent anatomy | Relevant contact or movement restored | A new inherited trait. |
| Recovery / conditioning | Supported living function under its species/material profile | Usable response after actual care | An unlimited supply of absent limbs. |
| Structural repair | Damaged retained material plus actual repair input | The new working envelope | Automatic original strength, shape or mass. |
| Interface replacement | Retained core plus a separately supplied junction | Integration and relevant working response | False same-donor provenance. |
| Subassembly replacement | Identified new anatomy replaces an identified old region | Fit and whole working relationship | A duplicate spare made from the replaced tissue. |
| Regeneration | New biological material formed by a specifically approved mechanism | Identity, supply, growth and resulting function | A blanket right to copy rare paid parts. |

<a id="w20-recovery-01"></a>

**W20.RECOVERY.01 — A visible closed wound and restored load-bearing capacity are different observations.** A smooth outer surface is not enough to restore the prior qualification.

<a id="w20-recovery-02"></a>

**W20.RECOVERY.02 — A damaged configuration can regain all of its former useful performance where actual material and work support that result.** Do not impose a permanent stat scar on every service merely to sustain replacement demand.

<a id="w20-recovery-03"></a>

**W20.RECOVERY.03 — A partial repair may be useful without meeting the original duty.** The narrower declared use and changed material remain visible. An owner can reject the alteration before irreversible work.

<a id="w20-recovery-04"></a>

**W20.RECOVERY.04 — Recovery evidence from a whole living insect does not establish isolated-graft repair.** Detached tissue requires the named support and physiology in its fictional profile. No source paper validates a transplant medium, root renewal or a storage timer here.

### Material state can mislead selection

Research on locust cuticle found that desiccation changed different mechanical properties in different directions. A stiffer specimen was not necessarily the more damage-tolerant one. RRR adopts the lesson about named measurements and comparable state, not the real coefficients or a universal insect-material law. [W20.S11](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s11)

A catcher who observes a stiff old joint has evidence worth checking. A breeder selecting only that observation may select damaged or poorly conditioned stock rather than a useful inherited contribution. The same warning applies to full reservoirs, tightly polished guides and artificially restrained wings.

### Rare core, replaceable interface

A source-defined spring architecture, sensor, horn core, vein network or inherited body is not regenerated by replacing its common exchange support. The replacement can restore integration while retaining the prized core. If the rare core is lost, the plan must supply another real core, an approved regeneration process, or a different outcome.

This distinction supports restoration without abolishing collecting. A historic horn can keep its original core and gain a new working interface. Its functional life can continue while its provenance becomes more specific. A replica is an additional object made with additional inputs.

### Known terminal source preparation stays terminal

Vital extraction still ends the donor’s living reproductive role in the named source profile. A historical record remains, but it is not a parent waiting in a hidden tank. Nonterminal extraction changes the body’s real capabilities and care; reproductive usefulness must be assessed rather than awarded automatically.

**No later market feature may require abuse, repeated forced injury or deliberate exhaustion as the optimal generator of valuable repair stock.** The useful business is support, preparation, recovery and actual replacements for activity people already wanted to pursue.

## 6. Race incidents: keep the finish, identify the consequence

`W20.CH.INCIDENT`

Open, Fireworks and Speedsters retain their established equipment distinctions and contact identity. This chapter does not convert the sport into noncontact racing, and it does not introduce last-roach-standing scoring. The meeting records a valid course and finish. A disabled competitor is not automatically its opponent’s scored kill.

### Four outcomes that must not be merged

**A race loss:** another valid finisher is ahead. **A maneuver failure:** an action misses, wastes time, or loses position. **A retirement:** this entry no longer continues under the event’s procedure. **A biological/material consequence:** a particular object needs care, repair or replacement, or has irreversibly lost a function.

One outcome can occur without all the others. A late grasp can lose time without tearing tissue. A poor route can retire a healthy roach. A valid finisher can later need service. The result is not retrospectively invalid merely because inspection finds ordinary post-race wear.

### The incident record

The meaningful record is: circumstance and contact; actually involved anatomy; observed movement or failure; current affected function; remaining capabilities; declared race action; and post-event options. A downstream solver may represent this at different fidelity, but a remote damage roll cannot claim a contact that never occurred.

### Withdrawal, retrieval and re-entry

The ordinary sanctioned reference format provides a declared retirement/withdrawal procedure and a practical retrieval arrangement. Calling retirement ends scoring progress at the permitted boundary. Retrieval does not teleport the roach forward, refill it for free or convert a nonfinish into a finish. A continued-run or checkpoint-recovery event is possible only if announced with its time and progress consequences.

A racer cannot receive a secret service while the heat runs. Between declared rounds, the same host can be worked on and inspected again. A meet-level host restriction is separate from the owner’s later ability to enter another animal. A host swap is not disguised as a component repair.

A known uncontained Boiler, unsupported flight root, or nonfunctional required receiving system can fail readiness for the actual event. Failure is attached to the demonstrated condition, not a universal exclusion of creative anatomy or an invented maximum equipment price.

### Protection without making every hit meaningless

Armor protects covered anatomy under the relevant load. Uncovered roots remain distinct. A load-spreading support can protect a junction incidentally through its mechanism without becoming an all-body shield. Failure of an optional preparation need not kill the host; failure of a vital integrated system can have more serious consequences under its separately authored profile.

A detachable protective arrangement is not required to detach automatically. If a later design uses a release mechanism, its material, trigger, detached object and resulting movement must be specified. This milestone does not add a universal sacrificial part that guarantees survival of every impact.

### The death decision is not smuggled into incident probability

The recommended commissioning direction is recoverable ordinary setbacks, meaningful component loss where supported, and explicit severe-outcome policy. That direction does not grant invulnerable hosts. Global mortality, lifespan and rare-material regrowth remain open in the decision register. A production team must select and evaluate that policy before setting severe-risk rates; a missing rule is not permission to fill the gap with a random death tax.

The world can still depict an intense charge, a torn exposed preparation or a frightening near miss. The handbook specifies what the event and workshop do next, not a mandatory gore style or a hidden anti-player rule.

## 7. Breeding for real improvements after the race

`W20.CH.SELECTION`

A recurring defect is a research question before it is a genetic diagnosis. First separate a badly prepared batch, misaligned fixture, insufficient supply, unsuitable course, current condition and an inherited contribution. Then decide whether breeding is the useful intervention.

### Four targeted projects

**Glassfoot: retain clean release after ordinary fouling.** The line already grips adequately. Record matched clean and challenged observations without converting every captive test into damage. Compare actual descendants. Ordinary clean-course output can supply current kits while candidates with useful recovery behavior remain in the project. A pad cleaned successfully is serviced, not genetically upgraded.

**Hopper: repeatable useful receiving after a strong launch.** The line already has return and synchronization. Compare settling under a disclosed receiving demand; check whether the fitted Keel and feet were functioning. Select a contribution only after excluding those construction causes. A receiving improvement can be valuable without raising maximum jump energy.

**Bullhorn: retain useful load transfer in the intended direction.** The owner does not need the largest silhouette. Observe root and core response for the actual contact geometry. Relate a controlled preparation result to source records honestly; a terminally prepared donor is not a still-living prospective parent. Compatible descendants and known related observations can inform the next line.

**Longwing: useful deployment after routine supported transport.** Distinguish inherited form from a poorly designed case and from damaged handling. Fix the case first when it is the cause. A line that repeatedly provides suitable geometry can then improve ordinary preparation yield, while specialist arrays and appearance-led pieces remain separate products.

### Performance breeding and production breeding

A exceptional one-off racer and a reliably supplied kit family are different achievements. Performance breeding pursues useful properties. Production breeding may pursue the proportion of intact, suitable stock under a specified facility plan, workable maturity transitions, or consistent preparation dimensions. The facility and its handling remain part of the evidence; a yield claim cannot attribute every nursery improvement to heredity.

### Do not multiply uncertainty until nothing is useful

The complete project includes encounter, capture, preserved anatomy, eligible stage, reproduction, expression, development, measurement and actual customer acceptance. Each filter needs a decision or an intended scarcity purpose. Reducing every rate independently can make a desirable construction effectively unavailable even while each local probability looks reasonable.

Buying measured components, known reproductive stock, or a supported stage service remains the alternate route. The same object has the same useful potential regardless of its acquisition path. Nothing here requires every owner to research repair tolerance before joining a race.

### Split output and bounded reserves remain current

Potential research candidates, current-use components, actual ordinary supply and a maintained replacement target share a brood without duplicating it. When the reserve is full, new candidates require a decision instead of automatically becoming additional permanent dependents. Production can stop while existing care continues.

An unscreened bulk-sale policy remains a legitimate convenience chosen by the owner. It is not secretly optimized using hidden alleles. A protection based on visible evidence can suggest review, but it cannot lock every possibly valuable individual forever.

## 8. The complete product now includes a continued-use promise

`W20.CH.PATTERNS`

A complete kit still means a living foundation, actual included preparations, identified native functions, assembly guidance and an appropriate first-use arrangement. A conversion set says that no host is included. Product selection does not install the construction automatically.

The milestone adds a **continued-use promise**: which ordinary service is supported, what can be rematched or replaced separately, and which rare or integral element requires a new source. This is useful packaging and supplier responsibility, not an unlimited warranty or a permanent-maintenance tax.

### Three independent kinds of compatibility

**Functional:** does it supply the intended mechanism? **Physical:** does it fit and work on this host with its neighboring anatomy? **Service:** can the actual preparation be handled or partly replaced by the offered procedure?

A part can be functionally appropriate but too wide. Another can fit but deliver the wrong operating range. An excellent bonded assembly can be less modular to service than a documented exchange preparation without being an inferior racer.

### Reproducing a construction without consuming its original

The personal build remains an actual animal and its current parts. A commercial recipe describes a promise and permitted substitutions. A physical kit contains other actual objects. Source origin, part identity and observed measurements are maintained, while the buyer can finish and name the new racer personally.

A multi-role Hopper set satisfies its listed functions once. A Longwing array does not include drive or control merely because its product photograph depicts a complete Flyer. Native control can be adequate where the complete construction supports it; expensive Gyropods are not a compulsory missing brain.

### Eight cross-region offers

The companion [Product Atlas](RRR_Wave_20_Product_Atlas.md) specifies eight offers: Tin Can Special / First Bench; Needle & Thread / Clear Run; Last Laugh / Turnaround; Foundry Heavy / Rootline; Glassline / Mixed Surface; Skyhook / Courtyard Service; Flashboil / Airflare Service; and a named customer-stock restoration brief.

They use current patterns and source roles, not eight new species or forced set bonuses. Each declares contents, original or retained functions, fitting choices, a typical useful opportunity, a non-enabling context, ordinary service, a special-work boundary, source selection goals, producer, customer and alternate acquisition route. Their names identify new working printed briefs, not falsely dated historical products.

### A service pack contains material or work

A spare sleeve pack holds real sleeves within known working ranges. An interface-support pack holds actual usable junction supplies. An ordinary contact service provides performed cleaning and a relevant check. A contract alone does not generate a spare when needed.

The attractive package still matters: the lid shows the promised construction, the wells protect real anatomy, and the guide makes the assembly approachable. A product with perfect paperwork but no recognizable roach is incomplete art direction.

## 9. Services that earn a keep without manufacturing damage

`W20.CH.ECONOMY`

Repair and replacement demand follow real participation, collection, differing conditions and wanted projects. The world must not damage every animal at a prescribed rate to keep a shop solvent. There are already other useful products: new racers, spare arrangements, expanded collections, convenient ready components, preparation, transport, fitting and performance improvement.

### Who pays for what?

**The catcher** preserves a promising individual and its useful anatomy. **The breeder** supplies actual stage-matched stock or observed contributions. **The preparer** produces a working component. **The fitter** makes it work on a specific host. **The carrier** performs the agreed handling. **The maker** coordinates a complete project. **The restorer** preserves or replaces explicitly identified material. One person can perform several roles; payment is not automatically repeated for each label.

The remedy for a failed supplied promise differs from a customer changing their goal. A correctly described ratio that no longer suits a new course is not automatically defective. An unresponsive delivered arm is not made acceptable by the buyer losing a race. Evidence determines the next assessment; contract scope assigns performed work and remedies. This is fictional service design, not real legal advice.

### A limited, fully described worked comparison

The numbers below are authored credits for one customer-stock job, not new universal prices or amendments to historical cash records. The client owns a named host and retained support/guide assembly. The ordinary task is to restore useful contact and alignment, not buy a race victory.

| Outcome | Accepted fee | Actual cash outlays | Remainder after listed operator pay |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Quoted ordinary service | 48 | 44 | +4 |
| Specialist replacement silently added at the old price | 48 | 64 | -16 |
| Specialist work explicitly requoted and accepted before commitment | 70 | 64 | +6 |

The ordinary 44 consists of supplies 10, relevant testing 4, booked workspace 6, current stock care 4, operator remuneration 18 and handover 2. The specialist branch adds one actually purchased 20-credit replacement. If the customer declines the revised scope, no 70-credit receipt is invented. Ownership of the original or replaced part is settled explicitly; it is not unearned operator pay.

A 60-credit opening workshop balance can finance the ordinary job before receipts but cannot finance 64 of specialist work without a staged payment, additional working capital or a changed schedule. The difference between a positive end-of-job balance and financing the work remains visible. These examples show a coherent quote and a failure, not willingness-to-pay evidence or repeat demand.

### Lessons retained from earlier periods

The W12 project paid 150 and received 112, reducing cash by 38. That remains historical. W16/W17’s multi-period examples separately track capital, labor, care, unsold stock and actual delivered receipts. This wave does not reclassify the loss as valuable experience to claim profitability.

A fair livelihood review includes repair obligations, stock still held, sales not yet settled, repeated supply costs and meaningful nonmarket returns such as a personally improved racer. Those returns can justify a hobby expense, but they are not cash income.

### Recovery access without an obligation spiral

Normal care, paused reproductive starts, a rented bench, paid boarding, genuine ordinary sales and customer-stock work create practical alternatives to running every production system. Severe-cost arrangements must be quoted with a feasible scope; a debt can exist only under an explicitly authored policy. No automatic arrears, foreclosure of protected founders or confiscation of a champion is introduced here.

A player can finish a job and stop. Default offline pause remains. There is no abandoned-race billing timer in real-world days.

## 10. The sporting charter, reconciled for actual returns

`W20.CH.SPORT`

The source hobby establishes the importance of inspection, starts and classification. RRR retains those purposes while changing the participants, contact and locomotion. Its Open class is not bound by the source manufacturer’s parts restrictions or anti-damage rules. [W20.S07](RRR_Wave_20_Research.md#w20-s07)

### The current axes

**Locomotion:** ground and sustained Flyer competition are separate. **Equipment:** Open, Speedsters and Fireworks have their actual capability policies. **Progression:** Locals → Regionals → Factionals → faction representation in New World City. **Format:** practice, single race, series, exhibition or a declared specialist event. **Support:** attendance, receiving, between-round work and travel. These are independent descriptions of one event.

Open has no new universal parts budget. A published aperture and actual flight capability are legitimate event facts; an arbitrary donor-name ban is not. Speedsters rejects dedicated offensive functionality, not every anatomical form whose source once hunted. Fireworks requires working offense and light protection, including the real coverage supplied by an unusually named structure.

### Qualification and personal continuity

An entrant holds the campaign eligibility. A host holds its identity and race history. An inspected build records the actual heat configuration. A faction delegation holds the earned edition-specific representation. Buying a champion does not buy its seller’s berth; changing components does not erase the host’s achievement.

Between separate meetings, the qualified owner may choose an actually owned or legitimately borrowed racer. Within the standard meeting, the same host remains the entrant. Permitted between-round work and readiness checks are published. A forced host change follows an explicit reserve rule or ends participation; it is not concealed as an ordinary part replacement.

### Standard-service reporting does not make race damage optional fiction

A host can be withdrawn after a serious contact. Its current classification and its post-event care are recorded separately. A component that recovers later does not retroactively award the lost heat. An uninjured host that leaves the valid course still fails that event’s completion requirement.

The relevant event document states whether self-recovery, checkpoint return, outside assistance and re-entry are permitted. Aerial retrieval reaches an actual receiving location; it does not provide free flight energy. Unsupported rescue is not an off-screen success assumed for every condition.

### A world invitation must remain usable

W19’s support floor is retained: the promised representative route provides declared outward and return arrangements, ordinary receiving and required temporary support. Failure of a provider triggers an actual contingency rather than an unrelated mandatory earning quest. This does not provide infinite premium parts or erase severe-care uncertainties.

The local hobby continues when special purse editions finish. Practice, rivals, rebuilding, trade and display exist outside that particular prize list. A new sponsored meeting requires new resources; replay of an old result never pays again.

### Conditions, not level multipliers

Cinderhouse can present loaded starts, Tinwater mixed practice, Crown Basin aerial choices, Saltglass measured surface decisions and Spanrest portable courses. Imported anatomy keeps its properties. Rivals use the same construction rules and bounded information. A Regional label cannot strengthen their undisclosed genes or perfect-counter the player’s private stock.

## 11. Used parts, remembered racers and a real downstream customer

`W20.CH.MEMORY`

The first owner’s construction and the recipient’s kit are distinct physical projects. Their relationship can be recognizable without copying an animal or assuming every satisfied buyer becomes a scripted friend.

### A five-object restoration example

A remembered winning host still exists. Its old build sheet depicts a Bullhorn preparation. The horn’s original structural core remains, but the working interface is no longer suitable. A new separately supplied interface restores the retained core to a stated working use. A display replica of the old construction, if made, is a fifth object with new inputs.

The historic record can truthfully say **original host**, **original core**, **replacement interface**, **current restored build**, and **new replica**. No one label establishes all of them. The old result continues to identify its original configuration.

### Repair marks have visual purpose

A repaired interface, a replaced contact pair or a differently supported wing should be visible where it changes the silhouette or working material. A repair does not need a compulsory ugly scar or statistical penalty. The owner may preserve, finish or deliberately expose that history.

Natural patterns and applied finish remain different. Selling a repaired patterned wing does not create a heritable version of the added decoration. A signature line can retain its ancestry while a current individual has different preparation or condition.

### Recipient feedback is about use

A club buyer of a Glassline-style kit assembles the delivered stock and later finds that a new loose-surface venue asks something different of the feet. The maker can offer an appropriate alternative using real anatomy. This is not necessarily a complaint about the original correct delivery.

Another recipient discovers a working fault inside the supplied promise. The remedy follows that observation and the agreed service, not an automatic new-sale opportunity. Reputational recognition can make future dealings easier; it does not replace a failed functional requirement or generate unbudgeted cash.

### Older does not mean obsolete

A former race component can remain a working spare, a source for a permitted partial preparation, a restored original, a display or a record worth studying. Each needs a real object and purpose. There is no universal salvage buyer and no rule that every old item deserves the same asking price.

A specialist can make a livelihood through rematching, accurate descriptions and returning useful anatomy to a particular owner. The hobby’s continuing collection is therefore not just accumulated overhead or a machine for destroying old stock to sell new stock.

## 12. The atlas supports the same hobby at different depths

`W20.CH.WORLD`

The six settlements and four factions remain a regional proposal, not the whole planet. Their useful differences survive the milestone, but their relationship to a local owner becomes more specific.

**Tinwater / Foundry / Two Nets:** complete first projects, ordinary stock, included receiving and useful bench sessions. The local return route should not demand exact lineage documentation before a novice can clean and refit known anatomy.

**Cinderhouse / Rivet Commons:** support and force under load, repeat dimensions, root adaptation and paid workshop work. More substantial reinforcement is useful only when it transmits actual loads without binding. The civic faction does not own all private stock.

**Crown Basin / Reedwater Compact:** full stage-aware supply and flight preparation. A returning Flyer can obtain specialist root matching or supported emergence without creating a private pond. Ground racers still live and race there.

**Saltglass / Lantern Exchange:** distinct product promises, rematching, appearance-led work and historical descriptions. A beautifully presented ordinary animal remains an honest offering; accurate descriptions make travel and consignment useful.

**Spanrest / Traverse Freeholds:** portable support, receiving, joint work and keep-running arrangements. A traveler can book actual work instead of acquiring a new profession. A portable section collection is owned construction, not permission to invent unlimited venues.

**New World City:** championship grids coexist with neighborhood competition, markets, benches and displays. A maker can visit without a world berth. A representative receives promised support without receiving every premium part. Returning home does not delete the racer’s public identity.

### Source availability is an authored economy, not an inventory reset

A patch supplies a population with actual cohorts. A Wrangloid supplies actual stock from specified sources or an explicitly modeled ordinary production boundary. A retailer may carry known prepared anatomy even when the player has never visited its habitat. Unmeasured wild stock and a seller’s measured stock can have different evidence, not different laws of inheritance.

Where a producer aggregates ordinary output, the world still needs its life-stage inputs and feasible supply. Aggregation can reduce simulation cost; it cannot grant a worker-only bee box a self-reproducing colony or allow the same host pupa to become both a Bankseed adult and a consumed Caustic developmental input.

### A public vocabulary, not a licensing monopoly

Shared pattern names, fitting ranges and product cards let people work across factions. They do not own assimilation. A novel Open construction may remain outside commercial patterns if it has real anatomy and meets the event’s actual conditions.

Mixed-source repairs and imported stock are normal possibilities. Their provenance remains detailed enough for the relevant question without requiring a complete scientific paper before ordinary trade. Precision is purchased where it changes a promise, not charged as a toll on every interaction.

## 14. How the next team uses this edition

`W20.CH.HANDOFF`

This milestone is a rewritten current desk reference, not another pile of unranked rules. It has three entry routes.

**Build a racer:** use the translation contract, component passport and product brief. Follow its actual source only when designing acquisition or production.

**Design a consequence:** use the local cause, required function, offered return route, actual material input and opportunity case. Do not infer an entire death model from one fracture.

**Extend the world:** choose a new source, institution or course because it supplies a different meaningful possibility. Reuse the shared anatomy and participation rules; state the new facts and what they replace.

### Minimum handoff packet for one new feature

1. The particular user desire and a complete achievable outcome.
2. The source-hobby function or explicitly RRR-native extension.
3. The actual objects and their identity across the activity.
4. The anatomical mechanism, inputs and working situation.
5. What the player can observe, what remains unknown and how additional information helps.
6. At least one affordable or service-based participation route and one optional expert route.
7. A meaningful failure, a valid fallback and the material/cash consequences.
8. An enabling case, a non-enabling case, and an altered-build comparison that the later implementation must demonstrate.

These are authoring fields, not eight UI prompts. Shared records make the game simpler to present because the content agrees before a screen is built.

### Editorial acceptance

The companion ledger identifies twenty substantive current-reference amendments and their earlier scopes. Thirty-four use-and-service entries cover the thirty foundational preparations and four explicit flight preparation supplements. Each includes source, mechanism, changed-condition example, response and remaining value. Eight product briefs list actual included/native roles. Six cross-volume reference cases follow the same objects through ordinary and adverse outcomes. The current interpretation JSON points to the appropriate local records rather than asking an agent to merge all historical rules indiscriminately.

Programmatic checks can confirm identifiers, links, prior-file preservation and arithmetic. They cannot establish fun, fairness, retention, real physiology or sustainable pricing. No runtime suite is rerun here and no old test count is presented as evidence that this new experience works.

### Next authored block

Define a bounded set of ordinary event-and-service days spanning Tinwater, Cinderhouse, Saltglass and Crown Basin, including a first kit, a returning racer, a breeder with actual surplus and a customer receiving a product. Use this edition’s condition and identity rules. Add qualitative sequence and actual resource offers before choosing timing or numerical risk.

Then carry the selected severe-outcome/scale decisions into the corresponding dimensioned and experience studies when the author chooses those directions. Do not disguise another prototype as completion of the world bible.
