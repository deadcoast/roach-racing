## Workflow and authority

Original author premises govern. Every additional value is versioned and subject to revision. Supporting tests protect the integration; they do not establish player enjoyment, novelty or long-run commercial balance.

## Playable route

Open the HTML; keep the existing workbench or visit **Live stock**. Choose the donor source and containment method. Inspect captured identity and condition. Assay, protect, breed, prepare or sell. Preparation moves a specific anatomical source into the same sect tray. Return the competing assembly in the draft, place the new part, fit the build and enter the circuit.

A manually started brood has a visible staged schedule. The ordinary-production preset deliberately trades individual selection depth for routine sales while maintaining protected descendants. Repeating a pair and dispatching prepared stock are separately chosen policies. Neither is enabled by default.

Whole-world backup and exact-instance build sheet are separate formats. Browser storage is best-effort; portable backup round trips were executed through real file controls.

## Reviewed repairs

1. A timed preparation or assay reserves its donor against automatic ordinary sale and re-pairing.
2. Incoming captures reserve capacity before travel; repeat production cannot use their promised space.
3. Care forecasting uses a conservative capacity bound rather than hidden brood-survival data.
4. Ordinary surplus waiting on quota can settle at later funded renewal; the animal remains owned and cared for while waiting.
5. Restoring a world clears stale replay controls while retaining attendance summaries.
6. Same-role replacement offspring are not described as a guaranteed reproductive pair.

## Explicit limits

This is a local browser slice, not a complete sandbox, authored story or finished game.

Field collecting is a route-and-containment simulation, not a freely navigable wilderness.

Hopper genetics use three independent diploid loci with fixed coefficients; no mutation, linkage, inherited morphology or general mating system.

Cicadas can be caught, assayed and prepared; a complete root-to-adult cicada breeding profile is not implemented.

The single nursery uses a conservative six-place commitment and fixed stage schedule, not the full general interval scheduler from W03.

Rates, costs, survival and stage lengths are prototype parameters; observed first-seed performance is not a profit guarantee.

Market renewal is explicit modeled off-screen customer funding; national NPC commerce, two-way retail, transport orders and contracts are not fully simulated.

Care credit prevents silent stock loss at zero funds; interest, creditor enforcement, general injury and lifetime care are future branches.

Native exchange is the W05 retained-cradle profile; broad tissue storage, regrowth and damage behavior remain open.

The unchanged two-racer path model retains its earlier aerodynamic, ground-only and entry-mass simplifications.

Cicada efficiency is stored as a selectable donor property but is NOT yet connected to the constant .75 race efficiency; current drive effect is cadence and force.

World JSON is user-editable local data, not competitive authority or protection from intentional cheating.

Archive bounds are 1200 specimen/component records, 200 broods, 15000 ledger entries and bounded world turns; world-scale performance is not established.

File and loopback HTTP navigation were blocked by this host. Exact HTML was exercised via set_content; native origin persistence and Windows/macOS launch remain unverified.

No human playtest or retention measurement. No final MIVIM numerical grade is claimed.
