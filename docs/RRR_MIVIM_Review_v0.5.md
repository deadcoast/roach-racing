# MIVIM — Wave v0.5 contribution review

**Contribution:** Editable eight-sect browser workshop plus bounded path-model races, foundation integration and separately credited supporting validation.
**Novel objective:** A personally arranged biological kit, with known donor history, directly authors a fresh observable race and reusable building knowledge.
**Comparator:** Equipment selection with cosmetic placement, unrelated race scores, canned replays and recipes that duplicate stock.

**Hard eligibility:** `quality_at_declared_scope AND novel_development_adherence_at_declared_scope`.
**Scoring:** No authorized numerical grading formula.

## quality
Implemented and visually reviewed at slice scope; native-platform behavior, human usability and finished-game quality remain open.

## novel_development_adherence
Concrete project-specific integration of anatomical placement, component provenance, builder setup and racing; no worldwide novelty claim.

## effectiveness
Direct editing, custody, entry snapshots and selected outcomes tested; livelihood effects remain specified rather than simulated.

## creativity
Original procedural anatomical presentation and integrated kit interaction delivered; player-perceived distinctiveness unmeasured.

## scaling_potential
Shared pure model, typed stock/profile boundary and versioned snapshots provide an extension path. Fixed inventory and path-space geometry do not establish world-scale capability.

## Supporting work
Port parity, schemas, manifests, input validation and test counts protect the intended feature but are not themselves an achieved gameplay innovation.

## Review repairs
1. An editable draft, atomic fitting and live race entry share the same component identities.
2. Port the unchanged W04 operations and compare six reference experiments numerically.
3. Support and root overlap gate fitting; alignment, reach and yaw map to stated behavior. Inert positioning changes are not falsely credited as performance.
4. The fixture explicitly preserves native exchange units in a cradle. This is not regrowth or a global removal rule.
5. Import only a bounded arrangement against existing local stock; do not import cash, genomes or race authority.
6. Freeze the entry payload and keep later edits separate from recorded result and visualization.
7. Bound desktop panels with internal scrolling; keep the creature and commit controls in view.
8. Keep the source node intact during a tray drag; test the real DataTransfer path, not only Fit buttons.

## Not closed
No global claim that the hobby, economics, visual quality or retention is finished. Fixed-stock editing is not actual in-world collecting or breeding. The next wave must join this usable surface to the shared world without discarding it.
