# RRR — Wave v0.16
## Three Livelihood Reference Sheets

All figures below are authored design cases in illustrative credits. They demonstrate what a coherent viable or unviable offer would contain; they are **not measured market prices, calibrated wages, simulated survival forecasts or proof of sustainable demand**. These budgets do not alter historical prototype values.

Actual customer receipts are conditions of each case. Where purchased supplies are assumed available at a quote, that quote is an explicit scenario input; upstream productivity and a complete supply economy have not been established. A positive scenario is not evidence that those prices will emerge naturally.

Operator remuneration is shown as a real transfer from workshop cash to a separate household/labor account. The remainder is after that payment. Household spending from the remunerated account is not charged a second time as workshop overhead. Any unpriced general tax, future capital replacement, interest or later retained-stock care remains outside the stated period; this is not formal net profit.

Wave12’s original 150-credit manufacturing outlay, 112-credit receipt and −38-credit post-meet cash change remain historical. These new sheets do not relabel that loss as investment or invent missing line items. They describe three distinct future service/production offers under explicit assumptions.

<a id="w16-econ-fitting"></a>
### Customer-owned-stock fitting


`W16.ECON.FITTING`

**Accounting period.** A booked work block with capacity for four completed appointments; not a universal number of world turns.


**Funded customer purpose.** Four funded local hobbyists each authorize a 24-credit fitting job on stock they already own. Foundry provides booked workspace; it is not an unlimited quest wallet.


**Value supplied.** Prepare a usable arrangement, perform the included comparison and return the same stock with a clear account of the work. The quote promises work and acceptance conditions, not a race win.


**Opening cash.** 80 illustrative credits.


**Starting assets and responsibilities.** No biological inventory is sold or financed by the fitter. Rented bench/equipment is in costs; customer hosts and sects remain customer assets. The 80 credits are disclosed working capital, not earned income.


**New capital purchase:** none in this sheet; the required rented facilities appear in operating costs.

**Accounting boundary.** Remuneration is an actual transfer from workshop cash to the operator’s separate household/labor account. The reported remainder is after that payment and before taxes or later capital purchases; it is not a formal net-profit claim. **Working-capital and downside review.** At four jobs the 70-credit cash requirement fits the 80-credit opening balance even before receipts. The 26-credit positive remainder is a conditional budget. A full-cost 82-credit loss case may require staged ordinary payments or an 82-credit precommitment reserve; the sheet must not silently assume cash can become negative.


**Useful decisions.** A fitter can decline risky raw extraction, charge a separate adaptation, use a customer-supplied alternative part, or keep the job within the supported allowance. Breeding is not required. A job can be worthwhile as a learning experiment while being financially unattractive; label the reason.

### Repeat two-tin production


`W16.ECON.KITS`

**Accounting period.** Two accepted ordinary club-entry tins within one agreed production window. The same recipe can be offered again only when new stock and funding exist.


**Funded customer purpose.** Two funded club buyers each need a host, a useful supplied drive, quick-enough contact and the remaining native functions. The 96-credit price is a declared ordinary-kit design quote, not a world-wide tariff.


**Value supplied.** Selection, compatible contents, actual anatomy, preparation, clear assembly and convenience. No hidden set bonus, copied champion or mandatory rare donor.


**Opening cash.** 180 illustrative credits.


**Starting assets and responsibilities.** The recipe is known; no new research line is commissioned. Workspace/equipment is rented in-period. Every host and component has an acquisition line. Supplier quotes are hypothetical accepted offers for actual suitable stock; their upstream viability is not demonstrated here.


**New capital purchase:** none in this sheet; the required rented facilities appear in operating costs.

The appropriate response may be a different specification, supplier, service scope or no commitment—not a universal price buff.

### Observed Hopper founder supply with ordinary output


`W16.ECON.BREEDERS`

**Accounting period.** Two observed production broods and the declared accounting window. Outcome counts are scenario inputs, not predictions from the gene model.


**Funded customer purpose.** One funded Cinderhouse-purpose request pays 76 credits for an eligible measured A/B descendant pair with relevant two-brood evidence. Other funded ordinary buyers accept up to eight suitable intact adults at eight credits each.


**Value supplied.** A useful synchronization contribution and appropriately scoped observations; ordinary siblings supply standard projects. No certification of all future offspring or race victories.


**Opening cash.** 240 illustrative credits.


**Starting assets and responsibilities.** Working capital is disclosed. The 96-credit establishment outlay purchases the four adult stock and reusable hardware rather than granting them as free endowments. Current facility cost covers use/support separate from buying those physical assets. Their upstream quoted prices remain illustrative.

**Retained or undelivered.** Four mature offspring remain unsold and continue consuming care/capacity. They do not become additional permanent reserve animals automatically.


**Accounting boundary.** The steady production cash remainder is reported separately from initial capital. Under the base inputs, 140 receipts minus 92 operating payments leaves 48. Starting from 240 and paying 96 establishment capital as well gives 192 closing workshop cash, not a claimed first-period gain. The retained four adults and reusable hardware remain assets, not receipts.


**Working-capital and downside review.** Base cash committed before any receipt is 188 including initial capital, within 240. The low-yield case yields a 40-credit operating deficit, despite smaller grow-out costs. The saturated case leaves only eight cash credits after current costs and also retains four new care obligations; continued identical starts cannot be justified from that snapshot alone.


**Useful decisions.** Maintain the reserve at two; new replacement decisions explicitly displace or add stock through an actual plan. Stop new starts when demand or capacity is the bottleneck. Sell useful ordinary output without forcing research-grade pedigree on every customer. A selected-pair order can be declined or timed after evidence exists rather than gambling essential care money.
