## Cicada Redline

`W20.COMP.REDLINE` · Motor / drive

**Source:** Furnace cicada.

**Working mechanism:** Contractile work reaches the actual gait or flight linkage through supported roots.

**A meaningful changed condition:** Cadence falls during a loaded run. Empty supply, fatigue, binding or injured tissue are competing causes.

**Ordinary response:** Check supply and free movement, then the appropriate supported working response.

**Replacement boundary:** Dead contractile tissue is replaced with real drive stock; interface-only repair does not resurrect it.

**Other actual value:** A sound ordinary drive can supply a kit even while a premium efficiency line is pursued.

**Distinction to retain:** Correcting a binding sleeve does not improve the cicada genotype.

**Material requirements:** active.

**Prior definition:** [W14.PART.REDLINE](../reference/W19/reference/W18/reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-redline) · [W16.PREP.REDLINE](../reference/W19/reference/W18/reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-redline)

## Scarab Torque Bundle

`W20.COMP.TORQUE` · Motor / drive

**Source:** Kilnback scarab.

**Working mechanism:** Thick working musculature supplies useful force; gearing determines its delivery.

**A meaningful changed condition:** The body stalls at a restart although the reserve contains fuel.

**Ordinary response:** Check contact, output delivery and supported movement before declaring inadequate inherited force.

**Replacement boundary:** A failed muscle column or root is assessed regionally; no free whole bundle appears.

**Other actual value:** Support plates from separately allocated anatomy can retain their own value.

**Distinction to retain:** A static push does not establish every cadence or efficiency property.

**Material requirements:** active.

## Mole-Cricket Lowstep

`W20.COMP.LOWSTEP` · Gears / delivery

**Source:** Ballast mole cricket.

**Working mechanism:** Specified lever geometry trades working travel against force through articulating interfaces.

**A meaningful changed condition:** Motion becomes resistant after gritty contact.

**Ordinary response:** Clean the named interface and verify the actual travel and ratio; change setup only where needed.

**Replacement boundary:** An included lining cannot simultaneously be sold as a new separate sleeve; a replacement is a new real item.

**Other actual value:** Ordinary ratio-range stock remains useful even without a premium force donor.

**Distinction to retain:** A high ratio and a jammed hinge are not two forms of increased power.

**Material requirements:** structure, elastic, contact.
