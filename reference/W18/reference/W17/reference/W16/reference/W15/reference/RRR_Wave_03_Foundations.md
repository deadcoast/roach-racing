## 5. DEVELOPED OPERATIONAL PROJECTS

### W03.PROJECT.HOPPER — Complementary-stock breeding

**PCR connection:** obtain an improved working component; understand the parameter it improves; fit it to a personally assembled racer.

**RRR extension:** produce that component from selected live stock rather than only purchasing a finished part.

**End-product target, fixture protocol:** spring return at least 0.68; phase error at most 0.03; settling time at most 0.08. These normalized thresholds do not establish final game tiers.

**Current project:** high return is already represented; repeatable synchronization is missing.

**Candidate criterion:** phase error at most 0.03 under the matching assay. This is deliberately different from the end-product target. A low-return high-synchrony specimen can be the right founder while being the wrong finished product.

**Where:** Switchyard trait run; grassy embankments and sleeper edges. **Inspect:** paired release during short hops; both hind joints intact. **Why preserve it:** introduce the missing useful characteristic. **Why retain other stock:** immediate assemblies, complementary later crosses, reproductive replacements, appearance or ordinary sale.

**Field observation limit:** seeing a good jump is evidence of phenotype under those conditions, not an exact genotype display. The executable intake uses visible observations only.

**Mechanical witness:** one three-locus hopper model connects inherited return, phase error and settling to component behavior. General fertility, mutation, linkage and other donor profiles remain outside its implementation scope.

### W03.PROJECT.WHOLESALE — Useful intermediates pay for the project

**Product:** ordinary prepared hopper assemblies. **Fixture order:** four accepted units at 20 credits each; 80 credits reserved by a funded buyer; arrival deadline tick 8.

**Acceptance:** return at least 0.60; phase error at most 0.08 under the named protocol. The ordinary order does not demand the premium breeding project's final genotype.

**Disposition of eight viable fixture offspring:** two retained replacements; four provide delivered assemblies; one provides a personal graft; one remains intact and unsold. Prepared donors retain their remaining recorded bodies and any associated care obligations. Delivering their hind-leg assemblies does not teleport the whole donor to the customer.

**Commercial purpose:** useful intermediate generations can fund continuing selection. The player does not wait for perfect offspring before the project has value.

**Boundary:** the eight survivors, maturation and noise-free acceptance observations are declared fixture inputs. This is not a forecast of actual brood yield or deadline reliability.

### W03.PROJECT.DRIVE — Income becomes a racing improvement

**PCR connection:** select a higher-performing motor, while retaining a separate choice of gear ratio and contact/handling setup.

**Donor:** Furnace cicada. **Where:** Cooling-kiln power run and appropriate rooted/pipe habitat. **Select for:** cadence under controlled load and useful force; do not substitute loudness or body size for performance evidence.

**Acquisition:** buy a measured prepared drive, catch a candidate, or supply a complete breeding/rearing facility. The route changes cost and history, not an invisible performance multiplier.

**Normalized fixture profiles:** ordinary cadence 10 / stall force 2; improved cadence 12 / stall force 2.5. The bench fixes mass, stride geometry, delivery and resistance. Higher speed is permitted and can consume more work without an arbitrary new penalty.

**Boundary:** the integrated ledger buys an actual prepared drive asset. That part remains in inventory; no installation or race win is awarded from the receipt.

## 7. STATE AND TRANSACTION CONTRACTS

### Public evidence versus internal truth

`world genotype → controlled or field observation → observer knowledge → project recommendation`

The internal model can enumerate genotype support for tests. The player-facing intake cannot read those truth fields. Same visible evidence means same default advice even when two specimens have different hidden genomes. A missing observation requests review; it does not become zero, false, guaranteed rare or guaranteed useless.

### Future stage occupancy

`reserve parents + incubation + grow-out + handling → start → care → stage transitions → mature → allocate output`

Reservations use stage-specific resources and half-open time intervals. A brood expected to produce six survivors from up to twelve initiated young does not fit a ten-place grow-out facility under the safe preset merely because its average is six. Use real overflow, another schedule, a smaller initial commitment or an explicitly risky alternative.

A delayed live cohort retains its obligation. Future unstarted jobs can pause and release their whole reservation bundles. Conflicts with other already-living cohorts require actual supported relocation or another declared consequence; the model refuses to manufacture capacity.

### Anatomy and custody

`whole donor → explicit disjoint extraction → prepared sect → loose inventory / kit custody / installed host / transferred buyer`

One Hopper assembly can satisfy hind-limb, jump and landing roles while remaining one asset. It is not three saleable products. A whole-set harvest and a joint-only harvest sharing the same tissue are alternatives; different scarab regions can supply multiple disjoint products.

The witness enforces harvest ownership, protections, source tissue, kit custody and simple installation custody. Full geometry, damage during preparation, storage, unpacking and removal require further runtime implementation.

### Funded order settlement

`buyer budget → reserved escrow → accepted arrival → simultaneous payment and custody transfer`

Unknown observations do not certify a selected order. Immediate ordinary spot sales remain a separate supported design path. One event ID commits once; a repeat with the same payload replays the receipt; the same ID with changed content is rejected. Whole delivery batches succeed or fail together.

A cancellation releases remaining buyer money under the contract rather than paying the seller an unearned balance. Arrival at the deadline tick precedes end-of-tick expiration. The current operator witness checks product, protocol bounds, custody, quantity, funding and deadline; full transport, deterioration and every condition predicate are not implemented.

## 8. EXECUTED WITNESSES

### Inheritance

`RRssDD × rrSSDD → RrSsDD` in the declared independent-locus additive fixture.

First-generation offspring can supply ordinary assemblies while not achieving the premium target. A cross of two three-locus heterozygotes has a 1/64 chance of the triple homozygous target; the previous two-locus target remains 1/16.
