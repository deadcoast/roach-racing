### The Derby Compact: a limited sporting agreement

`W19.CHARTER.COMPACT`

The four described factions and New World City’s host administration recognize a shared finish, a way to qualify, ordinary inspection, record exchange and responsibilities for advertised support. This proposed **Derby Compact** governs the programme it sanctions. It is not a peace treaty, universal licensing authority, global bank or explanation of every faction’s laws.

The **Circuit Registry** records which events confer advancement and whether an entrant has earned it. The **Stewards’ Table** hears sporting disputes under published procedures. The **Host Works Office** contracts the city’s physical venue, receiving capacity and event labor. No one office simultaneously sells the entrants their parts, selects a favorite winner and reviews its own complaint.

Ordinary informal races and shops can exist outside Compact sanction. They produce real local outcomes and enjoyment. They cannot advertise a Derby berth unless that consequence is actually recognized. The point of recognition is shared trust in a specific result, not permission to run a roach in a gutter.

A source record, product qualification, maker advertisement and race result remain different claims. Race officials inspect capability and the actual fitted entry. They do not demand research-grade ancestry for every organ, and no manufacturer owns the assimilation interface.

The Compact is intentionally narrow enough to let disagreeing societies compete. It protects a common start and finish while leaving who breeds, what people collect, and which local styles they enjoy open.

### Four records that must not be merged

`W19.CHARTER.ENTRY`

**Lanista entry.** The responsible person or declared stable team holds a nontransferable competitive record for a discipline and campaign. The ordinary solo player satisfies this role; team administration is optional depth.

**Host passport.** Each living roach carries its own identity and race history. Owning a former winner does not transfer the previous lanista’s qualification. Selling it does not delete what that animal did.

**Build entry.** Each heat freezes the particular host, actual components and permitted settings after inspection. A later fitting change creates another configuration; it cannot alter a past finish or the product depicted in a recorded win.

**Faction delegation.** A registered lanista’s earned Factional result supplies a specific faction seat for one discipline and edition. Support people, loaned stock and sponsors can be named without becoming competing duplicate entries.

Qualifications therefore allow a lanista to continue improving and using their stable between meetings. The host that earned an earlier win remains credited, even when another owned or legitimately loaned host enters the next meeting. This is a new proposed entry-right definition, not a retroactive mutation of earlier historical fixtures.

Within a meeting the entered host remains fixed unless a separately registered reserve rule explicitly permits replacement between heats. No mid-heat host exchange occurs. The standard championship templates below use one fixed host per meeting; a withdrawn animal is not replaced invisibly. Refit windows change anatomy, not which living animal crossed the previous finish.

A host and responsible operator cannot occupy incompatible simultaneous commitments. Entering several disciplines requires a workable schedule and the actual inspection/fitting intervals; a roster line does not create another body, caregiver or set of loaned parts.

### Affiliation is a sporting commitment, not a species unlock

`W19.CHARTER.AFFILIATION`

A person may enter ordinary local Open competition without joining a faction. For the representative ladder, they declare one sporting affiliation for the campaign through a recognized club before entering their Regional qualifier. A supported contact address can be a club, household, workshop or carrier yard. No ancestry, housing ownership, workshop profession, reputation grind or unrelated faction task is required.

Affiliation is consistent across that campaign’s divisions. Ground Open success does not itself qualify Flyer Open; each discipline has its own results. A buyer who never breeds can follow either ladder using suitable actual stock and personal assembly.

Changing faction after a Regional qualification preserves the historical results but ends that edition’s route to a representative seat under the previous affiliation. The competitor can enter the receiving faction’s next announced Regional campaign with the recognized Local record, subject to that edition’s public eligibility period. An already-issued world seat returns through the recorded reserve process; it is not carried as private property into another flag.

A sponsor change is not a faction change. Declining a commercial condition does not nullify an earned sporting result. A public finish cannot be converted into a contract requiring surrender of the champion’s descendants or purchase of an exclusive brand.

A faction member may buy from, breed stock originating in, or sell to another faction. Public identity can be strong without making the biology proprietary. Claimed ownership of a particular animal remains an ordinary custody question, independent of this freedom to participate.

### An earned route through four different scopes

`W19.CHARTER.LADDER`

| Stage | Requirement | What a win supplies | What it does not supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Local | Eligible personally prepared racer; no prior title | A recognized Local circuit win in the declared discipline | A world invitation, rare genes or professional status |
| Regional | Local win and campaign affiliation | Entry to the corresponding faction’s selection final | A committee-selected berth or permission to enter another locomotion division |
| Factional | Regional qualification under that affiliation | The published faction seat and contracted travel/support offer | Ownership transfer of the host or guaranteed world placing |
| New World City Derby | Earned faction seat, ready inspected host and event participation | A valid final determines the discipline champion | A title from damage, spectator popularity or the largest purchased stable |

Each qualifier advertises its winner condition before entry. Supporting heats can determine the qualifying final or a declared series winner; the player is not required to win every heat. There is no hidden quota of repeated identical local wins. Additional legitimate attempts are distinct events with actual competition, not a replay payout.

The worked atlas gives each of its four signatories one primary world seat and one ranked standby per active discipline. These are seats for this four-signatory reference format, not the worldwide maximum roster. Further factions or a larger event require published heats and capacity. A standby must have earned the recorded Factional rank; the seller of the winner’s host is not the standby.

If the primary cannot attend, the seat offers to the eligible standby under the same declared support terms. If neither can field a valid entrant, the seat remains vacant. It cannot be auctioned, assigned to a donor, or filled by an invented NPC result. A world title needs a completed competitive final; an administrative no-contest is not silently converted into a victory.

Qualification belongs to an edition, while the historical victory is permanent. New seasons create new competitions without deleting living collections or old achievements. Time advances within the world; leaving the application does not consume an entry window under the default pause policy.

A factional programme needs enough real qualified entrants. A single region may host several separately funded, recognized Regional meetings in a campaign; the atlas entries name families, not the only four occurrences. One lanista occupies at most one place in a discipline’s Factional roster. Repeated wins do not clone their entry. If too few distinct winners qualify, use the announced further qualifying opportunity or smaller valid format rather than silently inventing three defeated NPC champions.

### The divisions stay recognizable at every scale

`W19.CHARTER.CLASSES`

**Ground Open.** Dedicated offense, protective constructions and inventive combinations are welcome. The route, start, valid completion and actual outer boundaries still apply. No universal part-point ceiling or donor-family ban is introduced.

**Flyer Open.** Sustained powered-flight entrants use an aerial course and compete against Flyers. The build freedom of Open remains. Passive aero, ballistic hopping and ordinary ground running are not relabeled flight to share its grid.

**Speedsters.** The preparation has no operative dedicated weapon. Native biting, collision and opportunistic bodily contact remain part of RRR. Merely selecting a peaceful race plan does not disable a fitted weapon. A genuinely altered non-attacking preparation can qualify on its actual capability.

**Fireworks.** The fitted construction has functioning offensive anatomy meeting the advertised minimum and remains within light protection. Cosmetic horns do not fulfill the purpose; heavy protection cannot evade the rule by calling itself a frame. Ground and Flyer Fireworks use their own venues and locomotion requirements.

**Ordinary kit and family-style support events.** Local clubs may offer known-content or assisted-building formats. These are access routes and social occasions, not compulsory first chapters or hidden bans on exciting horn/hopper kits. Their titles do not automatically advance another discipline.

An inspected dormant configuration can be entered only under a published capability procedure that physically excludes prohibited functions for the entire heat. A control-menu promise is not enough. The ordinary alternative is to swap the prepared anatomy at the bench. The exact mixed-locomotion border remains W18’s disclosed capability question, not an arbitrary list of insects permitted to grow wings.

<a id="w19-charter-format"></a>

### Shared race grammar with explicit experimental parameters

`W19.CHARTER.FORMAT`

The intended flagship competition uses simultaneous entrants with actual contact and passing space. Historical paired-heat software is not evidence that this exists as runtime. The following **M4 format** is a proposed four-entry content template, not a claim copied from PCR.

**Single final.** One valid race decides its winner. Published preliminary heats reduce a larger roster to four; each heat’s advancing places are stated before it begins. A final requires at least two actual starters and a valid winner. A field reduced below that records no contest and uses the announced rerun or postponement procedure.

**Four-race series.** The same four hosts race two declared layouts twice each, with an ordinary service/refit window between races. Valid placing earns 5, 3, 2 and 1 points; a nonfinish earns zero. Equal valid finishes split the occupied points. This authored schedule rewards race results, not attacks, sales, rarity or time spent in the nursery.

**Series order.** Points, then more race wins, then more valid finishes. A tie affecting the sole qualifying place receives an additional race on the announced tie-break layout after the same service allowance. Other unresolved places may be shared under published prize terms. A qualifying series winner must have at least three valid finishes; otherwise the qualifier is inconclusive and is rescheduled rather than awarding advancement by administrative survival alone.

**Starting fairness.** For the four-race template, the four starting positions rotate so every entrant starts in each position once; the two layouts are used in a publicly specified alternating order. For a single qualifying final, all entrants receive the same advertised familiarization access and the start positions are allocated by a witnessed draw. A different seeding method must be declared before registration, with the comparable inputs it uses; organizer preference is not seeding. The layout must be reviewed for remaining advantage; rotation is not proof that every geometrical bias has vanished. Wind or surface changes may require regrouping or restarting a genuinely affected heat under the same rule for all entrants.

**Tied single final.** If the available timing and finish evidence cannot separate entrants competing for one berth or title, the tied entrants run an additional race after the same declared care and fitting allowance. Already-separated places remain recorded. This resolves the same edition and does not issue another purse. If no valid finish occurs, the unresolved final is rerun or rescheduled; it is not awarded from attack count or stock value. A sole valid finisher in a race that began with at least two actual eligible starters wins by completing the course, not by merely remaining alive.

**No imported retirement law.** Self-righting, legal hops and declared gate retries follow actual RRR capability and course rules. Tamiya’s immediate retirement rules are not copied merely because the source has them. Receiving after the finish and scored course completion remain distinct.

### A won seat needs a usable journey

`W19.CHARTER.SUPPORT`

A qualifier must advertise the support floor before accepting entries: outward and return carriage, declared stock/case allowance, ordinary receiving, entry costs and temporary care or an equivalent funded arrangement. Exact rates await the economy and scale chapters; the obligations do not disappear because numbers are pending.

Before confirming the final qualifier edition, the organizer reserves the physical or funded minimum. If it cannot, it must revise or postpone the advertised offer before competitors commit. Later loss of a provider requires rebooking, agreed postponement or an openly documented failed undertaking—not making the winner perform an unrelated grind to reclaim the journey already promised.

The support floor enables attendance, not a guaranteed win or a free optimum loadout. Optional premium components, additional animals, extra staff and unusual personal consignments are separately paid and carried. A bought part, a faction loan, a gifted brood and a prize animal each retain the correct ownership. Support lending states return condition and responsibility; a loan is not automatically confiscated to cover a race loss.

The service plan includes home obligations the entrant chooses to retain: a booked caretaker can support already-committed stock while the player travels. It does not operate a secret perfect breeding business or pause one life cycle while competitors’ lives advance. A nonbreeding racer may need only their cases and a return slot.

A concise travel card presents the ready route and the first unresolved requirement. Specialists can inspect each custody and care entry. The reference must not turn every handover into a separate compulsory minigame; ordinary complete arrangements bundle ordinary work once.

### Inspection protects the result without owning the invention

`W19.CHARTER.INSPECTION`

Before a heat the steward checks the actual discipline, course envelope, prohibited capabilities, available readiness and declared stock. Prepared components can be remounted during published service intervals and inspected again. The entry is fixed from its accepted inspection to the end of that heat, apart from actual physical changes caused during racing.

Open permits attacks between competitors within the declared course. That does not permit a human to interfere with another racer, secretly alter a surface, change a rival’s case or manipulate a result. Protective course boundaries and spectator separation must suit the event; they are not an excuse to remove RRR’s stated offense from every competitive class.

A protest identifies a rule, an actual event and available evidence. The local desk can settle a simple mistake; a contested decision goes to stewards without a direct interest in the outcome. A missing camera view is uncertainty, not permission to invent a confident reconstruction. Publication distinguishes provisional and final classification.

Material failure, outside interference, organizer error and an ordinary losing build are different cases. A builder can lose because a legal opponent outperformed them. No automatic compensation applies to every loss. An invalid qualifier can be rerun under its declared remedy instead of granting private points to a favorite.

No new racing rule is quietly applied retroactively. Necessary emergency changes are announced, the affected entry is reassessed, and a fair opportunity is offered before scoring proceeds. These are fictional sporting procedures, not a claim about current real-world law.

<a id="w19-charter-session"></a>

### Keep the full hobby available during a campaign

`W19.CHARTER.SESSION`

A race attendance is a world activity; a replay is a record. Broods, care and actual scheduled commitments progress only with the corresponding world time. Reading a rule, comparing known builds, browsing an issue or pausing on a completed result does not initiate a second race or invoice.

Local practice, rival challenges, displays and ordinary commerce continue when an advertised championship edition closes. A known local champion may still enjoy a new stock kit. The player does not have to sell their old roach because the competitive ladder moved on.

A campaign card shows one chosen next ambition with alternatives: refit, race locally, visit a source, fulfill a job, prepare for travel or stop. Changing that intention does not edit the source population, cancel somebody else’s life stage or compel a new contract. Later seasonal content adds actual opponents, courses and customers while permanent records and owned stock remain.

Spectators, makers and collectors can visit New World City’s public hobby. A world entry record is needed for the Derby grid, not for the right to appreciate a kit or buy an ordinary component at its market. A fast career remains attainable through purchased stock, while detailed source work is available to those who choose it.

### Tinwater / First Current route

`W19.MEET.FIRST`

### Cinderhouse / Rivet Reach Regional

`W19.MEET.RIVET`

### Four factional finals; one sporting basis

`W19.MEET.FACTIONAL`

**Common eligibility.** A Regional winner, correct campaign affiliation, eligible ready host, and the declared discipline. The reference final has four entrants and one primary representative place. Where the qualified field is larger, advertised preliminary racing determines the four. No workshop judge substitutes a preferred sponsored entrant.

**Common Bench Final — Rivet Commons.** An M4 programme alternates mixed ground or equivalent declared aerial layouts with a common service interval. Shared work tables let spectators recognize real changes. It is not a judged build-off; the series winner earns the seat.

**Crown Trials — Reedwater Compact.** The appropriate ground or Flyer M4 programme exposes supply, working-root and controlled-recovery differences. Stage-care and receiving demonstrations occur alongside it, without contributing points. The winner and standby receive booked biological support.

**Lantern Exchange Final — Lantern Exchange.** Racing and appearance display have separate judges and result records. Public build cards can be shared with permission; private pedigrees are not forcibly published as a condition of the berth. The racing winner and standby are recorded before commercial invitations.

**Moving Final — Traverse Freeholds.** A published portable layout travels to the declared host yard. Its relocation procedure and reserve venue are part of entry information. Winning confers the same standing as any fixed-hall final; itinerant origin is not a lower class of qualification.

Each final records a primary winner and a ranked eligible standby. A tied standby position uses the same advertised sporting tie-break, not a donor’s bid. All four cultures can support fast, offensive, restrained or aerial constructions within the relevant rules. The faction identity changes the people and resources around the racer, not how its tissue is scored.

### The What’s Left of the World Derby

`W19.MEET.DERBY`

**Reference field.** One earned representative from each of the four described signatories per active discipline. Standbys replace genuinely unavailable delegates before the published cutoff, not after seeing whether a first heat was favorable. A larger future signatory field requires a separately published qualifying programme.

**Public headliners.** Ground Open is the central ground title; Flyer Open is the aerial headline. Speedsters and Fireworks receive their own titles where staged. There is no comparison converting flight time, ground time and destructive effect into one fictitious universal champion score. Open remains the most famous construction class in the public framing.

**Sporting resolution.** Equal-exposure preparation and declared seeding precede a single final per discipline. The first valid finisher wins that final. Actual ties use the announced rerun with unchanged qualifying standing and an ordinary refit/inspection interval. A no-contest does not produce a title. The full intended final is simultaneous physical racing, not a claim that the historical pair solver already supports it.

**Course character.** The ground championship combines long pace runs, legible shared traffic, a disclosed surface change and at least two approach opportunities. The aerial championship combines actual height, broad passing, clear gates and one meaningful alternative route. No exclusive organ is made the only way to finish. Novel builds must satisfy actual validity, not conform to the silhouette on the festival poster.

**Material consequence.** A prize is actual money, stock, service or object under advertised terms. A large cup, a faction banner and a breeder’s publication can all commemorate the result but do not improve inherited characteristics. A recorded winning build can support a maker inquiry, not guarantee descendants or copy its anatomy.

**Afterward.** The champion retains the animal, line records and personal projects they actually own. The host city remains a place for ordinary participation, and the returning hometown retains its own identity. The final closes a campaign without deleting the rest of the hobby.

### Common Bench Hall

`W19.CITY.BENCH`

**Purpose.** Bookings give entrants actual working space and ordinary test access. Regions may decorate their areas but cannot obstruct neighboring participants or monopolize all common tools.
