# MIVIM — Wave v0.8 contribution review

**Objective:** An individually sourced or bred whole host carries its native geometry into personal kit assembly, persistent stable custody and the same racing system, with measurement-led component and lineage evidence.
**Comparator:** One fixed roach recolored as several models; kit purchase spawns a fitted racer; each bench duplicates shared parts; names or rarity labels award synthetic performance.
**Hard gates:** `quality_at_declared_scope AND novel_development_adherence_at_declared_scope`.
No authorized numerical scoring formula or weights are invented.

## Quality
Implemented model, interactions and reviewed renders at declared local-prototype scope. Complete game and human usability gate open.

## Novel Development Adherence
Concrete project-specific whole-organism, kit, ownership and evidence integration. No worldwide novelty claim.

## Effectiveness
Demonstrated for tested body selection, purchased/caught/bred host registration, fitting, races, custody, backup and public evidence.

## Creativity
Distinct whole-kit presentation and shared biological origin-to-racer path delivered; experiential originality remains unmeasured.

## Scaling Potential
Reusable body/specimen profiles and shared command paths; a small local stable is not a large-world or multi-server benchmark.

## Supporting assurance
Schema, reference checks, numerical parity and custody tests protect the intended integration. Their existence is not proof of fun or commercial success.

## Repairs discovered through development
Host-aware native replacement accounting had to distinguish dry structure from declared loaded reserve; added reservoir energy mass is now carried.
Cross-host availability needed both parked drafts and fitted components, not merely the active bench.
A broad loaded kit could stall indefinitely at zero forward speed beside a rival. Wrapped proximity and a bounded powered sidestep repair the deadlock without a free lateral teleport.
A whole-host shop initially reused one generic donor glyph. The current live shelf uses the visible native plan as the stable and kit lid do.
Lineage guidance was initially repeated twice in stock cards; one evidence-based reason now leads the card.
The single lineage record now fills its available row; narrow kit, bench and stable layouts remain separately inspected.

## Still unproven
Human usability, perceived distinctiveness, retention, world-scale runtime and economic balance. Source-game fidelity is functional, not a claim of copied hidden formulas.
