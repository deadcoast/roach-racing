# RRR — Wave v0.18
## Twelve Interpretation Amendments

These amend named qualitative meanings, not historical test inputs. [Main volume](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md) · [Research](RRR_Wave_18_Research.md)

<a id="w18-amend-01"></a>

### Longwing is a differentiated four-surface flight array

`W18.AMEND.01`

**Prior entries.** [W14.PART.LONGWING](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-longwing) · [W16.PREP.LONGWING](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-longwing)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Earlier shorthand described long paired surfaces without specifying the forewing/fan-hindwing distinction.

**Current definition.** The complete reference array contains a left/right forewing pair and a left/right fan-hindwing pair with their required roots. Surface-only and complete-rooted packages are different offerings.

**Purpose.** Removes a misleading two-wing shortcut and gives folding, root control and useful deformation explicit design jobs.

<a id="w18-amend-02"></a>

### Wingbeat coordination is not universal synchronization

`W18.AMEND.02`

**Prior entries.** [W14.MAP.GAIT](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-map-gait) · [W14.PART.LOCKSTEP](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-lockstep)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Paired release precision can be desirable for a Hopper and was adjacent to general coordination language.

**Current definition.** Hopper bilateral release, fore/hind flight phase and transmission leverage are distinct. Cyclic arrays may need a useful relative phase rather than simultaneous motion everywhere.

**Purpose.** Prevents the best jump synchronizer from becoming an automatic best-in-slot flight gearbox.

<a id="w18-amend-03"></a>

### A flight-drive label does not include the entire flight system

`W18.AMEND.03`

**Prior entries.** [W14.PART.REDLINE](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-redline) · [W14.SET.SKY](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-set-sky)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Adapting a drive for flight could be read as supplying new control, four wings and a second complete running transmission.

**Current definition.** Every output path, yoke, supported root, control response and retained native function is declared once. Prepared duty and mount change; undocumented duplicate systems do not appear.

**Purpose.** Keeps the motor/gears/body/reserve comparison faithful to its functional source.

<a id="w18-amend-04"></a>

### Air-relative motion refines the old stationary-aero shorthand

`W18.AMEND.04`

**Prior entries.** [W14.ANATOMY.RACE_READINGS](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-anatomy-race_readings) · [W14.PART.BROWFOIL](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-browfoil)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Earlier text rejected effects while stationary without always naming the reference frame.

**Current definition.** Aero depends on actual relative air interaction. A stationary-on-the-ground surface in moving air can be loaded; still air plus no relevant surface motion supplies no such force.

**Purpose.** Corrects a physically important oversimplification before adding airflow courses, without importing aircraft coefficients.

<a id="w18-amend-05"></a>

### Sweep and carrying envelopes are different

`W18.AMEND.05`

**Prior entries.** [W14.ANATOMY.BODYPLAN](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-anatomy-bodyplan) · [W16.MATERIAL.WING](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-wing)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A narrow packed body or folded wing could be mistaken for the width the racer occupies throughout powered flight.

**Current definition.** Record rest, active sweep and usable transition envelopes. Folded passage is legitimate only if the resulting mode can complete the movement with actual support and supply.

**Purpose.** Makes fit and aerial passing concrete without an arbitrary species-size ban.

<a id="w18-amend-06"></a>

### Rivetback names the previously unnamed recovery donor

`W18.AMEND.06`

**Prior entries.** [W14.PART.KICKBACK](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-kickback) · [W16.PREP.KICKBACK](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-kickback)

**Earlier ambiguity.** The reference named a click-beetle source but did not distinguish a source form, larva, adult or component lifecycle.

**Current definition.** Rivetback is the working name for that source. Adults supply the complete rooted hinge; larvae and pupae have their own roles and do not contain a ready miniature module.

**Purpose.** Closes the source monograph without inventing an additional independent recovery item.

<a id="w18-amend-07"></a>

### Recovery needs support and a compatible load path

`W18.AMEND.07`

**Prior entries.** [W14.ACTION.RIGHT](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-action-right) · [W16.MATERIAL.ELASTIC](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-elastic)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A powerful recovery snap could be interpreted as universally righting, airborne rescue or a timed reset.

**Current definition.** The actual support, clearance, loading anatomy and receiving contacts determine recovery. Lost time, route, material and energy are not restored.

**Purpose.** Gives a useful optional mechanism and its counterexamples rather than a spare-life mechanic.

<a id="w18-amend-08"></a>

### Boiler readiness and material supply are separate

`W18.AMEND.08`

**Prior entries.** [W14.PART.BOILER](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-boiler) · [W16.MATERIAL.GLAND](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-gland)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Recharge language can conflate control recovery with the production of new discharge contents.

**Current definition.** Mechanical readiness, fill, outlet condition and replenishment are separate. A short recovery interval cannot mint material; a loaded failed valve is not ready offense.

**Purpose.** Creates legitimate valve, supply and preparation work without a cooldown-based infinite ammunition loop.

<a id="w18-amend-09"></a>

### The Caustic juvenile uses a different production resource

`W18.AMEND.09`

**Prior entries.** [W16.BIO.01](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-bio-01) · [W15.BROOD.03](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-brood-03)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A generic paired adult and feeding drawer would miss a distinctive source lifecycle.

**Current definition.** This fictional Caustic profile uses a compatible identified Bankseed pupa during larval development. The support animal and donor then have different fates and inventory histories.

**Purpose.** Provides meaningful specialist supply while preserving direct purchase of a ready gland for ordinary racers.

<a id="w18-amend-10"></a>

### Aerial gates are route objects, not raised ground rails

`W18.AMEND.10`

**Prior entries.** [W14.SPORT.COURSE](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-sport-course) · [W15.RACING.02](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-racing-02)

**Earlier ambiguity.** An ordered air route could still be authored as one thin ground line at a fixed height.

**Current definition.** Define apertures, face direction, transition volumes, alternative gate sequences, working-body clearance and safe recovery spaces. Air movement and heights create real construction choices.

**Purpose.** Creates an aerial circuit identity while retaining owned modular pieces and comparable race completion.

<a id="w18-amend-11"></a>

### Glide, powered flight and hopping are inspected capabilities

`W18.AMEND.11`

**Prior entries.** [W14.SPORT.BOUNDARIES](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-sport-boundaries) · [W15.SPORT.05](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-sport-05)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A wing name or declared peaceful/ground plan could hide operative flight capability or wrongly ban a passive surface.

**Current definition.** Repeated powered support defines the enabled Flyer capability. Hops and passive glide follow the declared ground event policy; a promised lack of use is not physical disabling.

**Purpose.** Keeps Flyers with Flyers without turning every unusual silhouette into an arbitrary prohibited item.

<a id="w18-amend-12"></a>

### Missing anatomy documentation is not cured by a grade

`W18.AMEND.12`

**Prior entries.** [W14.MAP.SETS](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-map-sets) · [W15.GRADES.04](reference/W17/reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-grades-04)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A high-labelled wing, sensor or offensive organ can appear to cover several undeclared missing functions.

**Current definition.** Full-kit documentation must expose the work, supply, support, sensing, actuation, ground-contact and route requirements it actually satisfies. No high grade grants missing anatomy.

**Purpose.** Preserves substantial upgrades and entry accessibility while rejecting incomplete “complete” kits.
