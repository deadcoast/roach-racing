## Current-reference hierarchy

1. The explicit author premise and subsequent author corrections.
2. Named working definitions and explicit W14 interpretation amendments.
3. Older working proposals where still consistent and not specifically refined.
4. Experimental coefficients, prototype restrictions and results, used only within their named scope.

External references are evidence for their own facts, not an authority that can overwrite the fictional premise. Newly proposed W14 morphology and exchange practices remain open to revision.

## Historical context

The cumulative JSON retains W02–W13, the earlier seed records, and the W10 handoff gap as they were. W14 appends a new compendium volume; it does not assert that unrecovered original W10 code was recovered. No old test or runtime is rerun in this reference-only edition.

## Next authoring sequence

**W15.C01 — Donor monographs: inheritance, life history and selection depth.** For each selected form: life stages, reproductive eligibility, heritable variation, morphology, developmental confounders, useful-stage anatomy, founder selection, population differences, preservation and distinct customers.

**W15.C02 — The living material lifecycle: preparation, recovery and preservation.** Product-specific raw preparation, exchange, supported-body handling, storage states, material loss, transport and repair definitions, with a clearly proposed injury/aging philosophy.

**W15.C03 — Biosphere, scale and chronology.** A consistent proposed post-winter recovery account, creature scale reference, resource ecology and explicit separation of apocalypse time from the sport’s thirty-year history.

**W15.C04 — Factional sport and regional supply atlas.** Named factions and regions with livelihoods, ecological sources, trade, club culture, representation process, championship role and everyday participants.

**W15.C05 — Flyer division and complete aerial kit handbook.** Wing/drive/linkage/sensing combinations, aerial track grammar, preparation, viable landing and race distinctions; supply businesses and kit forms included.

**W15.C06 — Catalog editions, appearance inheritance and hobby history.** Natural variants versus finishes, supported inheritance proposals, breeder/product histories, historic originals versus revivals, collector claims and usable artifact briefs.
