THE BENCH

Select a sect in the tray. Drag it onto the host, or use Fit to draft. Move fitted roots directly, use the arrow controls, or focus a component and use keyboard arrows. Shift makes larger steps. Graft positions have visible supported ranges; clashes must be resolved before fitting.

THE BUILD

Draft changes are previews. Fit this build commits all changes together. Returning a sect to the tray uses a supported, reusable bench-graft fixture; it does not establish global painless removal or infinite tissue regrowth. Prepared native exchange units are retained in the bench cradle and reinstalled when a replacement is returned. This is a disclosed fixture, not free regrowth.

THE RACE

The browser calculates a new two-roach path-model race from your committed parts and settings. It is not a library of pre-rendered results. Open, Speedsters, and light-shell Fireworks apply to actual fitted weapons. Preload affects jumping only when a Hopper is fitted and its approach is enabled. Race attendance advances the same world clock and care as collecting and breeding. Replay does not. Full limb physics, destructive damage and flight remain open.

STOCK & SAVING

H-6 retains its Wave 3 B1-6 donor and measured inherited properties. UPGRADE-DRIVE is the previously purchased drive. Six further modules and workshop facilities are explicit starter endowments for this experiment. Historical stock measurements are disclosed fixtures. New field stock begins unmeasured; assay or preparation reveals a controlled profile. Local autosave may be restricted by your browser; export sheets as portable backups. Imports restore recipes against this stock, never create ownership or race wins.

HOSTS, KITS & LINES

Stable & lines keeps each owned roach, its draft and its installed parts. Complete tins include a real host: buy, open, bring it to the bench, and optionally lay out the guide before personally fitting. Shared parts must be removed from another host before transfer. Whole host breeding uses a declared inherited body plan and three physical traits; acquired grafts do not reproduce. Named lines report observed descendants, not guaranteed future yields. Component grades describe named measured profiles only.

ONE LIVING WORLD

Live stock now includes twelve profiled donor forms plus host stock, their distinct stage nurseries, a two-way live shop and actual conversion kits, plus, protected breeders, preparation, real funded sales and a care ledger. Turn lengths, prices, capacities and biology are explicit prototypes. The opening founders, equipment, 142 credits and existing sects are disclosed starting assets. Supported donor bodies still receive care. If cash is exhausted, emergency boarding credit records the care debt and blocks new commitments; club jobs or stock sales provide recovery routes. No wall-clock farming or surprise racer death.

TRACKWORKS

Eight Gutter Runs and four Bends are disclosed starter sections. Snap a draft using owned pieces, correct open connectors and floor overlap, then lay and publish a version. Extra sections are finite purchases. Saved plans grant no inventory. Brake Rise has a low launch lip: a low pad drags on its approach; a raised pad does not. A short landing before a bend can cause a course-out. Rail shoes reduce losses only during wall contact; Ride the rails chooses an outer curve line. The model is still a simplified path-space ground race. A funded club layout job pays once for actual work, not for replay.

CLUB MEETS

Enter a published card with your committed racer. Three paired rounds use real rival builds and swapped starting sides. Refit the same host between rounds. Valid finishes earn points; the final table pays its funded purse once. A Local Open win can qualify for Regional and supply one protected donor pair. Replays are free. Four roster entries do not mean simultaneous four-roach physics. The competition code continues Wave11. Player-made kits and breeder commissions are explicitly rebuilt from the written maker requirements in Wave12.

Whole-world backups preserve your expedition. Original quick routes remain available; no career or championship gate is introduced.

MAKER COUNTER

Publish a committed reference construction without consuming it. Choose an alternate measured host, match actual loose components, preview their fit, and pack one physical kit. Inspect exact working ranges: a substitute must satisfy the recipe as well as the final geometry. Packing costs four credits and one care turn. The original racer remains intact. The recipient opens and assembles the kit; a recipe does not duplicate stock. Breeder commissions purchase an intact observed descendant A/B pair and require distinct measured broods. Publication and accepting orders do not earn cash; qualifying delivery does. Your approval can publish a completed champion round and request one funded product enquiry. Numeric limits and customer quantities are authored finite prototype briefs.
