## Playable route

**09 · Maker counter:** publish a committed build, or explicitly choose a completed club round. Set the printed measurements, choose an actual alternate host and qualifying loose components, inspect whole-kit fit, then pack. A funded accepted order pays only on delivery. The same sealed product appears at Two Nets. Opening it makes a native host and real components available; loading its guide creates a draft, not a completed racer.

A separate breeder path selects an existing line, measures descendants across broods, and delivers an eligible actual A/B pair. Ordinary buying, catch-and-sell, component production, building and racing remain available without this career.

## The useful comparison to Pocket Circuit

The complete kit retains the source hobby’s foundation plus selected interchangeable parts and personally performed assembly. The maker chooses what a repeated kit promises; the breeding operation supplies suitable biological instances. A race-winning individual is neither consumed by publication nor cloned when its construction is reproduced. Gear ratio, foot release, drive efficiency, body support and native mass retain their separate purposes.

The maker does not promise equal race times for every substitute. The recipe states component measurements, native requirements and fit. Other individual properties, settings, course, traffic and actions can differ. A reference victory remains one historical result.

## Printed requirements and real supply

| Function | Requirements represented | Supply reason |
|---|---|---|
| Host/body | Same stated plan; observed native mass ceiling, stride and support minimums | Select a foundation that can fulfill the construction rather than merely match its name. |
| Drive | Cadence, useful force, conversion efficiency and mass | Select actual cicada or scarab musculature; gearing remains independent. |

| Reserve / damper | Capacity and delivery versus separate rebound properties | Separate physical donors are required where both products consume one region. |
| Brakes / guides | Operating friction/travel; low rail loss and adequate support | The intended surface interaction determines the requested material. |
| Stabilizers | Actual sensing response or a specified support arrangement | A sensor is not silently substituted for a brace because both share a catalogue heading. |
| Horn / claws / aero | Useful geometry with actual impact, timing, resistance and mass | Match the promised construction; no universal longer-is-better tier. |

Starting margins of 0, 10, 15 or 25 percent are disclosed conveniences. Explicit per-measurement bounds can replace them at publication, but must cover the chosen reference. Geometry has bounded ranges. Each published recipe is immutable and receives a new identity. Version-series editing and alternate-plan adaptation remain later work.
