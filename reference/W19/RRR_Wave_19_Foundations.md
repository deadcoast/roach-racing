## 1. Reference and authority

<a id="w19-premise"></a>

### Four factions, six settlements, one shared hobby

`W19.PREMISE`

The atlas defines a connected reference region, not the entire planet. Four proposed civic factions, six settlement entries and their clubs share a sporting agreement while retaining different ordinary livelihoods, local priorities and construction traditions. Tinwater Junction, Cinderhouse and Saltglass Exchange are existing places. Crown Basin and Spanrest are new settlement proposals. New World City is the author-established championship destination, not an invented replacement capital or proof of a world government.

The Rivet Commons, Reedwater Compact, Lantern Exchange and Traverse Freeholds are new named factions. Their membership, politics and geography are proposals introduced here. Existing Wrangloids, breeders, carriers and publications are not retroactively owned by those factions. A high wave number does not elevate new fiction above the author’s premises.

A faction can support benches, water, transport or a representative. It cannot change the properties of a purchased insect. A region can favor particular supply and racing traditions without every resident using the same build. A newcomer can still buy a complete tin and race locally; a trader can visit the world market without earning a championship berth.

The governing promise stays visible: personally assemble a living racer, enjoy its speed and identity, and try to win. The surrounding world supplies other complete pleasures—an exceptional capture, a useful brood, a paid piece of craft, a display, a memorable rival—not mandatory steps before the real game begins.

<a id="w19-layers"></a>

### Geography, representation and supply are different maps

`W19.LAYERS`

A settlement is a place with water, food, work and residents. A region is a useful travel/ecological grouping. A faction is a civic organization capable of maintaining agreements and backing representatives. A competition circuit is a set of recognized events. A supply route is a practical connection for people, stock, goods and information. Their boundaries can overlap without being identical.

The current atlas is topological. It states useful connections, receiving points and optional detours; it does not assert a compass bearing, distance, travel duration, country analogue or global climate. No route is priced by an invented number of kilometers. Broad environmental recovery and absolute scale are treated separately in the scope paper.

The sporting title applies to a declared discipline: locomotion plus equipment policy. Ground Open and Flyer Open share an Open construction principle but not one mixed race. Speedsters and Fireworks have their own corresponding ground or Flyer programmes where the venue can support them. A faction may fail to field one discipline without making that sport disappear from the world.

The four factions are the described signatories in this reference region, not a maximum faction count. The worked world-final format covers their four earned berths. Further signatories require a declared qualifying format and capacity; they do not appear as automatic empty podium places.

## 2. Six settlements and their ordinary work

<a id="w19-region-tinwater"></a>

### Tinwater Junction — an attainable first bench

`W19.REGION.TINWATER`

**Existing place refined.** Pump repair, reclaimed-water work and freight servicing support the settlement. Whitewashed interiors, patched enamel, readable shelves and clean working tanks contrast with worn industrial exteriors. It is one possible beginning, not the mandatory start location.

**Local hobby.** Foundry Club remains a local cooperative venue. Mara Venn’s Two Nets still sells whole insects, prepared sects and coordinated projects; Jess Rill remains a pump attendant and catcher; Ivo Pell remains a freight clerk who prefers buying prepared stock. None receives an invented championship history. Nera Kest publishes only what Benchline has actually learned.

**Sources.** Switchyard Verge, Split-sleeper bank, Ballast trench and Service culvert remain their existing habitats. Rad-roach foundations, Hoppers and mole-cricket contributions support complete local projects. Observing release timing or intact contact anatomy can make the same familiar route newly useful when a project changes. Tinwater imports conditioned flight arrays and specialist gland preparations rather than offering every lifecycle in one back room.

**Race tradition.** Short mixed layouts place an ordinary fast run next to one clear passing or recovery opportunity. First Current remains an existing local Open example, not a universal sole gateway. A quiet reference run, a funded qualifying meet and a show evening use different parts of the club programme. An imported kit can compete on its actual merits.

**Accessible project.** Tin Can Special provides a real host and the printed contact/guide edition. A local Hopper conversion is available without requiring a premium lineage. The owner can build and race before choosing whether to inspect a wild source or a breeder’s ledger.

**Economic opportunity.** Stock intake, prepared-contact supply, receiving work, routine fitting and communal-course setup can all earn under actual demand. A common donor buys no guaranteed sale; a customer’s useful ordinary specification is what creates an outlet.

**Faction relationship.** Tinwater is a proposed Rivet Commons member, while Foundry and Two Nets retain their own assets. The Commons can fund shared access; it does not seize Mara’s breeding line or require party membership to purchase a Hopper.

**Local tension and visible objects.** Bench space used for visiting representatives can crowd out regulars; funded extra sessions solve capacity more honestly than secretly cancelling local access. The club board visibly separates booked work, open benches, practice and qualifying races. Visitors see named racers and actual kits, not a wall of progression locks.

### Cinderhouse — support under load

`W19.REGION.CINDER`

**Existing place refined.** Heat recovery, repair workshops and reusable hardware are its ordinary economy. Recovered heat supports work; it is not food or a source of mutation-powered energy. Food, plant matter, Bankseed stock where relevant and ready living materials enter through actual producers and carriers.

**Local hobby.** Cinderhouse Broodworks remains an independent cooperative supplier. The new Loadhouse Club borrows workshop space for racing rather than owning every industrial floor. Members value a construction that works again after transport, fitting and an ordinary rough heat. Some still campaign very light sprint racers; regional identity is not a mandatory heavy-build trait.

**Sources and imports.** Cooling compost and furnace-bed scarabs supply separate force, inner support and outer-protection projects. Bullhorn stock creates contact-shape work. Forceful drive, light supporting plates and protective coverage must be compared independently. Fast-release Glassfoot material and conditioned arrays are sensible imports rather than locally spawned rewards.

**Race tradition.** Boiler Rise, Rivet Wash and long return straights create decisions about loaded starts, contact recovery and how much speed to carry. Every advertised obstacle must offer another plausible response: source quality, fit, stance, reduced unnecessary load or a different legal approach. A Boxbrace is not automatically the answer to a poor landing, and a broad host receives no regional acceleration aura.

**Accessible project.** Foundry Heavy combines an appropriate host, Torque Bundle, suitable Lowstep and actual support. The printed set may retain native contact where adequate. An ordinary complete kit and an expert local reinforcement commission can serve different customers without inventing a rare-gear entrance fee.

**Economic opportunity.** Repeated-dimension plates, customer-stock support work, section repairs and ordinary torque preparations supply everyday business. A premium research line may run beside ordinary accepted stock; neither output is certified by the town’s reputation alone.

**Faction relationship and tension.** The Rivet Commons can call on workshop labor for travelling events. That request competes with water-pump repairs and paid orders. The sporting office pays for a defined replacement shift or books ahead; winning does not confiscate a worker’s stock or leave their dependents’ service unstaffed.

**Material identity.** Open rib samples, load-direction marks and repaired reusable section cases communicate the local specialty. A finished tin records a real prepared support path rather than a generic steel-colored bonus frame.

### Crown Basin — the stages before the wings

`W19.REGION.BASIN`

**New settlement proposal.** Crown Basin is the inhabited service settlement beside the already described Cooling Works ponds and reed margins. This adjacency is new authored geography, not a claim that every earlier Cooling Works patch was part of a mapped municipality. The settlement maintains catchment channels, grows food, repairs water hardware and provides juvenile/emergence space.

**Local hobby.** Crown Loft is a proposed club with an outdoor practice volume, a sheltered introductory loft and an ordinary ground circuit. Inhabitants do not all fly. Ground racers buy contact and reserve stock here, and a new Flyer can buy a ready Skyhook tin rather than own a pond.

**Sources and imports.** Glasswing juveniles and conditioned adults, Longwing reed populations, Clockleaf useful stages and suitable wet-habitat Sumpflies support several distinct products. Water alone is not a productive nursery: prey or plant provisioning, usable stage space and actual reproductive stock are required. Root supports, cases, funds and prepared drives are imported when appropriate.

**Race tradition.** Broad climbing choices, distinct air gates and receiving aprons define the Crown circuit. A ground counterpart crosses a smooth causeway and declared loose inserts; it does not simulate flight by levitating the ground ribbon. Shorter direct aerial climbs and wider gradual climbs can suit different array/drive arrangements without guaranteeing equal speed.

**Accessible project.** Skyhook / Courtyard includes the array, appropriate drive and yoke, supply, control provisions and ordinary receiving support. Longline / Reedfold is the longer forward-flight alternative, not a higher numbered dragonfly. Both show their actual working and carrying shapes.

**Economic opportunity.** Paid grow-out, intact emergence, array matching, stage-aware transport and ordinary conditioned stock give the settlement livelihoods that are not all dependent on a world champion. A failed expansion can leave a reproductively eligible adult; it cannot fulfill an intact-array order by changing its label.

**Faction relationship.** The Reedwater Compact coordinates catchment work and shared facility commitments. Capacity bookings can be denied when genuinely full; the faction does not own a species or award permission to breed it.

**Local tension and objects.** Tournament visitors require support at the same time as developing cohorts. Reserving separate temporary arrival benches avoids emptying productive tanks for guests. Float-level markers, emergence rails, folded-frame cases and broad identification signs make both everyday work and race spectacle visible.

### Saltglass Exchange — good descriptions travel

`W19.REGION.SALTGLASS`

**Existing place refined.** Trade, glass repair, live-stock packing and careful display support the settlement. Tinted cases, sunshades and legible stock cards distinguish it from a universal rust-brown market. The Saltglass Counter remains a particular broker, not the town government or every resident’s employer.

**Local hobby.** The proposed Lantern Yard venue combines ground racing, an overhead course operated at separate times, and a finishing salon. Preparation samples and ordinary ready tins sit near unusual collector stock. A customer should not need premium ancestry paperwork to buy a dependable contact set.

**Sources and imports.** The Overglass group—Dewglass wall, Hanging Frames and Felt loft—is attached to the surrounding catchment as a proposed regional relationship. Glassfoot, mantis, Ashveil and Sandfelt sources retain separate populations and life histories. Rooted drive tissue, reserve larvae and appropriate hosts arrive through named supply routes; attractive packaging does not make them local captures.

**Race tradition.** Legible narrow/wide line choices and broad overtaking opportunities reward responsive anatomy, while a longer clean run keeps outright pace desirable. Regional Flyer courses use true height and sweep rather than translating every rail into an invisible air wall. A Horn Tax-style build can win here if the actual race permits it; the broker culture grants no foot-release multiplier.

**Accessible project.** Glassline includes contact and useful corrective sensing only where the host has response anatomy. A signature finish is a separate purchase or craft. The same measured ordinary sect may appear in a plain tin and an illustrated local edition without secretly changing its performance.

**Economic opportunity.** Rematching, receiving inspection, appropriate consignment, finishing, documented restoration and exact requested stock are useful services. A visibly rare animal is worth what a real customer values; no city-wide rarity price is asserted.

**Faction relationship and tension.** The Lantern Exchange faction coordinates market-court agreements and road receiving support. A dispute official cannot adjudicate their own shop’s delivery claim or override a race result because a major exhibitor lost. Merchant influence is a source of institutional tension, not a scripted corruption fee every customer must pay.

**Objects.** A repair card separates original wing panels from replacement roots; a counter display separates a natural pattern from applied finish. This accuracy makes products desirable beyond the seller’s acquaintances and gives honest specialists a reason to exist.

### Spanrest — the circuit that arrives in cases

`W19.REGION.SPANREST`

**New settlement proposal.** Spanrest is a causeway junction with permanent repair households and seasonal crews. Its work is road-surface repair, carrier staging, food provision, reusable packaging and section hardware. Mobile residence is a normal local arrangement, not evidence that somebody lacks the right to participate.

**Local hobby.** The proposed Foldout Club travels with numbered section cases and meets at a fixed receiving yard when crews return. A race can be hosted in a borrowed covered space after the route is properly built; it need not await a stadium. Flat sealed lane panels, supported portals and independently owned kits retain their identities when packed.

**Sources and imports.** Timber-seam and Slab-edge Rivetbacks are associated with maintained verge structures; darkling and mole-cricket source pockets occur where their resources exist. This is an additional locality proposal, not relocation of earlier individuals. Predatory insect colonies need food, and a repaired road is not itself a diet. Delicate wing arrays and useful-stage juveniles are normally received through supported services.

**Race tradition.** A travelling layout changes in published, reproducible ways between meetings. Competitors compare familiar pieces in a new order. An efficient re-fit, sound joint and recoverable landing have value alongside powerful drive. A course builder must provide coherent connectors and real recovery access; unpredictability is not achieved by hiding a broken connector under rust.

**Accessible project.** Tin Can Special, Apron conversions and Right Again are understandable travel projects. A complete Right Again arrangement still requires the host-specific support that lets it work; a recovery hinge is not mandatory insurance sold with every case.

**Economic opportunity.** Packing repair, section lending, route setup, customer-stock adaptation and root-safe transport can support a keep. The Case Runners contract with these crews without becoming identical to the Traverse Freeholds faction. A returned loan is the original section or part, not a newly generated replacement.

**Faction relationship and tension.** The Traverse Freeholds recognize both fixed settlements and registered itinerant households. Their sporting office must accept a supported contact address at a club or carrier yard. The problem of scarce shipping capacity is solved through bookings and support, not a requirement to own land or undertake unrelated courier quests.

**Objects.** Fold marks, paired carrying diagrams, return tags and a repaired timing mast make travel expertise legible. A scuffed kit tin can retain honest history without becoming a higher stat tier.

### New World City — destination, not the only place that matters

`W19.REGION.CITY`

**Author-established destination; new civic detail proposed.** The city hosts the What’s Left of the World Derby. In this working atlas it is a trading and repair metropolis governed locally, not the unquestioned ruler of all factions. It depends on incoming food, living-stock support, specialist production and transport. Hosting the sport does not explain the entire post-winter recovery.

**Two kinds of arrival.** Qualified competitors arrive to represent a faction in their declared discipline. Makers, collectors, workers, spectators and ordinary visiting racers may arrive for the market, displays and public clubs without a Derby berth. Access to the city and access to its championship grid are not the same unlock.

**Local identity.** The spectacle magnifies a bench-scale hobby: named racers travel in supported cases; technicians work at practical benches; crowds see enlarged views and clear course markers. Great public halls coexist with neighborhood hobby rooms. The championship is extraordinary without turning every insect into a rideable war beast.

**Everyday supply.** The city has many customers and higher aggregate demand, but not infinite purses or universal premium prices. A plain prepared part can sell through a routine shop order. A commemorative lid can appeal to a collector without restricting the performance component to a single final-day purchase window.

**Sporting standing.** City-based competitors affiliate through one of the signatory clubs and follow the same ladder. The host municipality has no purchased wildcard or automatically crowned home champion. The title belongs to the valid winner of the final, not to the faction with the largest visiting delegation or most kills.

**Persistent meaning.** Returning from the Derby changes the actual records, possessions and relationships affected. It does not empty the player’s stable, reset their breeding knowledge or make hometown racing obsolete. A world-qualified lanista may still enjoy developing a new ordinary kit alongside a local beginner.

**Institutional areas.** The Receiving Apron, Common Bench Hall, Ground Bowl, Sky Galleries, Counter Ring, Display Walk and Finish House perform separate jobs described later. Their geography remains schematic until an architectural scale plan is approved.

## 3. Four proposed civic factions

<a id="w19-faction-rivet"></a>

### The Rivet Commons

`W19.FACTION.RIVET`

**Civic foundation.** A compact of pumpwright wards, heat-recovery workshops and trackside households. Tinwater and Cinderhouse are proposed member settlements. Local assemblies manage their own work; elected delegates maintain the shared repairs and travel commitments. This is not the Cinderhouse Broodworks renamed as a state.

**Why the sport matters.** A working construction demonstrates craft that residents recognize, while shared race halls give different occupations a public activity. The Commons values a locally built racer but cannot certify quality from a cooperative stamp. Private builders and imported components remain ordinary parts of the scene.

**Representative institution.** The Rivet Assembly Board organizes the factional final and appoints event staff from clubs that are not involved in the decision under review. Its mandate is sporting administration and funded team support, not ownership of members’ bodies or breeding lines. Selection follows the published race results; an impressive workshop inspection cannot replace the winning race.

**Support package.** A chosen representative receives a contracted bench allowance, replacement shift support where needed, booked outward/return carriage and a bounded stock-maintenance allowance. Goods and services have actual suppliers. No cash or spare anatomy is generated merely by wearing the badge. Personal sponsor additions are recorded separately.

**Construction culture.** Visible ribs, local repair marks, legible root access and reusable tins. Loaded and rough-course expertise is well represented, but the Commons also fields fast narrow racers and complete Flyers. A resident’s choice of natural color or finish is not a political statement unless they make it one.

**Selection occasion.** The Common Bench Final uses a changing multi-layout race programme. Spectators can compare the same host before and after a permitted refit. The demonstration benches beside it are not scoring stations. The winner earns the seat through valid racing.

**Internal pressure.** Workshop time devoted to elite preparation competes with communal maintenance. The public budget separates required repairs, local hobby access and representative support. This creates commissions and debates without an obligatory sabotage storyline or a hidden faction tax on all stock.

**Relations.** Reedwater supplies biological grow-out; Lantern brokers difficult components; Traverse crews provide transport and setup. The Commons exports useful support preparations and hardware. Members can cooperate on a product and compete fiercely on the track.

**Emblem and objects.** Three offset rib shapes joined by a small circular fastener mark. Printed on cases and banners, not burned into specimens as an ownership requirement. The most characteristic object is an annotated working kit guide beside its still-owned reference racer.

### The Reedwater Compact

`W19.FACTION.REED`

**Civic foundation.** A federation of basin tenders, growers, emergence operators and adjoining households. Crown Basin is its reference settlement. Catchment councils coordinate scarce usable water, working perches and supply routes; private enclosures and independent Wrangloids coexist with shared facilities.

**Why the sport matters.** The full life of a component is visible here: a juvenile, an intact adult, a fitted array, then an aerial racer. The sport makes careful raising and skillful construction public achievements. It also supports ordinary ground racers who buy the region’s timing or reserve stock.

**Representative institution.** The Basin Race Council runs Crown Trials and keeps public course and result records. Its ecological/maintenance role is distinct from its sporting role. It may restrict access to an occupied working enclosure or announce a habitat closure for a real stated cause; it cannot grant exclusive permission to know, own or breed a species. Equivalent lawful acquisition and paid grow-out remain possible elsewhere.

**Support package.** The representative’s existing committed brood can be boarded with named capacity and endpoints; the faction does not require the representative to donate it. Array transport, arrival matching and a declared reserve-stock allowance are booked through actual operators. Care includes the return journey and an agreed contingency, not merely departure.

**Construction culture.** Exposed wing-sweep diagrams, clear stage labels, natural veining and shaped supports. Quadwing and Longwing both appear, with ground-foundation kits beside them. Published control and duty differences matter more than calling every long wing an endurance upgrade.

**Selection occasion.** Crown Trials race entrants through legal route choices and repeated usable maneuvers. An easy-to-observe court or mating display is not an examination for a racing berth. The final winner, not the keeper of the most expensive nursery, represents the faction.

**Internal pressure.** A headline meeting can consume the same space required by uninterruptible emergence. The council must book visiting arrival space rather than evict late-stage stock. Specialists can earn through extra capacity and matching; the default novice package still arrives ready for ordinary assembly.

**Relations.** The Commons supplies root support, Traverse carries stage-sensitive stock, and Lantern supplies specialist buyers. Reedwater’s dependence on imported structural and prepared-drive stock prevents the faction from becoming a self-sufficient 'water plus flight' bonus package.

**Emblem and objects.** An open ring crossed by a single curved reed. Gates carry the ring as a venue identifier, not as a player-only boost. The characteristic object is an emergence reservation board that leads to a physical array case, with a separate public roster for the race.

### The Lantern Exchange

`W19.FACTION.LANTERN`

**Civic foundation.** A charter association of market wards, packers, artisans and trade-road settlements. Saltglass Exchange is its reference center. Membership is not restricted to merchants; repair workers, caregivers and customers have civic standing. The Saltglass Counter remains one private business among several.

**Why the sport matters.** A recognizable racer moves knowledge and demand between communities. The faction sponsors both competitive events and exhibitions because these reveal different forms of craft. An excellent public result can prompt an inquiry, while a beautiful display can be valuable without winning a race.

**Representative institution.** The Lantern Race Exchange publishes selection conditions and separates judges from commercial interests in the affected entrants. Donor assays and delivery evidence are useful, but the factional berth follows race results. A buyer cannot purchase the berth by buying the winning host or paying a judge’s employer.

**Support package.** Funded receiving, approved fitting work, shipment space and return care can accompany a representative. Sponsors may request public maker or supplier credit; they cannot demand a hidden equipment restriction contrary to the entered Open class. A representative may decline the commercial offer while retaining their already-earned sporting qualification and seek another legitimate support route.

**Construction culture.** Clear windows, labeled parts, restrained printed livery and exact edition names. Complex and simple kits coexist. A commemorative product can use ordinary excellent stock; a plain crate can contain a valuable specimen. Honest substitutes and well-described custom fit are part of the local craft identity.

**Selection occasion.** The Lantern Exchange Final takes place beside a separately judged display salon. Neither score carries into the other. The race programme tests actual contact, approach and air/ground route behavior. Judges cannot award racing positions for an elegant tin or a popular publication.

**Internal pressure.** Large suppliers want guaranteed exposure; independent makers want fair access. A rotating exhibitor roster and separately allocated public bench spaces address capacity. Exceptional attention never becomes an automatic genome buff, unlimited order or universal price multiplier.

**Relations.** The Exchange imports from all three other factions and sells many of their products onward. Its disputes with them concern practical promises and transport responsibility, not an assumption that outsiders’ components are prohibited. Shared measurements reduce the cost of comparing unfamiliar stock.

**Emblem and objects.** A shaded lamp outline above two opposing case handles. The factional sash is detachable; a natural shell pattern does not become counterfeit livery. The characteristic object is a paired product card: the construction’s actual contents on one face, its honestly supported provenance on the other.

### The Traverse Freeholds

`W19.FACTION.TRAVERSE`

**Civic foundation.** A federation of fixed roadside households, mobile repair crews and settlement-to-settlement support yards. Spanrest is its reference receiving node. The political compact maintains passage infrastructure and negotiated stopping rights. It does not claim every caravan or the independent Case Runners as members.

**Why the sport matters.** A few well-packed section cases can bring a real meeting to a place with no permanent track. Showing up with a working racer and sharing a course is a recognizable civic act. Travelling builders develop distinctive constructions because preparation, repeat fitting and preservation matter, not because distance grants a handling stat.

**Representative institution.** The Traverse Circuit Office records mobile club contacts, recognized results and the moving final’s published location. A competitor can use a club or receiving yard as a contact point. No land deed, permanent address, employer sponsorship or carrier career is required for sporting affiliation.

**Support package.** Reserved case space, setup labor, a shared tool allowance and return carriage are real commitments. A selected entrant need not carry unrelated freight as a compulsory quest to redeem their ticket. Extra voluntary carrier work remains a separate livelihood with its own quote.

**Construction culture.** Repairable cases, retained native anatomy where useful, sectional supports, visible return paths and easily inspected working clearances. A quick lightweight kit can travel well; a substantial Open monster can also be carried with suitable support. 'Portable' is not a universal small-part whitelist.

**Selection occasion.** The Moving Final announces its layout, rules and venue before entrants commit. Changing the location after registration requires a practical rebooking or renewed qualifier opportunity; it does not secretly reward people who were near an unannounced stop. In-world travel creates decisions, while real-world absence remains paused by default.

**Internal pressure.** Road work and race traffic compete for the same crews and cases. The office contracts enough capacity for earned delegates before promising free journeys. Delays are handled through actual rerouting or timetable revision, not a teleporting case or a blank new funding pool.

**Relations.** The Freeholds sell useful carriage and construction access to the other factions. They buy stage-specific support and workshop work in return. Their champions can use imported premium anatomy and remain recognizably Traverse through the preparation, record and community that supported them.

**Emblem and objects.** Two staggered chevrons passing through an open square. Numbered case corners and portable course diagrams give the mark a material context. The characteristic trophy travels in the original repaired transit case; later copies are labeled reproductions.

## 4. The sporting charter

<a id="w19-charter-compact"></a>

### The Derby Compact: a limited sporting agreement

`W19.CHARTER.COMPACT`

The four described factions and New World City’s host administration recognize a shared finish, a way to qualify, ordinary inspection, record exchange and responsibilities for advertised support. This proposed **Derby Compact** governs the programme it sanctions. It is not a peace treaty, universal licensing authority, global bank or explanation of every faction’s laws.

The **Circuit Registry** records which events confer advancement and whether an entrant has earned it. The **Stewards’ Table** hears sporting disputes under published procedures. The **Host Works Office** contracts the city’s physical venue, receiving capacity and event labor. No one office simultaneously sells the entrants their parts, selects a favorite winner and reviews its own complaint.

Ordinary informal races and shops can exist outside Compact sanction. They produce real local outcomes and enjoyment. They cannot advertise a Derby berth unless that consequence is actually recognized. The point of recognition is shared trust in a specific result, not permission to run a roach in a gutter.

A source record, product qualification, maker advertisement and race result remain different claims. Race officials inspect capability and the actual fitted entry. They do not demand research-grade ancestry for every organ, and no manufacturer owns the assimilation interface.

The Compact is intentionally narrow enough to let disagreeing societies compete. It protects a common start and finish while leaving who breeds, what people collect, and which local styles they enjoy open.

<a id="w19-charter-entry"></a>

### Four records that must not be merged

`W19.CHARTER.ENTRY`

**Faction delegation.** A registered lanista’s earned Factional result supplies a specific faction seat for one discipline and edition. Support people, loaned stock and sponsors can be named without becoming competing duplicate entries.

Within a meeting the entered host remains fixed unless a separately registered reserve rule explicitly permits replacement between heats. No mid-heat host exchange occurs. The standard championship templates below use one fixed host per meeting; a withdrawn animal is not replaced invisibly. ### Shared race grammar with explicit experimental parameters

`W19.CHARTER.FORMAT`

The intended flagship competition uses simultaneous entrants with actual contact and passing space. Historical paired-heat software is not evidence that this exists as runtime. The following **M4 format** is a proposed four-entry content template, not a claim copied from PCR.

**Single final.** One valid race decides its winner. Published preliminary heats reduce a larger roster to four; each heat’s advancing places are stated before it begins. A final requires at least two actual starters and a valid winner. A field reduced below that records no contest and uses the announced rerun or postponement procedure.

**Four-race series.** The same four hosts race two declared layouts twice each, with an ordinary service/refit window between races. Valid placing earns 5, 3, 2 and 1 points; a nonfinish earns zero. Equal valid finishes split the occupied points. This authored schedule rewards race results, not attacks, sales, rarity or time spent in the nursery.

**Series order.** Points, then more race wins, then more valid finishes. A tie affecting the sole qualifying place receives an additional race on the announced tie-break layout after the same service allowance. Other unresolved places may be shared under published prize terms. A qualifying series winner must have at least three valid finishes; otherwise the qualifier is inconclusive and is rescheduled rather than awarding advancement by administrative survival alone.

**Starting fairness.** For the four-race template, the four starting positions rotate so every entrant starts in each position once; the two layouts are used in a publicly specified alternating order. For a single qualifying final, all entrants receive the same advertised familiarization access and the start positions are allocated by a witnessed draw. A different seeding method must be declared before registration, with the comparable inputs it uses; organizer preference is not seeding. The layout must be reviewed for remaining advantage; rotation is not proof that every geometrical bias has vanished. Wind or surface changes may require regrouping or restarting a genuinely affected heat under the same rule for all entrants.

**Tied single final.** If the available timing and finish evidence cannot separate entrants competing for one berth or title, the tied entrants run an additional race after the same declared care and fitting allowance. Already-separated places remain recorded. This resolves the same edition and does not issue another purse. If no valid finish occurs, the unresolved final is rerun or rescheduled; it is not awarded from attack count or stock value. A sole valid finisher in a race that began with at least two actual eligible starters wins by completing the course, not by merely remaining alive.

**No imported retirement law.** Self-righting, legal hops and declared gate retries follow actual RRR capability and course rules. Tamiya’s immediate retirement rules are not copied merely because the source has them. Receiving after the finish and scored course completion remain distinct.

## 8. Support and recurring demand

<a id="w19-support-cycle"></a>

### The festival creates demand without manufacturing scarcity

`W19.SUPPORT.CYCLE`

**Course publication.** Clubs, fitters and suppliers see what types of running, contact and air movement will actually be useful. No course is altered secretly to sell one supplier’s exclusive counter.

**Preparation period.** Existing lines can supply ordinary measurements; specialists offer a missing contribution or adaptation. Price pressure requires a real buyer, quantity and supply cause. A new poster does not instantly multiply every ask in every town.

**Qualification and travel.** The event pays its agreed prizes and the representative programme books the promised support. Spare-stock loans state actual return obligations. No qualifier badge buys unrecorded extra insects.

**The festival.** Display, racing, kit building, source trading and ordinary social attendance coexist. Most transactions can remain aggregated at a stated market boundary; important recurring customers preserve individual products and histories.

**Return.** A racer, maker, breeder or collector brings back owned objects, records, observations and actual paid or offered work. A published win may prompt a request; it does not certify a whole line or create an already-settled sale. Back home, ordinary clubs continue.

**Another season.** New legitimate events, layouts and work renew interest. Rare appearance stock can be desirable, but its availability is not tied to real-world login streaks. Historical editions remain historical; a revival identifies what was newly produced.

**Related reference:** [W19.S04](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s04) · [W19.S10](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s10)

<a id="w19-support-meet"></a>

### A meeting budget must buy the meeting

`W19.SUPPORT.MEET`

An event offer separates host work, participant costs, prizes and travel support. The host quotes section use, setup, timing, receiving, ordinary care, venue use and staffing. The purse is a separate funded commitment. A promised travel floor is another commitment, not an amount the winner must extract from the prize after the event.

Sponsorship, exhibitors, participant fees, public support and ticket income are possible sources when actually authored. Expected crowd size is not cash already available. Each promised service has a responsible provider or funded contingency before confirmation. A small supported club meet can be legitimate without commercial ticket revenue.

Setup crews, fitters and nursery services can earn around the meeting, but participants are not compelled into those jobs. A breeder who dislikes racing can supply accepted ordinary stock; a racer who dislikes breeding can buy it. Prize money is not the sole source of income in the world.

No price table is added here merely to make this sector appear profitable. W16/W17 budget examples remain conditional and unchanged. Later matched comparisons must include care during travel, unsold inventory, hired labor, actual event renewal and the source of customer funds. An unpaid advertisement or a displayed trophy cannot settle those bills.

**Related reference:** [W19.S08](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s08)

## 9. Three craft roles

<a id="w19-person-len"></a>

### Len Voss — a travelling joint fitter

`W19.PERSON.LEN`

A new Spanrest craftsperson who prepares and rematches existing joint stock. Len rents Foldout Club bench time, buys or receives particular linings, and returns customers’ hosts and retained components after agreed work. A customer job and the fitter’s own specimen collection are separate inventories.

Len’s expertise concerns working travel and contamination under known conditions. It does not reveal wild genomes, guarantee every mixed-source pair, or confer authority over regional inspection. The personal product line, Bridgejoint, records fit ranges and preparation revisions. Brood supply comes from cooperating keepers unless Len actually operates the required nursery.

This role supports ordinary paid work, remembered clients, testing advice and kit supply without requiring a championship career. It is not a new protected profession or a claim that every Traverse resident knows the same craft.

<a id="w19-person-eska"></a>

### Eska Ruun — a finisher with a living pattern collection

`W19.PERSON.ESKA`

A new Saltglass independent finisher who maintains a bounded Ashveil appearance project and accepts work on customer-owned racers. Eska records natural markings before applying finish, identifies any replaced wing roots, and refuses to certify speed from a pattern. The collection, sale stock and customers’ loaned display material remain distinct.

The Lantern Thread project seeks a recognizable veined/banded appearance. Its lineage record holds relevant observations, not a universal genetic certificate. Some descendants serve aerodynamic preparations, others living displays; destructive preparation is not performed on a supposedly retained untouched parent.

Eska can be an exhibitor, supplier or customer without becoming a racing judge for their own work. Public recognition can lead to a request, but it does not automatically mint money or the next rare specimen.

<a id="w19-person-sela"></a>

### Sela Orr — emergence and receiving specialist

`W19.PERSON.SELA`

A new Crown Basin operator who books actual juvenile space, emergence supports and array-receiving work. Sela’s service promises the agreed care and endpoint, not a perfect inherited wing. Parents, juveniles, soft adults and conditioned arrays have different records and responsibilities.

The operator can arrange a working replacement from another legitimate donor and report the newly matched array. Shared species or similar pattern does not establish that it is an original sibling. A damaged animal may remain eligible for another use; a failed intact-array delivery is not repaired by relabeling it.

Sela helps the Reedwater representative programme through a paid, bounded service contract. Being a council supplier does not grant priority to a favorite racer at inspection. The same service is available commercially to a novice who wants one complete Flyer tin.

## 10. Bounded edition and material histories

<a id="w19-history-frame"></a>

### Hobby-year dating and the meaning of an original

`W19.HISTORY.FRAME`

The following three histories are new fictional reference cases, not recovered past events or simulated sales. **H0** denotes the founding-rivalry era; **H30** denotes the current thirty-years-later setting at the resolution of this working timeline. These are hobby-year labels, not Earth dates, proof that the apocalypse was thirty years ago, or exact birthday records.

A lineage, an individual, an extracted core, a prepared component, a fitted build, a guide, and a printed tin can each have a history. Do not use a single 'original' flag for all of them. A maintained line can span several animal generations without requiring a founder to remain alive for the entire thirty-year history.

These case records are content authored for the bible. They do not add animals or transactions to a player save. When later instantiated, every living object needs the same custody, anatomy and condition treatment as other stock. Archived observations can remain after the source is no longer living.

<a id="w19-history-bridgejoint"></a>

### Bridgejoint: an ordinary service pattern becomes a recognized product

`W19.HISTORY.BRIDGEJOINT`

**H27 / working example.** Len Voss fits a customer’s existing Lowstep using separately identified lining material. The useful achievement is freely repeated travel within that customer’s geometry, not an improved ratio. The customer keeps the host; Len keeps a permitted preparation record. No racing win is invented to justify the product.

**H28 / first guide.** Bridgejoint 1 prints a specific supported motion range and explains whether sleeves are supplied separately or already included in the hip assembly. Its case is an open-sided motion frame, not a clamp that arrests the joint. Multiple real donors can satisfy the preparation.

**H30 / second printed pattern.** Bridgejoint 2 adds a second fitted geometry and a clearer grit-service interval. The number is a preparation/guide revision, not generation count or automatically superior genes. Edition 1 components remain useful where their geometry works; no global obsolescence is declared.

**Objects.** W19.HERITAGE.BJ_GUIDE1 is an archival annotated guide. W19.HERITAGE.BJ_CASE2 is a new edition’s product-case pattern. They are not living stock. A later manufactured set identifies its own sources and does not acquire the first customer’s anatomy or history by using the name.

**Demand.** Travelling fitters and ordinary kit makers can buy known dimensional suitability. A collector may separately want the marked guide. Any working or collector price requires the actual buyer’s purpose. No faction-exclusive stock or logo multiplier is introduced.

**Related reference:** [W19.S10](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s10)

<a id="w19-history-lanternthread"></a>

### Lantern Thread: a pattern line and two kinds of display

`W19.HISTORY.LANTERNTHREAD`

**H26 / observed appearance.** Eska’s project begins with two separately recorded Ashveil founders showing the desired vein-and-band relation. Their apparent symmetry is a field observation, not a guarantee of offspring or aero performance. Parentage uncertainty is retained where present.

**H28 / repeated selected display stock.** Recorded descendants across broods supply a bounded appearance claim. Some useful adults remain in the line. Specimens prepared into vanes are marked as such; retained parents are not simultaneously harvested. The first living display case presents the actual animal and its current stage.

**H30 / printed display edition.** Lantern Thread / Veil card offers either a living specimen or a clearly labeled prepared display surface. Those are separate listings with different care and provenance. A finished racing kit can combine selected patterned material with another measured component source, provided the supplied contents and functional promises are honest.

**Objects.** W19.HERITAGE.LT_LEDGER is the archived observation record. W19.HERITAGE.LT_VEIL_CARD is the contemporary edition pattern. Their existence does not prove the early founders are alive, create a line-wide speed trait or assign all similar natural patterns to Eska.

**Demand.** A collector seeks the natural design; a racer may seek a measured surface regardless of color; a finisher composes the selected material with the host’s actual silhouette. A display award belongs to the exhibited work, not automatically to every descendant from the line.

**Related reference:** [W19.S04](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s04) · [W19.S10](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s10)

<a id="w19-history-returnedhorn"></a>

### Returned Horn: original core, replaced working interface

`W19.HISTORY.RETURNEDHORN`

**H24 / original assembly.** A newly authored record identifies horn core RH-1 in a club demonstration build. Its role is a recorded construction example; it is not retroactively assigned a world victory. The donor and former host histories remain separate.

**H29 / material failure.** A receiving record finds the structural core sound but the working interface no longer serviceable. The core can remain an original object. A display does not need to conceal the loss of working readiness, and filling a reserve cannot restart a failed root.

**H30 / restoration.** An independent fitter supplies a new identified interface and performs the agreed support/fit check. The restored component retains RH-1 as its original core and records the new material separately. It can be used on a different eligible host without inheriting that host’s future races in advance.

**Objects.** W19.HERITAGE.RH_CORE1 is an actual historical-core concept; W19.HERITAGE.RH_RESTORE30 is the restoration record. A contemporary 'Returned Horn style' tin contains another newly sourced horn, not another original RH-1.

**Demand.** The restorer earns for useful work. A collector may pay for the supported history; a competitor may value the working geometry. Neither demand requires the two values to be equal. Ordinary examples with the same effective geometry are allowed to race just as well.

**Related reference:** [W19.S10](RRR_Wave_19_Research.md#w19-s10)

## 11. Explicit scope proposals

<a id="w19-scope-scale"></a>

### Preferred scale-study envelope — not adopted world measurements

`W19.SCOPE.SCALE`

**Decision status.** Absolute scale remains open author authority. This volume recommends a concrete comparison frame for later art/logic studies while leaving D02 unresolved in the preserved records. It is a proposal to test, not a statement that every previously named animal has these dimensions.

| Candidate | Proposed native host body length | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| S0 / very small | 30–50 mm | Dense home circuits and compact cases; anatomy and display may require more magnification. |
| S1 / preferred study frame | 70–100 mm | Recognizable roots and limbs at a bench, still hand-carried; broad deployed arrays require larger shaped cases. |
| S2 / large pet-scale | 200–300 mm | More costly housing, carrying and venue footprint; risks displacing the small-kit identity toward large animal handling. |

S1 is preferred because the kit can remain hand-built and personally carried while individual anatomical mechanisms can be recognized. It does not set a sporting size ceiling. Donor anatomy, additional limbs, horn projection and working wing sweep can extend beyond the body-length range. Those extensions need actual case and course clearance, not an invented universal miniaturization field.

A later dimensioned study must put the same host beside an adult hand, a tray, a complete tin, a home course, a crowded pack section and an aerial gate. The route must accommodate the intended field rather than simply multiplying body length by a decorative coefficient. Numbers chosen for a prototype board do not supply this evidence.

Until the study is adopted, W19 locations use relative working relationships and route adjacency. No travel fare, housing capacity, collider or lifespan is derived from S1. The final teams can retrieve the recommendation without mistaking it for settled canon.

<a id="w19-scope-recovery"></a>

### Two candidate directions for the post-winter account

`W19.SCOPE.RECOVERY`

The original premise gives roaches exceptional survival while the current hobby requires people, donor insects, food webs and materials. The account of how that world returned is still an author-level choice. W19 names alternatives instead of quietly copying an earlier vault story into history.

**R1 / continuous surface lineage, recommended for review.** Rad roaches were the only insects maintaining a continuous widespread active surface lineage through the full winter. Other life persisted in restricted refuges, dormant stages or maintained collections and re-expanded afterward. This is a proposed interpretation of the strong survival premise, not proof that the phrase always meant this. It explains why the roach is a universal adaptable host while donor diversity can be ecological and regional.

**R2 / deliberate restoration.** A narrower surviving roach biosphere is later enriched through protected stock and deliberate reintroduction. This can support dramatic recovery histories, but requires an independently authored restoration capability, actual founders and food-web support. It must not turn present-day access to ordinary insects into a mandatory faction research license.

**Shared requirements.** Present inhabitants need food, water, energy and care. Heat and scrap provide shelter or useful work, not calories. Predatory donors need prey; stage-dependent production needs its actual developmental inputs. The sports calendar is thirty years after the founders, not automatically thirty years after the bombs.

**No silent selection.** The regional livelihoods in this volume state what supports them now without deciding R1 or R2 as past truth. The preferred proposal remains clearly identified for author review. Broad death, aging and regeneration are not settled by either history, and material service does not grant unlimited copies of rare anatomy.

<a id="w19-scope-culture"></a>

### A regional difference has a mechanism

`W19.SCOPE.CULTURE`

A meaningful regional variant changes one or more of: source availability, field evidence, developmental services, accepted product purpose, preparation practice, course geometry, local relationships, visible craft or transport access. A name and color alone are not sufficient.

A meaningful faction difference changes ordinary organization, shared resources, representation, public priorities and the way support is provided. It is not a passive stat package. A faction should contain internal disagreement and several kinds of hobbyist without requiring a narrative chapter to justify every transaction.

A meaningful later championship changes opponents, commitments, course repertoire, audience and stakes. It must not quietly multiply an NPC’s drive, read the player’s hidden genes or remove supported counterplay. The same useful part still means the same anatomy at a local club and the world final.

The full course and tissue simulations remain downstream. This reference defines conditions a developer must represent and examples of alternative responses; it does not claim that every stated construction is already balanced or effective in an executable engine.

## 12. Physical objects and visual information

<a id="w19-object-passport"></a>

### Two-layer competition passport

`W19.OBJECT.PASSPORT`

**Physical form.** A durable host record sits beside a replaceable campaign entry sleeve. The animal’s name and image stay prominent; lanista, discipline, affiliation and edition are separate fields.

**Hobby use.** The old winner can be sold without selling the former owner’s berth. A different host can join the next meeting without rewriting the previous race.

<a id="w19-object-roster"></a>

### Delegation sash and roster board

`W19.OBJECT.ROSTER`

**Physical form.** A detachable faction sash is shown beside the named person and actual host; a separate line lists crew, loaned stock and the standby.

**Hobby use.** The visitor can see who represents whom, and which parts are borrowed, without implying all members of a species belong to that faction.

**Misreading to prevent.** One historical win cannot authorize several simultaneous delegations.

<a id="w19-object-roadcase"></a>

### Outbound-and-return racing case

`W19.OBJECT.ROADCASE`

**Physical form.** Supports for the actual packed state, a destination and receiving contact, an ordinary support allowance and a visible return booking.

**Hobby use.** Preparation accounts for both journeys; extra spare stock consumes real space. The case can remain a cherished object after the campaign.

**Misreading to prevent.** The outbound ticket is not a storage-life reset or a compulsory courier mission.

<a id="w19-object-coursecard"></a>

`W19.OBJECT.COURSECARD`

**Physical form.** An overhead/side layout schematic, piece identities, actual rough/loose/air sections, route choices, finish and retrieval boundaries.

**Hobby use.** Entrants can notice useful contexts for their existing components and see why another approach might work.

**Misreading to prevent.** A colored region cannot secretly activate an item or stand in for an unbuilt bridge.

<a id="w19-object-directory"></a>

### Club-service directory leaf

`W19.OBJECT.DIRECTORY`

**Physical form.** A clean listing distinguishes shop stock, benches, nursery stages, course type, public sessions and supported receiving.
