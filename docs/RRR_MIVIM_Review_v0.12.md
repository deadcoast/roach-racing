# MIVIM — Wave v0.12 contribution review

**Objective:** Turn a real personal construction into a repeatable living kit and useful breeding commissions while keeping component function, actual anatomy, evidence and the original racer intact.

**Comparator:** A recipe that clones the reference, family-name substitutions without useful measurements, cosmetic shop output, or a lineage name paid as a universal quality guarantee.

**Hard admission:** required quality at the declared scope AND novel-development adherence. No authorized numerical weights or grade bands are asserted.

| Criterion | Finding | Remaining evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | Model checks and actual controls exercise publication, alternate-stock assembly, physical kit custody, funded sales, living breeder delivery and recipient fitting. Rendered desktop/narrow captures reviewed. | Human usability, native platforms and completed-game quality remain open. |
| Novel-development adherence | Concrete integration of source-hobby kit functions with specimen-level selection, measured promises and biological production. | No claim of global uniqueness; supporting test count cannot replace the creative result. |
| Effectiveness | Actual winner → recipe → bred component → separate kit → funded payment → shop shelf → recipient draft/fitting/race is exercised. | Financial sustainability is not established; the first tested kit project loses 38 credits after the meet. |
| Creativity | The original racer stays intact while the maker reproduces its construction using different useful biological stock and explicit promises. | Player-perceived distinctiveness and pleasure remain unmeasured. |
| Scaling potential | Declarative profiles, existing shared inventory and atomic operations support further products without bespoke worlds. | One active local catalogue and finite buyers are not national-economy or multi-server evidence. |

## Corrections credited at their scope

Per-axis promises replace vague blanket equivalence; geometry is a target range; real source defaults become truthful reference data; historic opened-kit receipts stop owning animals forever; restored catalogues are synchronized; the sold kit appears at the existing shop; circular same-counter buying cannot exploit an already-posted higher commission; observed-line denominators remain explicit.

The maker runtime is reconstructed, not recovered from Wave10. Existing race, anatomy, bench, circuit and club operators remain byte-identical to Wave11. Their preservation does not demonstrate improved limb mechanics.

## Unclosed questions

Production affordability across matched careers, repeat independent customer behavior, material viability in transport, staged body condition, user-directed recipe revision/adaptation, articulated racing, multi-racer physical simultaneity, full championship progression and human engagement remain open. Successful tests are evidence of the tested operations, not proof of being addictive.
