THE BENCH

Select a sect in the tray. Drag it onto the host, or use Fit to draft. Move fitted roots directly, use the arrow controls, or focus a component and use keyboard arrows. Shift makes larger steps. Graft positions have visible supported ranges; clashes must be resolved before fitting.

THE BUILD

Draft changes are previews. Fit this build commits all changes together. Returning a sect to the tray uses a supported, reusable bench-graft fixture; it does not establish global painless removal or infinite tissue regrowth. Prepared native exchange units are retained in the bench cradle and reinstalled when a replacement is returned. This is a disclosed fixture, not free regrowth.

THE RACE

The browser calculates a new two-roach path-model race from your committed parts and settings. It is not a library of pre-rendered results. Open, Speedsters, and light-shell Fireworks apply to actual fitted weapons. Preload affects jumping only when a Hopper is fitted and its approach is enabled. Full limb physics, destructive damage, flight, and a coupled world clock are not implemented.

STOCK & SAVING

H-6 retains its Wave 3 B1-6 donor and measured inherited properties. UPGRADE-DRIVE is the previously purchased drive. Six further modules and workshop facilities are explicit starter endowments for this experiment. All available measurements are declared fixture observations. Local autosave may be restricted by your browser; export sheets as portable backups. Imports restore recipes against this stock, never create ownership or race wins.

KEYBOARD

Tab between controls. Arrow keys reposition a focused graft. Ctrl/Cmd Z undoes a draft step; Ctrl/Cmd Shift Z redoes it. Cosmetic finish does not affect performance. All values use normalized experimental units.
