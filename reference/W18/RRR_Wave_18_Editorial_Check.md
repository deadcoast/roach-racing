## Desk-review decisions

Longwing has differentiated forewings and hindwings rather than a vague pair of interchangeable panels. Root timing and leverage remain distinct. A source-paired set can fail and a mixed-source set can be valid when actually matched. A wing system requires work, supply, support and control. Relative air motion is not identical to ground speed. A missed gate does not become valid through reaching the finish early.

Kickback needs an available reaction/contact and room for the host to move. Native recovery remains possible. Boiler has contained material, pulse mechanics, outlet control and a separate replenishment operation. The Caustic supply model explicitly consumes a suitable developmental host; the ready-component buyer need not perform that rearing.

These are authored interpretation cases, not observed races or experiments. Source-paper figures, unseen methods and source-game numerical item tables were not represented as analyzed.
