## The receiving sequence

**Before dispatch:** name the actual product and intended use; state included roots, components, support and known condition. **In transit:** preserve its particular working requirements and record exceptional events without changing its heredity. **At arrival:** identify it, check its relevant present condition, perform only the agreed nondestructive work, and accept or identify a specific defect or mismatch. **Afterward:** perform the agreed service, authorize adaptation, replace actual material, negotiate a reduced-use purchase, or return the object with its care and custody still assigned.

The three histories stay separate: **whole-donor development; detached-component condition; and service/transport commitments**. A later molt of a donor or sibling does not age-transform detached anatomy. A delayed appointment does not reroll genes. The default offline pause remains; in-world travel advances its actual biological and service obligations.

**Mismatch, damage and recovery.** A missing tooth-like bearing surface, deformed lever or failed root blocks ready acceptance. Replacing a sleeve is new actual material; a new ratio needs a revised specification rather than a reset of the old label.

**Useful customer and alternate outlet.** Loaded start kits, known-ratio replacements and fit-to-size services; excessive mechanical advantage for one customer may still suit another.

<a id="service-paths"></a>

## Three useful service paths

<a id="w17-service-local"></a>

### Ready-to-bench counter handover

**Purpose.** A casual buyer obtains a current prepared component, ordinary supported case and a normal fitting/first-check allowance.

**Work sequence.** Inspect the quoted item → confirm receiving host/bench support → transfer → use included alignment/check → either accept the fit or identify a specific mismatch.

**Responsibility.** The shop owns current stock until the agreed handover, describes included contents and disclosed condition, and provides quoted initial support. The buyer supplies the declared host or books one of the existing supported benches.

**Failure and remedy.** A missing required interface or source defect is not a new charge for the buyer to discover. A buyer requesting a different host or new raw preparation receives a separate quote before work.

**Boundary.** No universal free remounting forever and no warranty of winning. This is ordinary first-use access, not a mandatory service purchase on every hobby action.

<a id="w17-service-shared"></a>

### Booked shared carrier case

**Purpose.** A regional hobbyist receives material through Case Runners with a known appointment and suitable receiving support.

**Work sequence.** Seller prepares and documents → receiver reserves support → carrier accepts specific custody → material travels under its profile → receiver checks identity and condition → agreed payment/remedy settles.

**Responsibility.** Sender, carrier and receiver have distinct duties. An intact seal can evidence handling history but does not prove working anatomy. Transporting stock does not give the carrier breeding or preparation rights.

**Failure and remedy.** If readiness or appointment slips, hold within real supported capacity, obtain authorized rerouting, return or agree another product. No spontaneous replacement, suppressed care or clock reset.

**Boundary.** Regional routes and currencies remain illustrative local services. No actual travel map or biological time limit is claimed complete.

<a id="w17-service-custom"></a>

### Special preparation and receiving commission

**Purpose.** An unusual raw graft, rematched pair, preserved historic core or stage-sensitive living source needs a defined additional process.

**Work sequence.** Name the valuable original material → agree risks and tests → reserve real replacement/support stock → perform authorized work → record changes → accept for the new stated use.

**Responsibility.** The fitter cannot replace a rare core and still label it wholly original. Customer-owned stock remains customer-owned unless explicitly transferred.

**Failure and remedy.** A nonrecoverable working function may leave an original display object or a narrower preparation, but only with owner agreement and an honest label.

**Boundary.** No real surgery, chemicals or clinical preservation recipe. Exact duration and risk parameters remain material-specific authoring/calibration.

## Six receiving decisions

<a id="w17-case-rematch"></a>

### One Catchclaw is slow on arrival

**Situation.** A delivered pair has one intact responsive arm and another whose supported closure fails the quoted range.

**Decision.** Keep dispatch and receiving measurements distinct. The source records say whether the arms originally shared a donor; neither common origin nor that label restores paired performance.

**Resolution.** A separately sourced replacement can be fitted and rematched with owner consent. The completed pair carries both donor records and a new pair observation. If no suitable replacement exists, service or return remains the actual outcome.

**Completed outcome.** An accepted working pair, an explicitly reduced loose-arm purchase, or a returned pair. No ghost second arm and no compulsory new brood.

<a id="w17-case-ratio"></a>

### Correct Lowstep, wrong customer course

**Situation.** The stated leverage and full fit are delivered correctly, but the customer is slower on a long smooth course.

**Decision.** Check the actual promise and the competing drive/contact/route inputs. The race loss does not establish faulty inheritance or shipping.

**Resolution.** Offer a separately agreed ratio/drive comparison or a different preparation. Do not refund a fulfilled performance specification as if it promised universal victory, and do not deny a real defect when the ratio itself is out of range.

**Completed outcome.** A useful comparison and chosen build; the original component retains its honest operating purpose.

<a id="w17-case-nap"></a>

### Feltbrake face is flattened in its case

**Situation.** The rigid stalk is intact but the working backing has been held compressed.

**Decision.** An unbroken outer box does not prove a ready pad. The receiving check distinguishes recoverable compression from permanent set.

**Resolution.** Perform only the supplied recovery procedure and retest. Permanent set requires backing replacement or revised product acceptance; lowering the pad is not evidence that its old material response has returned.

**Completed outcome.** Ready accepted brake, authorized re-preparation, or refusal of that use. Intake preserves the actual material rather than forcing it into a pass/fail rarity bin.

<a id="w17-case-core"></a>

### An original horn reaches the counter with an unready interface

**Situation.** A collector-builder purchased an identified historic core in a ready-to-fit assembly. The core survives but its living connection no longer meets the promise.

**Decision.** Record present working failure and original-material identity separately.

**Resolution.** Use an actual compatible new interface and a declared support check if the owner authorizes it. The restored object is described as original core with replaced interface, not wholly original or biologically regenerated.

**Completed outcome.** A working restoration or retained nonworking historical object under the accepted terms; no performance or provenance claim is fabricated.

<a id="w17-case-stage"></a>

### A developing donor changes stage before the appointment

**Situation.** A live larva or soft newly emerged adult was ordered for a particular receiving purpose; the actual journey or appointment no longer matches the quoted stage.

**Decision.** Check its species timeline and actual care, not a generic parcel expiry label. A prepared component already removed from another donor follows its material condition instead.

**Resolution.** Supported holding may preserve condition but cannot reverse development. Obtain consent for a different use, reschedule only if biology allows, or return/rehome under real capacity and care terms.

**Completed outcome.** An honest revised handover or closed order with living custody resolved. No arbitrary time reset or disappearance.

### Ashveil pattern remains, major vein does not

**Situation.** A marked adult or prepared vane has an intact attractive pattern but a damaged structural path.

**Decision.** Appearance promise, reproduction eligibility and working aero are assessed separately. No global quality bar decides all three.

**Resolution.** A display buyer may accept the object under a new purpose. A working buyer requires actual repair/replacement and evidence. A conversion cannot happen without owner approval merely because another customer would pay.

**Completed outcome.** One honestly described object with an actual destination; the same tissue is not sold to both customers.
