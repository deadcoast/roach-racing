# RRR — Wave v0.18
## Special Assemblies: Dispatch, Receiving & Ordinary Service

A ready product is defined by its actual contents and purpose. Source anatomy, current condition and fitting remain distinct. These are fictional practice definitions, not real biological shipping instructions or quantified survival guarantees. [Main volume](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md) · [Aerial handbook](RRR_Wave_18_Aerial_Handbook.md)

A normal complete kit includes appropriate initial support, intelligible handling and a usable first-use arrangement. The customer may buy that expertise rather than personally managing every donor, material and carrier. Observing, considering and comparing do not multiply charges; new physical work has a separately visible scope.

<a id="w18-receive-longwing"></a>

### Locust Longwing / complete array

`W18.RECEIVE.LONGWING`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.LONGWING](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-longwing)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.WING](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-wing) · [W16.MATERIAL.ACTIVE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-active) · [W16.MATERIAL.STRUCTURE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-structure)

**What is actually supplied.** Two forewings, two fan hindwings, named roots and included coupling elements. Separate drive/yoke/support components are listed as separate contents unless the printed complete assembly explicitly includes them.

**Dispatch condition.** Conditioned adult-derived assembly; each surface is identified by side and fore/hind role. The dispatch record states permitted rest position, whether its roots are ready living interfaces, and the observed deployment range.

**Carrying support.** A fan-contour case supports allowed root/vein points and preserves the actual folded geometry. No strap crushes a folded fan or loads its hinge to restrain the whole parcel.

**Receiving observation.** Inspect surface identity, intact useful veins, free permitted opening, living-root readiness and actual working sweep. Compare within the agreed motion and load range; a larger-looking span is not acceptance evidence.

**Included ordinary service.** Ordinary supported unpacking, orientation, reference deployment and the quoted first fitting allowance. A complete entry tin includes usable initial supply and a known first-use route.

**Changed decisions.** Wrong fore/hind piece, transport fold, lost root response or a curve incompatible with the agreed guide are separately reported. A correctly supplied array that the customer now wants on an unquoted body is an adaptation, not automatically a delivery defect.

**Recovery or remedy.** Correct recoverable support; replace an actual unsuitable element and rematch the array; agree a smaller passive or display use only from remaining material; or return it. Never restore working status by relabeling the surface or copying its former measurement.

### Dragonfly Quadwing / matched flight array

`W18.RECEIVE.QUADWING`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.QUADWING](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-quadwing)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.WING](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-wing) · [W16.MATERIAL.ACTIVE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-active)

**What is actually supplied.** Four individually situated wings, their specified roots and a recorded matching arrangement. An ordinary matched set need not be four unknown fragments claimed to share a source.

**Dispatch condition.** Expanded, conditioned surfaces and evaluated roots. The sheet identifies which observations concern the individual parts and which concern the matched array.

**Carrying support.** A four-root bridge clears membranes and preserves intended left/right and fore/hind identification. It never assumes dragonfly surfaces fold as a Longwing fan does.

**Receiving observation.** Inspect each surface, root response and the complete matched movement. Check the supplied arrangement at its named duty; one intact side does not certify the other three.

**Included ordinary service.** Identification, supported unpacking, ordinary alignment and the stated initial matched check. Advanced endurance or unusual phase trials belong to a separate quoted service.

**Changed decisions.** An unready recently emerged adult is not the conditioned array ordered. A same-source set can fail after damage; a mixed-source replacement can be useful after relevant matching.

**Recovery or remedy.** Maintain the young donor through its existing development if that was the agreed product; rematch an eligible replacement with provenance; accept a different use only by agreement. No new roots or wing membranes appear through a receipt.

<a id="w18-receive-flightdrive"></a>

### Flight-configured drive bundle

`W18.RECEIVE.FLIGHTDRIVE`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.FLIGHTDRIVE](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-flightdrive)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.ACTIVE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-active)

**What is actually supplied.** The particular drive bundle, actual output junction and supplied connection to the intended yoke. A ground-drive purchase is not automatically also a second dedicated wing drive.

**Dispatch condition.** Observed useful output and readiness under the declared flight duty, with required supply stated. Source identity and any re-preparation remain visible.

**Carrying support.** Supported buffered cradle for the active tissue, restrained through its carrying support rather than through the working attachment. Declared unsupported handling limits travel with the product.

**Receiving observation.** Confirm active response and available output through the supplied junction. Check that the intended supply and yoke are actually present before attributing weak complete-flight output to the donor.

**Included ordinary service.** Normal connection and ordinary commissioning within the quoted complete-kit session. No repeated fee for looking at an unchanged measurement.

**Changed decisions.** Separate inactive tissue, poor alignment, insufficient reserve delivery and an incompatible output path. These failures have different responsible work and remedies.

**Recovery or remedy.** Re-align a recoverable connection, supply a real replacement interface, return an out-of-spec drive or quote a different application. Dead drive tissue is not restarted by filling the reserve.

### Wingstroke Yoke / Crossphase pattern

`W18.RECEIVE.CROSSPHASE`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.CROSSPHASE](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-crossphase)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.ELASTIC](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-elastic) · [W16.MATERIAL.CONTACT](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-contact)

**What is actually supplied.** Actual matched coordinating junctions and adjustable root-link elements; the reference marks the surfaces served, adjustment range and whether a fixed or actively adjustable arrangement is supplied.

**Dispatch condition.** Supported unloaded geometry, free required travel and a declared timing relationship under the chosen test. No universally optimal phase label is used.

**Carrying support.** A marked bridge prevents paired elements from carrying shipping loads through their moving interfaces. Detached components retain each contributing source identity.

**Receiving observation.** Confirm travel, relative timing and unbound motion at the intended arrangement. Reaching the marked scale position is not proof of equivalent aerodynamic performance on a different array.

**Included ordinary service.** Reference orientation, alignment and ordinary fixed-setting setup. An active controller or altered motion range is included only when the invoice and physical assembly say so.

**Changed decisions.** A seized joint, wrong linkage or changed working phase is distinct from a correctly supplied fixed yoke lacking an unpurchased actuator.

**Recovery or remedy.** Service the actual junction, replace and rematch its physical element, or adapt the construction through a separately agreed procedure. A timing correction does not create power.

### Wingstroke Yoke / Return-link pattern

`W18.RECEIVE.RETURNLINK`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.RETURNLINK](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-returnlink)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.ELASTIC](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-elastic) · [W16.MATERIAL.STRUCTURE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-structure) · [W16.MATERIAL.CONTACT](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-contact)

**What is actually supplied.** Leverage and return-path structures for the identified Longwing array, including the source lining and root interfaces listed in the bill of material.

**Dispatch condition.** Measured permitted stroke, return clearance and linkage resistance in an unloaded reference. The intended fold sequence is printed.

**Carrying support.** Keep the supported linkage clear of the fan folds; do not stretch the return element to compress the shipment.

**Receiving observation.** Check opening, working travel and the entire return path without binding. A well-folded package can still contain a poor working linkage.

**Included ordinary service.** Supported unpacking and normal fixed-leverage adjustment to the printed compatible host/array. A different linkage ratio or body plan is a deliberate new construction.

**Changed decisions.** Lost travel, crushed lining and altered return geometry are not all cured by more drive force. The service identifies the restrictive element.

**Recovery or remedy.** Restore alignment, replace an independently sourced sleeve or quote changed geometry. Do not count an included lining again as a second free inventory part.

<a id="w18-receive-bridle"></a>

### Flight-root bridle / support arrangement

`W18.RECEIVE.BRIDLE`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.BRIDLE](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-bridle)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.STRUCTURE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-structure) · [W16.MATERIAL.ELASTIC](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-elastic)

**What is actually supplied.** A root-support pattern made from actual frame material and interface elements, with its allowed flex regions clearly identified.

**Dispatch condition.** Declared supporting directions, intact load paths and motion clearance. A photograph of the finished plate shape is not a complete support test.

**Carrying support.** Protect the support curve and active attachment separately. Do not suspend the whole load from the flexible portion intended to permit movement.

**Receiving observation.** Fit against the intended root and confirm that support does not block stroke, control or a separately fitted recovery hinge.

**Included ordinary service.** Ordinary comparison to the declared roots, support placement and clearances in a normal bench session.

**Changed decisions.** A static-strong support that binds a moving wing is not adequate working support. A different source plate may need real adaptation, not a hidden compatibility penalty.

**Recovery or remedy.** Reprepare an allowed segment, replace a physical support, or alter the chosen construction. Where the native thorax already satisfies the need, decline the unnecessary addition rather than invent a mandatory slot.

<a id="w18-receive-kickback"></a>

### Click-Beetle Kickback / Right Again preparation

`W18.RECEIVE.KICKBACK`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.KICKBACK](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-kickback)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.ELASTIC](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-elastic) · [W16.MATERIAL.ACTIVE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-active)

**What is actually supplied.** The coordinated hinge, latch, elastic region and stated loading/output interfaces. Included drive tissue is separate from a version powered by declared host anatomy.

**Dispatch condition.** Unloaded rest posture, functioning latch, known working range and supported root condition. A captured donor that clicks loudly is not the complete receiving test.

**Carrying support.** An unloaded hinge cradle supports the body-side roots and protects the latch without maintaining a shipping preload.

**Receiving observation.** Check the mechanism within a safe supported trial and inspect the intended host/contact/clearance before making a righting claim. A free snap is not proof that every toppled body can recover.

**Included ordinary service.** Ordinary installation review, unloaded response and the stated supported recovery demonstration. Destructive cycle testing is neither compulsory nor included by implication.

**Changed decisions.** A blocked host hinge, no external support, depleted drive supply and a damaged latch are different failures. Service cannot create the missing surface beneath an airborne racer.

**Recovery or remedy.** Correct the actual mounting or service a recoverable interface; replace physical damaged elements and record them; use native righting or another build when appropriate. Loss of working recovery does not erase a historic specimen’s display value.

### Bombardier Boiler / directional assembly

`W18.RECEIVE.BOILER`

**Assembly definition.** [W18.PREP.BOILER](RRR_Wave_18_Foundations.md#w18-prep-boiler)

**Material classes.** [W16.MATERIAL.GLAND](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-gland) · [W16.MATERIAL.ACTIVE](reference/W17/reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-active)

**What is actually supplied.** Identified storage and pulse structures, valves, controllable outlet, actual remaining contents and separately stated replenishment support. No real chemical preparation is described.

**Dispatch condition.** Inactive controlled outlet, sound containment, stated fill and permitted mounting range. Record fill separately from intrinsic capacity and chamber readiness.

**Carrying support.** An isolated supported gland case protects outlets and roots, separate from delicate membranes and exposed contact pads. Required service capability at the destination is declared before dispatch.

**Receiving observation.** Confirm identity, containment, control and the quoted inactive/service test. Live discharge, where part of an agreed demonstration, belongs only in an appropriate fictional receiving area; the buyer’s first use is not an unannounced safety experiment.

**Included ordinary service.** Ordinary orientation and control check, instructions for the supplied replenishment process, and a swept-clearance review when sold as an Airflare set.

**Changed decisions.** An empty sound component, an outlet that fails to close, unsuitable mounted direction, or a damaged living chamber are distinct states. A recharge indication cannot certify replacement contents.

**Recovery or remedy.** Refill through the actual provided supply, service an identified valve, remount, replace unsound anatomy, or reject working use. Do not require the ordinary racer to grow Bankseed stock to refill an existing sound Boiler; reproductive supply and replenishment are different businesses.

## Receiving cases

These are authored interpretation examples; no biological transit experiment or customer transaction has been run.

<a id="w18-arrival-wrongwing"></a>

### A Longwing set arrives with four intact surfaces but one forewing is replaced by an unrecorded fan hindwing.

`W18.ARRIVAL.WRONGWING`

**Required interpretation.** Count is not functional identity. Correct the contents or agree a newly engineered pattern; do not call the promised standard array complete.

<a id="w18-arrival-fresh"></a>

### A soft newly emerged Glasswing arrives for an order requiring conditioned ready anatomy.

`W18.ARRIVAL.FRESH`

**Required interpretation.** Retain the actual individual, support its stage, and resolve the product mismatch. Its future readiness does not retroactively make the shipment correct.

<a id="w18-arrival-rematch"></a>

### One wing root is damaged but an independently sourced replacement meets the relevant measurements.

`W18.ARRIVAL.REMATCH`

**Required interpretation.** Rematch the working array and retain all source identities. It may be a good functional product without being a same-donor original.

<a id="w18-arrival-hinge"></a>

### Kickback moves freely in the case but the fitted Boxbrace spans its required bending region.

`W18.ARRIVAL.HINGE`

**Required interpretation.** Correct the support pattern or choose native recovery. A static support rating does not waive a moving interference.

<a id="w18-arrival-contents"></a>

### The Boiler has recovered after a pulse but no material was added.

`W18.ARRIVAL.CONTENTS`

**Required interpretation.** Ready to cycle and sufficiently filled are different facts; the buyer cannot be promised another full charge without actual contents.

<a id="w18-arrival-hoststage"></a>

### Bankseed stock completes adulthood before it reaches a broodworks that ordered compatible pupae.

`W18.ARRIVAL.HOSTSTAGE`

**Required interpretation.** The stock may have reproductive value but cannot fulfill the original pupal requirement. Preserve actual custody, care and a separately agreed alternate use; no free replacement larval host is generated.

<a id="w18-arrival-complete"></a>

### A novice buys a complete Flyer kit and is then told the wings need an additional unlisted actuator to turn.

`W18.ARRIVAL.COMPLETE`

**Required interpretation.** The product was incomplete against its advertised first-use purpose. Supply the promised working system or revise/refund the offer; do not call hidden purchases advanced realism.

## Responsibility follows the actual agreement

A supplier is responsible for the promised product and its dispatch condition. A carrier undertakes specified support and handover, not universal biological immortality. The receiver observes the same material under the agreed conditions. Any fault attribution must follow actual evidence, not an automatic buyer-loss or seller-loss rule. Continued care, the location of stock, authorization for new work and agreed payment remain explicit while a discrepancy is resolved.

A correctly supplied Longwing that is poorly suited to a newly chosen tight course is not the same case as a bent root or wrong surface. A replacement, re-preparation or changed use requires actual material and agreement. General consumer law, insurance and regional contract institutions are not invented by this local practice sheet.
