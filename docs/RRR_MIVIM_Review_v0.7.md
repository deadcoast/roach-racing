# MIVIM — Wave v0.7 contribution review

**Novel objective:** the full core component-family translation becomes living, selectable, preparable and commercially useful stock on the same personalized racer.

**Comparator:** a broad catalogue whose donor traits do not change racing; shops that spawn generic loot; breeding unconnected to specific component requirements.

**Eligibility:** quality at declared scope AND novel-development adherence. Supporting tests cannot purchase either gate.

## Quality
Reviewed and tested at declared interactive/profile scope; whole-game and human usability gates open

## Novel Development Adherence
Concrete project-specific integration of useful anatomy, life-stage selection, alternate preparations, actual kits and causal racing; not a worldwide novelty claim

## Effectiveness
Demonstrated for bounded component, supply, interface and transaction paths

## Creativity
Authored biological execution and integrated commercial use developed; experiential distinctiveness unmeasured

## Scaling Potential
Declarative profiles reuse stock, rearing, preparation and bench operations; current small world and global active catalogue are not multi-world or world-scale proof

## Repairs
1. W06 stored cicada efficiency without using it. W07 compiles it into the same force/energy equations.
2. Frames, gears, stabilizers and independent suspension were not interchangeable current products. They now have donor/preparation/fit/race paths.
3. Native feet initially inherited a zero-release default, making ordinary tissue unrealistically unbeatable on that input. Native and legacy prepared foot baselines are explicit in W07, and selected stock is tested as a genuine full-circuit upgrade.
4. A donor-derived horn replacement initially bypassed the earlier yaw projection. The projection now applies to the measured reach.
5. Alternative preparations could otherwise duplicate a whole hind pair, mantis arms or larval fatbody. Region allocations enforce a physical choice; disjoint scarab and mole products remain allowed.
6. Live-stage expansion initially reused adult silhouettes in larval cards. The rendered cards and nursery now use stage-specific larval and pupal forms.
7. Long donor ids dominated product titles. The inspector separates recognizable product names from stable provenance metadata.
8. Two-column narrow shop cards compressed measurements. Narrow screens now use wider specimen and prepared-product cards.
9. The same shop could have funded an immediate unchanged retail-to-order buyback profit. Retail acquisition now exceeds that same-counter selected resale; preparation or real market differences remain legitimate value sources.
10. The inherited cumulative manifest described Wave 5 despite containing Wave 6. The new manifest explicitly records its current version and evidence; prior manifest remains in the byte-preserved baseline.

## Not established
Human enjoyment, professional final art, overall ecosystem balance, native-platform persistence and world-scale simulation remain unverified. A claim of being addictive is a design ambition, not a tested outcome.
