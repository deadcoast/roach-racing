# RRR — Wave v0.17

## Linked Livelihood Periods: Work, Stock and Cash

**All values are authored conditional design cases in illustrative credits.** Receipts below assume the named real delivery and buyer commitment occur. They are not measured prices, calibrated wages, probability forecasts, a simulation of the national economy or a fair comparison of player careers. Periods have different productive meanings. The earlier W12 loss remains historical.

Cash remuneration is transferred to a separate operator/household account once. The reported operating remainder is after that payment, before unmodeled tax, future capital replacement or costs beyond the stated horizon. Unsold animals and kits remain physical obligations, not sales. Capital is separately paid. Every base period can fund its stated cash outlays before receipts; there is no hidden overdraft. Supplied founder assets and quoted input availability are explicit assumptions, not earned income.

## Customer-stock fitter: three work blocks

**Accounting period.** A booked four-appointment work block. It is not a universal day count or an equal-time comparison with breeding.

**Starting stock and facilities.** Clients own every host and part. Tools and bench access are rented in the outlays. No breeder operation or biological inventory is endowed to this business.

**Customer and funding.** Four specific funded 24-credit appointments in each block; block 2 accepts only three under the stated remedy policy. No unaccepted fee becomes income.

**Capacity and commitments.** Four appointment positions per block; rework in block 2 consumes the fourth without a fifth job silently fitting into the same time.

**Opening cash.** 80 credits.

**New capital.** None; relevant rentals are cash operating costs.

**Cash after capital.** 80 credits.

## Ballast Club Tin: production, backlog and sale

**Accounting period.** A production-and-counter block; each completed tin includes a native host, Lowstep, Cleats and an assembly guide. All other required functions remain native.

**Starting stock and facilities.** No free host or sect output. Five new native hosts, five Lowsteps and five Cleat sets are bought at the declared scenario quotes across the three blocks. Facilities are rented.

**Customer and funding.** Foundry-associated customers fund two tins at 96 each in K1. Only one independent customer funds K2. In K3 two real buyers each fund one: one takes the identified retained K2 tin, one the new K3 tin. These budgets are explicit scenario inputs, not automatic respawns.

**Capacity and commitments.** Two simultaneous host/kit positions; K3 carries one existing kit and makes only one new kit. The unsold tin is cared for, not converted into a receipt or a free capacity slot.

**Opening cash.** 180 credits.

**New capital.** None; relevant rentals are cash operating costs.

**Accounting period.** Three consecutive biological work blocks spanning the specified two cohorts. Their lengths must cover the actual species schedule; they are not identical in duration to fitter or kit blocks.

**Starting stock and facilities.** A known compatible adult founder pair and initial identity records are disclosed supplied assets; their acquisition value is not called earned income. After the rack purchase, 150 credits remain in operating cash. Ongoing adult care is listed each period.

**Customer and funding.** Two Nets funds 4 ordinary mature animals at 12 each in B2 and at most 6 at 12 each in B3. A Cinderhouse selection customer separately reserves 76 for an eligible measured descendant pair supported by its requested evidence. Total possible receipts in the base case are 196; no other sale is assumed.

<a id="budget-boundary"></a>

## What these sheets establish—and do not establish

They establish internally consistent examples of an offer that pays for its stated work, a delayed sale that consumes working capital, a failed cohort that yields no promised stock, and a maintenance response that avoids accumulating more unwanted output. They do not establish expected value without outcome probabilities or the upstream productivity and market willingness needed to sustain the quotes. Later evaluation must include the same resource horizon, stock endowments, actual unused capacity, continuing care, capital and funded customers before comparing careers.
