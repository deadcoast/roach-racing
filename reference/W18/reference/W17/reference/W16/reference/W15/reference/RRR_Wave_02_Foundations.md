# RAD ROACH RACING — WAVE 02

## Living Parts, Selective Breeding and Earned Livelihoods

**Foundation version:** 0.2.0. **Status:** proposed, revisable rules. **Date:** 9 September 2026.

**Relationship:** extends Agent Context v0.1 and Handbook v0.2. Existing seed identities and source records are preserved. This wave does not replace the world with a new story.

**Scope:** targeted collecting; useful inherited traits; species/stage selection; breeding projects; enclosure construction; preparation; kits; temporary effects; sales; recurring production; league demand; failure and recovery.

**Evidence boundary:** source observations apply only to the cited source. Named RRR organisms, habitats, trait effects, care profiles and economics are proposed fiction. Numerical fixtures demonstrate isolated logic, not realistic biology, balance or retention.

**Production loop:** choose a use → target stock → catch/buy → inspect/sort → retain complementary parents → breed/rear → select → prepare/build or sell → finance a stronger racer, better stock or productive capacity.

## 2. Atomic rules

### Scope and translation contract

**W02.CONTRACT.01** — Every addition must specify an origin function, a player decision, a changed world state, an observable consequence and at least one downstream use.

**W02.CONTRACT.02** — Classify the origin as source-hobby translation, Mewgenics-inspired progression, or RRR-native biological/derby extension; do not fabricate 1:1 antecedents for breeding tanks or insect weapons.

**W02.CONTRACT.03** — Preserve speed, race victories, personally assembled kits, valuable collections, genuine upgrades and unrestricted hobby participation as simultaneous goals.

**W02.CONTRACT.04** — All new rules are versioned design proposals. Revisions identify the affected traits, products, facilities, recipes and tests rather than silently rewriting ancestry or race history.

**W02.CONTRACT.05** — No racing rank gates the existence of a donor, its genes, catching permission or reproductive competence.

**W02.CONTRACT.06** — Different career interests share one physical inventory. They are not exclusive skill classes.

**W02.CONTRACT.07** — Published measurement and pedigree confidence are separate from true internal state; a missing measurement is unknown, not zero.

**W02.CONTRACT.08** — No claim is made that Mewgenics succeeds solely because of breeding, or that RRR engagement is established by this document.

### Value, quality and specimen selection

**W02.VALUE.01** — Evaluate an insect independently as an immediate race donor, a breeder, a sale item, and a collectible; do not flatten those roles into one rarity number.

**W02.VALUE.02** — Heritable anatomy, developmental condition, temporary state, preparation quality and fitted geometry are different causes of performance.

**W02.VALUE.03** — A common animal can carry exceptional useful alleles; an uncommon form can be an ordinary racer; a rare specimen may be excellent in multiple dimensions.

**W02.VALUE.04** — Permit genuine Pareto improvements: a new sect may improve useful output without a compensating penalty. Keep meaningful search through further combinations, demand and frontier content, not forced drawbacks on every upgrade.

**W02.VALUE.05** — A damaged donor can retain reproductive value if its reproductive anatomy and condition allow breeding; do not assume every injury permits or prevents reproduction.

**W02.VALUE.06** — A visibly outstanding donor need not breed reliably. Fecundity, viability and offspring consistency are production traits distinct from racing traits.

**W02.VALUE.07** — A line sold as consistent needs demonstrated offspring results across multiple broods; pedigree is evidence, not a magical performance multiplier.

**W02.VALUE.08** — Sale prices depend on buyer purpose, acceptance criteria, available money and demand; grade and color do not dictate universal prices.

**W02.VALUE.09** — Hobby, Club, Circuit, Premier and Apex remain display bands from the prior proposal; classify using declared tests within a component family, not player level or an inherited rarity label.

### Targeted wildland collection

**W02.FIELD.01** — A field objective names a donor or trait, a use, a target life stage and a plausible search route before departure; general collecting remains allowed.

**W02.FIELD.02** — Habitat changes encounter composition and possible population trait distributions; it does not apply a free stat buff for being captured there.

**W02.FIELD.03** — For each fictional population, author both an ordinary distribution and an exceptional tail; do not reserve all interesting outcomes for new map tiers.

**W02.FIELD.04** — Tracks, calls, molts, feeding marks and repeated movement are evidence of presence or phenotype, never guaranteed proof of a hidden genotype.

**W02.FIELD.05** — Use route, time window, weather and search method to target encounters; exceptional capture is not a reward generated after generic combat.

**W02.FIELD.06** — Separate encountering, detecting, approaching, containing, and transporting a specimen. Skill or equipment can change a particular stage without upgrading its genome.

**W02.FIELD.07** — Capture tools declare which desired structures they are likely to preserve: rigid individual pods protect legs and wings; communal bulk boxes trade space efficiency against contact damage.

**W02.FIELD.08** — Transport consumes capacity and affects condition. Fragile stock, live predators and mixed stages need appropriate separation; capacities are physical units, not rarity weights.

**W02.FIELD.09** — Ordinary catches have declared common customers, kit uses or lawful feed-input channels; unmarketable or unsuitable specimens may have no profitable outlet.

**W02.FIELD.10** — A repeatable field route can pay through ordinary stock while exceptional finds supply improvement and larger gains. This is a balance target, not a guaranteed profit rule.

**W02.FIELD.11** — Population refresh is driven by the world clock, local cohorts and authored recovery; leaving and reentering a map is not a new instantaneous generation.

**W02.FIELD.12** — Capture scarcity can motivate alternative routes, trading and breeding; avoid mandatory real-time daily visits or disappearance because the player stopped playing.

**W02.FIELD.13** — Field runs settle retained animals, losses, consumed supplies, cash and observations separately; a journal entry is not ownership of a specimen.

**W02.FIELD.14** — Source location and old field records remain useful after discovery: later projects may require different stages, unrelated founders, or previously ignored traits.

### Return, sorting and stock disposition

**W02.SORT.01** — On return, place captures in holding and preserve identities; only then allocate each animal to keep, assay, pair, grow, prepare, sell, display, or release.

**W02.SORT.02** — Quick-sell evaluates the selected market and excludes locked breeders, protected founders, unresolved project matches and named collectibles by default; players may deliberately override.

**W02.SORT.03** — Unidentified stock can be sold as unidentified. A knowledgeable customer can value it differently; the game does not disclose unmeasured genotype solely through a precise sale-price oracle.

**W02.SORT.04** — A brood summary groups ordinary specimens but keeps individually valuable animals inspectable; batch actions preserve their distinct IDs.

**W02.SORT.05** — Repeated standardized assays reduce uncertainty; a single warmed-up, injured or recently fed performance is labeled with those conditions.

**W02.SORT.06** — Breeding and assay reservations are exclusive resource allocations. A specimen cannot simultaneously be shipped to a customer and used locally as a parent.

**W02.SORT.07** — An offspring chosen as a replacement breeder does not count as cash income; it becomes retained productive inventory.

**W02.SORT.08** — Selling a rare but redundant animal is a valid strategic choice; keeping every rare specimen is not compulsory progression.

### Inheritance and selective breeding

**W02.GENE.01** — Each donor form has a declared reproduction profile. Universal graft assimilation does not imply universal crossbreeding.

**W02.GENE.02** — For the initial fictional model, pair-breeding forms use a small diploid genome with quantitative loci, discrete anatomy loci and optional linked groups. Colony forms use an explicit colony-parent profile; their exact inheritance expansion is deferred.

**W02.GENE.03** — A quantitative trait derives from allele effects, possible dominance, interactions, development and condition. Do not use max(parent A, parent B) as the default inheritance rule.

**W02.GENE.04** — Installed donor grafts do not automatically enter the host germline in this working branch. Host breeding and donor breeding remain linked but distinct projects.

**W02.GENE.05** — Trait potential is sampled or inherited at creation; catching, renaming, selling and retesting do not reroll it.

**W02.GENE.06** — Only a declared new mutation can introduce an allele absent from both parents. Rare alleles can also enter a project through unrelated wild founders or trade.

**W02.GENE.07** — Discrete morphology can change a module action, not merely a stat: examples include a forked horn, a split spray outlet or an asymmetric-looking but functional vane.

**W02.GENE.08** — Fertility determines successful reproductive opportunity; clutch size determines eggs or offspring initiated; viability and rearing determine survivors. These are not the same number.

**W02.GENE.09** — A failed pairing consumes the specified time and supplies but does not automatically kill both parents or confiscate an unrelated rare gene.

**W02.GENE.10** — A production line is stabilized against named acceptance criteria, not against every possible trait; true-breeding target loci do not make all descendants identical.

**W02.GENE.11** — Relatedness is tracked. Inherited recessive defects and declared reproductive profiles determine consequences; a universal guaranteed bad-stat penalty for every related pair is not assumed.

**W02.GENE.12** — Line breeding and outcrossing are both available. The former can fix useful traits; the latter can introduce missing traits and reduce shared recessive risks while reopening variation.

**W02.GENE.13** — A breeding project reserves founder stock, tests offspring and records observed outcomes. It should permit meaningful partial successes rather than treating every non-perfect child as garbage.

**W02.GENE.14** — Measured averages and confidence belong in the player view; exact allele displays are an optional assay/progression design, not presumed beginner knowledge.

**W02.GENE.15** — Living breeders are consumable productive capacity through time, condition and eventual replacement. Absolute lifespan and permanent-loss policies remain in a separate decision branch.

### Facilities and production scheduling

**W02.TANK.01** — A breeding tank is a physical assembly of shell, climate control, substrate, partitions, feed/water delivery, reproductive surfaces, handling access and monitoring.

**W02.TANK.02** — A tank recipe defines a useful starting configuration, not a rigid species-exclusive slot; a custom enclosure satisfying the same needs can work.

**W02.TANK.03** — Distinguish adult holding, controlled mating, incubation, grow-out, isolation, performance assay and sect storage. A glass box does not perform all jobs automatically.

**W02.TANK.04** — Climate, density and nutrition affect declared development and viability parameters; they do not directly award inherited speed points.

**W02.TANK.05** — Partitions improve parentage control and protect incompatible occupants, sometimes reducing capacity. More equipment is not automatically more usable floor area.

**W02.TANK.06** — Grow-out capacity can be the bottleneck even when mating space is empty. Breeding forecasts include the future cohort, not just the present adults.

**W02.TANK.07** — Fertility support increases productive attempts or reduces environmental failure only as specified; it does not select the best allele at conception.

**W02.TANK.08** — Stage-control equipment slows or schedules development at a real resource or throughput cost; it does not suspend life indefinitely for free.

**W02.TANK.09** — Automatic mating plans execute only with mature, compatible, unreserved parents, supplies and a place for the forecast cohort; otherwise report the blocking condition.

**W02.TANK.10** — Automatic production stops starting new batches when safe capacity or feed reserve is insufficient. Already committed offspring remain visible obligations.

**W02.TANK.11** — Facilities continue during other in-world activities. Default offline behavior in this wave is paused world time; alternative capped offline advancement remains an explicit option.

**W02.TANK.12** — Fast-forwarding in-world time advances upkeep, development, orders and market replenishment together. Advancing only profits is forbidden.

**W02.TANK.13** — Care services and automation let a larger business reduce clicks. They consume labor or upkeep; advanced growth should increase strategic choices rather than repeated manual feeding.

**W02.TANK.14** — A warning is not an immunity: proceeding with incompatible conditions can waste a clutch, damage stock or cause escape according to the declared profile.

**W02.TANK.15** — Present basic readiness and forecast output first; expose detailed climate, density, pedigree and variance panels on demand.

### Harvest, preparation and assembly

**W02.PART.01** — A harvest plan lists the exact anatomy transferred and the changed donor state. Multiple products from one donor are allowed only where their physical sources do not overlap.

**W02.PART.02** — A complete Hopper set can include limb spars, springs and launch anatomy; installing it does not also grant free duplicate suspension and gear items.

**W02.PART.03** — Preparation can preserve quality, establish a mounting interface or alter geometry; it does not automatically increase the inherited potential of every processed part.

**W02.PART.04** — Raw grafting remains possible under the universal assimilation premise; prepared kits trade money and work for reliable fit, known geometry and convenience.

**W02.PART.05** — A prepared sect has a donor ID, anatomy inventory, condition, configuration range and storage requirements independent of its product name.

**W02.PART.06** — The assembly bench changes posture, leverage, clearance, mounting angle and placement. These settings are authored by the builder, not inherited by its offspring.

**W02.PART.07** — Keep the actual racer-building sequence visible and optional-depth: guided assembly for a kit; editable components and subassemblies for specialists.

**W02.PART.08** — Universal compatibility is biological; interference, unsupported load and blocked motion are physical problems to solve rather than hidden species bans.

**W02.PART.09** — Removal can make a sect available again only through the chosen removal/viability rules; no silent duplication or complete loss is presumed.

**W02.PART.10** — Native anatomy continues providing essential functions wherever it has not been replaced; starting roaches are functional animals.

**W02.PART.11** — A configuration is race-legal because of its actual capabilities and event rules, not because a seller renamed its attack module as a stabilizer.

**W02.PART.12** — Measurements compare the same load, energy state and surface where possible; the race remains the competitive outcome, not a substitute for a controlled component assay.

### Traits, setup and temporary effects

**W02.BUFF.01** — Every effect declares cause, target, operation, magnitude or formula reference, trigger, duration, resource draw, stacking family, removability, inheritance and event legality.

**W02.BUFF.02** — Use four separate categories: inherited anatomy; developmental quality; fitted setup; temporary state. Do not repeatedly count one property as all four.

**W02.BUFF.03** — Fuel loading changes usable reserve and carried mass; it is not an unexplained multiplicative speed buff.

**W02.BUFF.04** — Warm-up changes readiness and consumes reserve; overheating or fatigue follows only the declared physiological model.

**W02.BUFF.05** — Surface conditioning and grooming can restore or alter contact behavior; their effects are not copied into offspring.

**W02.BUFF.06** — An interaction bonus must identify a mechanism such as timing tolerance, energy delivery or joint fit. Matching donor species names alone is not sufficient.

**W02.BUFF.07** — Stacking resolves against shared resources: two high-draw organs share available delivery, rather than each receiving the full capacity independently.

**W02.BUFF.08** — One action may produce multiple consequences, but an energy store cannot be spent twice by simultaneous abilities.

**W02.BUFF.09** — Persistent experience is recorded separately from mutation. Heritable skill transfer requires a specific designed mechanism, not borrowing it from Mewgenics without translation.

**W02.BUFF.10** — No premium kit automatically receives an invisible bundle of buffs; show the parts, preparation or settings that supply its benefits.

### Commerce and sustainable income

**W02.MONEY.01** — Income routes include ordinary field supply, prepared sects, selected breeders, recurring brood contracts, custom kits, fitting, testing, boarding and race prizes.

**W02.MONEY.02** — A livelihood can cover food, housing/workshop costs and maintenance without championship wins; different routes still have distinct risk, labor and capital needs.

**W02.MONEY.03** — Recurring income requires useful stock, a productive facility, supplies, a production schedule and real customers. A tank is not a money generator disconnected from inventory.

**W02.MONEY.04** — Standing orders state accepted form, stage, traits, condition, quantity, price, delivery window, substitution rules and cancellation conditions.

**W02.MONEY.05** — Unsold stock is inventory, not revenue. Listed prices are not completed transactions.

**W02.MONEY.06** — Contracts and ordinary customers have funded demand and finite acceptance; saturation affects actual offers, not a hidden universal penalty on every player business.

**W02.MONEY.07** — Selling to distant demand can improve realized value while adding transport, delay and handling risk. Regions are market contexts, not mandatory tier locks.

**W02.MONEY.08** — Protect retained founders and reserve stock before automated sale. Every automated decision is inspectable and reversible before commitment where the operation permits.

**W02.MONEY.09** — A special breeding trait such as higher viable yield can make an ordinary race donor commercially exceptional; production expertise earns money in its own right.

**W02.MONEY.10** — Capital upgrades change throughput, output consistency, reachable donor profiles or labor needs. They do not automatically increase the market price of unchanged insects.

**W02.MONEY.11** — Race success can create attention, demand and sponsorship but is not proof that a donor line caused the result or that every offspring will win.

**W02.MONEY.12** — A broker can buy an ordinary intact insect, prepare multiple disjoint products and earn a margin; the model includes the original acquisition, supplies, time and sale capacity.

**W02.MONEY.13** — Repeat trading between identical buy/sell quotes cannot create money from nowhere. Profitable resale requires a real difference in place, timing, work, information or accepted stock.

**W02.MONEY.14** — Cash operating margin, retained inventory, replacement reserves and capital spending are separate ledger quantities.

**W02.MONEY.15** — The market need not punish every surplus with zero value; common-stock outlets should be designed deliberately and funded consistently with the economy.

### Leagues, builds and progression connections

**W02.RACE.01** — Open permits extreme equipment combinations without a universal point budget; course completion, locomotion division and event validity still apply.

**W02.RACE.02** — Speedsters excludes dedicated weapons but allows native contact. Useful body or movement changes are not forbidden merely because collisions can hurt.

**W02.RACE.03** — Fireworks requires functional minimum offense and light carapace. A heavy support structure that provides prohibited armor cannot evade inspection through labeling.

**W02.RACE.04** — Flyers race flyers; sustained flight and hopping are classified by declared capability tests, not donor species name alone.

**W02.RACE.05** — Competition tier does not dictate whether a gene can exist. Later tiers can feature stronger opponents, larger prizes and more demanding or varied race conditions.

**W02.RACE.06** — A kit-class event is an optional additional format; a beginner can also use an appropriate horn, hopper or flying kit in another legal event.

**W02.RACE.07** — Both genuine raw-speed upgrades and ingenious combinations can win races. Do not design every new part to be a compulsory correction for a scripted obstacle.

**W02.RACE.08** — The championship ladder remains Locals, Regionals, Factionals, and faction representation at New World City for the What’s Left of the World Derby.

**W02.RACE.09** — Rewards include money, useful stock, facilities, parts, access to customers, public recognition and qualifications; no event must award every reward type.

**W02.RACE.10** — Breeding and business can progress alongside racing. A profitable business can finance a stronger racer; the racer can advertise a useful breeding line.

**W02.RACE.11** — Trackmaker and hosting progression uses owned modular pieces, valid layouts and event participation; it does not require pretending that breeding tanks existed in PCR.

**W02.RACE.12** — Controlled course and component tests explain performance, while finish order determines the race. Winning remains an exciting achievement in its own right.

### Consequences, recovery and usability

**W02.RISK.01** — High-value decisions include retaining versus extracting a founder, specializing versus maintaining a second line, and accepting a deadline versus keeping enough rearing capacity.

**W02.RISK.02** — Failure can consume a capture opportunity, supplies, a brood, a contract payment or a race result. The cause must be attributable to a stated rule or disclosed uncertainty.

**W02.RISK.03** — Punishment does not require destroying unrelated systems at once; a failed order does not automatically erase the workshop and the champion.

**W02.RISK.04** — Do not guarantee every expedition, pairing or business produces profit. Preserve practical lower-capital ways to recover after an unsuccessful ambitious project.

**W02.RISK.05** — Protecting parents and raising backup stock is a strategic hedge, not mandatory busywork; production presets may manage it.

**W02.RISK.06** — The default wave branch adds no new global host permadeath rule. Donor extraction consequences are explicit; racer aging and permanent loss remain a separate decision.

**W02.RISK.07** — Never force the player to leave the game open or return on a real-world schedule to keep protected stock alive in the default clock model.

**W02.RISK.08** — Avoid obtaining engagement solely through repeated identical clicks, invisible probabilities or arbitrary new restrictions. Measure interesting choices and meaningful improvements in later playtests.

## 3. Trait-to-gameplay contract

A trait definition specifies the measured variable, its useful direction, genetic potential, development/condition effects and downstream operation. Field signs are preliminary evidence. Apparent body size, current activity and shop rarity are not substituted for measurement.

**Proposed phenotype model:** `expressed_trait = form_baseline + additive_allele_effects + declared_dominance + declared_interactions + development_effect + current_condition_effect`. Different traits use suitable units and bounds. The equation is an architecture, not a calibrated prediction.

**Race conversion:** usable drive depends on drive anatomy, delivery and load; delivered motion additionally depends on leverage, contact and timing. A better motor trait does not directly overwrite all race statistics. Better components may still be genuinely better.

**Commercial conversion:** `accepted_output = matured_stock meeting buyer specification`; `cash_receipts = accepted_delivered_units × actual_transaction_price`. Eggs, growing stock, advertisements and retained breeders are not cash receipts.

### Production traits independent of race performance

| Trait | Measurement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Successful mating frequency | successful reproductive opportunities per eligible pair-time | More productive attempts with the same mating space. |
| Viable brood yield | mature usable offspring per initial clutch | More saleable or selectable stock from the same initiated brood. |
| Development duration | world units to the target stage | Changes inventory turnover and delivery scheduling. |
| Target consistency | share meeting the project specification across recorded broods | Supports repeatable kits and standing orders. |

### Return sorting specification

| Disposition | Decision criterion | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Keep for use | Better or interesting anatomy for a current build | Reserve the whole insect or planned sect. |
| Keep for breeding | Complementary heritable traits, viable reproduction, suitable capacity | Reserve parents and project space; do not auto-harvest. |
| Assay | Uncertainty could materially change use or sale decision | Spend time/testing capacity; update evidence, not genome. |
| Grow | Desired anatomy occurs at a later stage | Commit care and future space; preserve a target-stage schedule. |
| Sell ordinary | Fits a buyer but not a higher-value retained purpose | Transfer actual ownership for a real payment. |

### Exact toy inheritance fixture — not a borrowed Mewgenics formula

**Assumptions:** two independent diploid codominant loci; no mutation, linkage or viability bias; counted offspring are viable. R contributes spring return; S contributes synchrony. Adult compatibility and successful reproduction are separate conditions.

`RRss × rrSS → all RrSs`

`RrSs × RrSs → P(RRSS) = 1/16 = 6.25% per viable offspring`

`P(at least one RRSS in n viable offspring) = 1 − (15/16)^n`

At 8 viable offspring the chance is about 40.33%; at 16 it is 64.39%; at 24 it is 78.75%. Use the machine-generated validation report for exact calculated values. These do not include failed mating, embryo/juvenile loss or sex/compatibility constraints.

**Interpretation:** F1 animals combine ancestry without automatically expressing both founder maxima. Later selection can combine target alleles. Non-target offspring may still be useful racers, alternative parents or saleable stock. Fixing two loci does not fix all traits or guarantee a race win.

**Relatedness:** the toy F1 cross illustrates segregation, not a recommendation that close-relative breeding is risk-free. Full inheritance must account for declared defects and reproduction profiles; unrelated equivalent parents give the same target-locus arithmetic.

### Batch scheduling contract

`reserve eligible parents → reserve future cohort space → commit supplies → attempt reproduction → incubate → rear → assay/select → deliver/retain → restore capacity`

Each transition records time, supplies, animals, occupancy, observations and money separately. A stalled transition reports its missing prerequisite. Existing young remain a responsibility when new reproduction is paused.

**Founder depletion:** harvesting every useful juvenile can leave no future adults. Retained replacement stock must be explicitly scheduled; there is no automatically regenerated parent at the next production cycle.

**Clock:** in-world progress continues during races, collecting, trading and permitted fast-forwarding; costs advance too. Default offline pause is a revisable branch, not a requirement to run the game continuously.

### Standing-order record

**Required fields:** buyer ID; available budget; accepted donor/form; stage; anatomical integrity; measured trait bounds; evidence required; quantity; actual price; due window; permitted substitutions; delivery responsibility; reserved inventory; payment state; cancellation and replacement terms.

**Production planning fields:** reproductive success estimate; mature viable yield distribution; target-pass distribution; development duration; future occupancy; supplied care; labor; committed outgoing orders; reserved founders; stock on hand.

**Risk:** do not multiply unrelated averages as if they were independent. A cold tank can simultaneously delay maturation, reduce viability and miss a contract deadline.

## 13. Acceptance criteria

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| W02.ACCEPT.TARGET | Player can name a trait, explain a racing or commercial use, choose a plausible habitat, and identify what observation would justify keeping the catch. |
| W02.ACCEPT.COMMON | An ordinary capture has at least one defined buyer or personal use; not every specimen is guaranteed a profitable sale. |
| W02.ACCEPT.BREEDER | An injured-but-reproductive or modest-performing specimen can still be correctly retained for useful inherited potential. |
| W02.ACCEPT.COMBINE | Complementary founders can produce a meaningful multigeneration project; perfect offspring are neither guaranteed nor locked behind player rank. |
| W02.ACCEPT.BETTER | A superior drive or contact part can be genuinely superior on relevant measurements without an obligatory compensating drawback. |
| W02.ACCEPT.TANK | Each enclosure upgrade changes an operational variable and names an affected cohort; no +speed from decorative furniture without a specified mechanism. |
| W02.ACCEPT.LIFE | Each brood and capture updates identity, anatomy, ownership and stage; preparation and sale cannot duplicate specimens. |
| W02.ACCEPT.SUPPLY | Standing orders only pay for accepted delivered stock; unsold output remains inventory and overhead continues. |
| W02.ACCEPT.RESERVE | Autosale preserves marked founders, reserved replacements and project matches; breeder retention is excluded from cash revenue. |
| W02.ACCEPT.PACE | Progressing time advances costs and obligations together with production; starting more broods than capacity supports is a visible decision. |
| W02.ACCEPT.ASSEMBLY | A kit requires selecting/placing its real components; buying or breeding does not bypass the build-it-yourself identity. |
| W02.ACCEPT.CLASS | Functional weapon and armor checks distinguish Open, Speedsters, Fireworks and Flyers without hidden rarity or species bans. |
| W02.ACCEPT.REPLAY | Rare capture genes and old race-result configurations do not change when retested or when a catalogue grade threshold is updated. |
| W02.ACCEPT.ACCESS | A shop-bought kit racer, a field catcher and a production breeder can each make progress without being forced into the other two activities. |
| W02.ACCEPT.QUALITY | Playtests must test enjoyment, meaningful loss, genuine improvement and livelihoods; passing data tests is not a substitute. |
