Native Windows/macOS persistence, physical touch and human playtesting remain unverified.

A technically completed kit sale does not establish profitable production: the first tested manufacturing project received 112 credits after 150 credits of post-meet payments. Broader economic calibration, retention, automatic customer simulation, aerial racing and full articulated physics are not certified by these tests.
