# RRR — Wave v0.16

## Interpretation Amendments

| ID | Earlier ambiguity | Replacement | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| W16.AMEND.01 | The earlier common A/B interface can be misread as the reproductive law of every donor. | A monograph names the biological unit: eligible adults and known mating history, a retained adult line that supplies useful juveniles, or a colony with reproductive and worker structure. Existing A/B experiments are retained only within their profiles. | Distinct supply businesses arise from distinct biology rather than renamed nursery timers. |
| W16.AMEND.02 | The animal presently housed beside a female could be assigned paternity for all later offspring. | Record mother, observed mating and verified paternal contribution separately. Unknown pre-capture mating remains unknown. Exact experimental founders can have explicitly known history; ordinary commerce need not certify every family. | Make pedigree useful without manufacturing certainty or requiring genetics paperwork for casual stock. |
| W16.AMEND.03 | A maturity flag could make soft newly emerged wings or plates immediately competition-ready. | Distinguish life stage, post-emergence conditioning, current integrity and the specific product test. A conditioned donor can still supply poor material; a promising unconditioned adult is not yet ready for every preparation. | Preserve useful emergence and handling expertise without adding a generic growth-to-tier ladder. |
| W16.AMEND.04 | A breeder could raise every form to adulthood before treating it as commercially useful. | Waxmoth larvae supply their named fatbody; Clockleaf juveniles supply the named coupling; retained adults produce later cohorts. The extracted part follows its material profile rather than the detached donor’s next stage. | Useful-stage collecting and replacement retention create purposeful decisions. |
| W16.AMEND.05 | A Nectar Crop donor could be treated as interchangeable with an adult founder of a solitary insect. | Orchard breeding units identify queen status, mating evidence, worker/brood support and transferred provisions. Two crop workers do not form a complete colony. A divided colony transfers actual individuals and leaves an actual remainder. | Give the colony specialist a genuine productive role without imposing that role on every sprint racer. |
| W16.AMEND.06 | All beneficial foot behavior could be reduced to adhesive strength. | Glassfoot anatomy distinguishes load-bearing friction pads and terminal attachment/release anatomy; preparation, orientation and working load matter to the final contact set. A comparison states which structure it measures. | Preserve the tire-equivalent choice through insect anatomy rather than copying a generic grip bonus. |
| W16.AMEND.07 | A durable plate could be interpreted as proving that its living root never needs care. | The rigid/elastic core and living interface have separate condition requirements. Service can retain an original core while replacing a separately sourced interface, with that replacement disclosed. | Enable repairs and heritage objects without infinite rare-tissue renewal. |
| W16.AMEND.08 | Loss of a working limb could make the whole donor worthless or guarantee that it remains a useful breeder. | Assess the remaining anatomy, reproduction requirements and support capacity of the specific form. A damaged but reproductively capable specimen can matter; lost or impaired reproductive function is not ignored. | Keep alternative uses honest and prevent every damaged capture becoming guaranteed cash or useless debt. |
| W16.AMEND.09 | A specialized site could become a location that adds a gene when visited. | Local frequency, source history, developmental conditions and current observations remain separate. The same site can yield ordinary or exceptional animals; a project selection never edits the existing population. | Knowledge improves search and selection while captures retain stable identities. |
| W16.AMEND.10 | Maintenance could be understood as freezing active juveniles or all expenses. | Maintenance halts new starts, not current development. Appropriate holding, transition capacity and support remain actual obligations. Default offline pause is a player-time rule; it does not selectively advance output while stopping care. | Carry the milestone’s accessible stopping rule without inventing costless biology. |
| W16.AMEND.11 | A positive receipt or a post-sale cash balance could be presented as a sustainable business. | State scope, paid operator remuneration, operating outlays, capital, retained inventory, accepted demand and cash timing. Distinguish a conditional operating surplus from lifetime return or equilibrium prices. Retain the Wave12 negative example unchanged. | Produce usable budget design rather than retroactively hiding the known loss. |
| W16.AMEND.12 | A attractive marking or historic tin could need a fabricated speed or universal price multiplier to be considered useful. | Appearance, provenance and applied finish have their own intended buyers and completed display outcomes. Natural variation needs its own evidence before inheritance is claimed. No exact antique is made genuine by an anniversary label. | Preserve collector and finisher livelihoods alongside racing and production. |
