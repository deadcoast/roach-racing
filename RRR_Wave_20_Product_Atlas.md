**Working compendium edition.** Author premises govern; named amendments replace only their stated scopes. External research, historical experiments, new design and open author choices remain separate. No application or historical world state is changed.

### Drive: usable pace, force and efficiency

`W20.ADVANCE.DRIVE`

**Accessible baseline.** A complete native drive or an ordinary measured Flat-Six/Redline preparation supplies an actual running system.

**Genuine improvement.** A selected Redline retains the required loaded cadence and force while using less reserve per defined work sequence. That is a genuine useful improvement even before a band label changes.

**A different useful choice.** A Torque Bundle can suit loaded starts through different output characteristics; a ratio change remains a separate setup choice.

**Selection project.** Seek efficient cadence under comparable burden and condition. A visibly frantic cicada or a recently fed specimen is not the finished specification.

**What later racing must show.** Matched configurations sustain the same duty with different supply use, or show the actual changed pace when cadence changes. Do not claim both from a one-variable result.

**Ordinary output and service.** Ordinary reliable drives feed accessible tins. A binding external sleeve can be serviced without replacing a healthy premium drive.

**Boundary.** Profile names and grade bands do not themselves alter output or certify every descendant.


<a id="w20-advance-contact"></a>

### Contact: purchase, release and contamination

`W20.ADVANCE.CONTACT`

**Accessible baseline.** Native feet or ordinary Sticktoe stock support a known clean surface.

**Genuine improvement.** A selected contact set releases faster while retaining sufficient purchase at comparable contact area and load. It need not acquire an unrelated penalty.

**A different useful choice.** A channelled face or Cleat preparation addresses a different contaminated or loose surface, through actual geometry and material.

**Selection project.** Complement a high-purchase line with a quick-release individual; later compare retained performance after the declared fouling exposure.

**What later racing must show.** Show changed planted/release behavior or reduced actual slipping. A faster motor is not added to explain the contact improvement.

**Ordinary output and service.** Clean-course ordinary descendants remain useful; permitted alternative pads supply brake work. Cleaning restores current state, not ancestry.

**Boundary.** A pristine laboratory surface result cannot silently become an all-surface guarantee.


<a id="w20-advance-delivery"></a>

### Linkages: chosen operating ranges with better execution

`W20.ADVANCE.DELIVERY`

**Accessible baseline.** Native linkage or ordinary Lowstep has a usable force/travel range.

**Genuine improvement.** Within the chosen range, smoother supported articulation and more consistent useful travel improve delivery without relabeling a higher ratio as quality.

**A different useful choice.** Longthrow changes working stroke and returning clearance; Lockstep changes paired timing. They are not consecutive numbered gear tiers.

**Selection project.** Seek stable root geometry and low-loss linings, or the missing timing contribution, under the same intended load.

**What later racing must show.** The limbs complete the actual stroke and return; a ratio change visibly trades its appropriate motion characteristics.

**Ordinary output and service.** Several printed fit ranges create legitimate ordinary product demand. Sleeves are a useful small maintenance product when anatomically unallocated.

**Boundary.** One linked assembly does not supply its complete joints twice as separate paid components.


<a id="w20-advance-landing"></a>

### Hoppers: spectacular launch and usable return

`W20.ADVANCE.LANDING`

**Accessible baseline.** An ordinary complete Hopper set can perform a controlled jump; its joints are included once.

**Genuine improvement.** A selected pair gives tighter release or quicker useful settling at the same actual working demand. A combined high-return controlled pair is a legitimate ambitious target.

**A different useful choice.** A joint-only Springknee preparation supports landing without granting active launch; a Keel addresses rebound through a separate source.

**Selection project.** Retain missing contributions, track actual combinations in descendants, and maintain only the chosen replacement reserve.

**What later racing must show.** Loading, release, clearance, touchdown and recovered running must all be visible where the scenario enables them.

**Ordinary output and service.** Useful intermediate pairs supply ordinary tins. Some joints remain suitable for receiving products even when the full jump objective is not met.

**Boundary.** No guaranteed pass from an item name and no forced unique obstacle that only the premium pair can clear.

### Structure: useful load paths at appropriate mass

`W20.ADVANCE.SUPPORT`

**Accessible baseline.** The native body or ordinary Flexrail/Boxbrace supports the intended roots.

**Genuine improvement.** Improved source material can retain the required load and motion at lower carried mass or support more useful work at the same burden.

**A different useful choice.** Articulated protective plate patterns offer different coverage; protection remains separate from frame support.

**Selection project.** Compare material and geometry with movement permitted; do not select maximum static rigidity alone.

**What later racing must show.** The intended limb, latch or wing motion remains available under the required load, rather than a whole-body armor animation.

**Ordinary output and service.** Fit-range consistency can make ordinary cohorts commercially valuable. A replaceable common interface can preserve an exceptional structural core.

**Boundary.** A bridle drawing is not extra material, and a brace cannot avoid Fireworks protection limits merely through its name.


<a id="w20-advance-reserve"></a>

### Supply: capacity, delivery and usable contents

`W20.ADVANCE.RESERVE`

**Accessible baseline.** Native reserves or ordinary crop/fatbody preparations support the chosen first-use duty.

**Genuine improvement.** A selected reserve can provide more usable capacity at comparable mass, or adequate delivery through a less restrictive interface. These are separately measured achievements.

**A different useful choice.** A compact crop favors a particular high-delivery duty; a large reserve favors another carrying/consumption plan.

**Selection project.** Evaluate source tissue at a known stage and current fill. Retain larval replacements for reproduction before harvesting every suitable store.

**What later racing must show.** Actual contents deplete and delivered work remains within output limits; an empty excellent reserve is still empty.

**Ordinary output and service.** Ordinary appropriate reserves supply many kits. Filling, valve service and new membrane acquisition are distinct useful offers.

**Boundary.** A reservoir does not supply free Boiler contents or double as a complete Keel made from the same entire fatbody.


<a id="w20-advance-tactic"></a>

### Contact tactics: control the encounter

`W20.ADVANCE.TACTIC`

**Accessible baseline.** An ordinary supported Bullhorn or Catchclaw construction expresses the founding derby identity.

**Genuine improvement.** Useful root strength, closure or release can improve under the same operating circumstances without an unrelated drawback.

**A different useful choice.** A forked contact shape, shorter grasp envelope, support-only conversion or properly supported recovery latch creates a different interaction.

**Selection project.** Separate donor decision timing, anatomy, reproductive contributions and the actual fitted load path.

**What later racing must show.** Contact happens at reachable anatomy; loading, hold, release and reaction are visible. The roach still wins by finishing.

**Ordinary output and service.** Size-appropriate ordinary arms and horns serve beginner derby kits; rematching and root services preserve valuable cores.

**Boundary.** No native trait imports the donor’s complete intelligence and no offense stat deals damage through a missed target.


<a id="w20-advance-flight"></a>

### Flight: full working systems, not a higher wing number

`W20.ADVANCE.FLIGHT`

**Accessible baseline.** A complete supported Courtyard kit makes aerial participation possible without a private aquatic nursery.

**Genuine improvement.** Improved root response, useful surface behavior or drive efficiency can improve a matched construction through its actual function.

**A different useful choice.** Longwing and Quadwing require different working arrangements; phase, folding and reserve choices remain operating decisions, not universal ranks.

**Selection project.** Choose the missing contribution while separately controlling emergence, transport and fitting conditions.

**What later racing must show.** Launch, powered passage, gate traversal, approach and receiving must correspond to the supplied arrays and controls.

**Ordinary output and service.** Conditioned ordinary arrays, emergence services and real replacement/matching work all have users. Natural pattern can support a separate collection.

**Boundary.** An excellent wing without a working root or drive is not a complete Flyer, and a ground brake cannot slow it in empty air.

### Tin Can Special / First Bench

`W20.PRODUCT.TIN` · **complete living kit**

**Provider and customer:** Two Nets or an equivalent ordinary Wrangloid; a first-time local racer.

**Purpose:** Complete one personally built racer and a useful first-use session without acquiring a breeding business.

| Function | Actual supplied or retained content |
|---|---|
| Host | One mature Lowline host |
| Drive | Native working drive |
| Linkage | Native linkage |
| Contact | One selected Sticktoe preparation; other native contacts retained |
| Structure/protection | Native frame and declared Raceskin |
| Reserve | Native reserve, initial contents supplied |
| Balance/control | Native sensing and response |
| Receiving/damping | Native joints; no new damper implied |
| Special anatomy | No dedicated weapon required; an appropriately scoped horn or Hopper entry is a separate printed variant |
| Finish/support | Assembly guide, chosen number/finish sheet and normal initial support |

**Builder decisions.** Place the supplied contacts, retain clearance, choose a finish and run. The guide does not require replacing every native role.

**Racing opportunity.** A supported mixed Local practice course. The tin promises a usable project, not a first-race win.

**Ordinary included support.** One declared initial fitting/receiving allowance and a short condition comparison; normal supplies listed before purchase.

**Additional-work boundary.** New raw grafting, missing replacements or unusual repairs are separate offers. An ordinary first test does not require premium lineage certification.

**Breeding and supply purpose.** Dependable whole hosts and ordinary acceptable contact stock. No founder-quality genetics or every display band is needed.

**Other legitimate participation.** Buy the tin; supply an eligible owned host to a conversion service; or self-source compatible material.

**What the object should communicate.** A low host compartment beside one protected contact well; the lid shows what the novice will actually assemble.

**Acceptance definition.** The real contents fit the supplied host, the receiving contacts work, and the owner can use the project before choosing a new occupation.
