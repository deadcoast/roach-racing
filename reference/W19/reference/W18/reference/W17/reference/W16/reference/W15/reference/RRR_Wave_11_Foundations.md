## 3. Playable route

**Workbench:** keep H-6, fit the owned UPGRADE-DRIVE and W05.PADS, then commit. **Club meets:** inspect the card and enter First Current. Run a round, watch the actual heats, and return to the meet board. Refit the same host between rounds or continue. Three rounds settle the complete table. A qualifying win grants the Regional invitation, trophy and a choice of one protected donor pair. This is an observed useful starter route, not a universal winning prescription.

A meet comprises four entrants. The existing engine races two at a time. Each round computes both pairings, each in both starting orientations, for four actual heats. The player runs two of those. Six pairings over three rounds produce twelve heats in total. No four-way collision simulation or fake scores are claimed.

Every rival is compiled from individually identified donor anatomy using the same component-descriptor and fitting pipeline as the player. Inspector measurements are disclosed authored club-stock observations. The rival does not become stronger merely because the player raises a grade, changes an unmeasured genotype or wins an unrelated race. Public target suggestions can be sent to the existing line-selection controls and route browser.

The Hopper build is an inspectable movement construction, not a guarantee of a takeoff in every heat. Each official card has three sponsored editions; the custom invitational has two. These are finite local content and funding, not a world-wide limit on racing. Other practice, buying, catching, breeding and income activities remain available. An exhibition on player geometry cannot award an official progression gate.

Standing order: points, then completed heats, then shared rank. Valid wins score 3; equal valid finish times score 1 each; an unfinished racer earns 0. An all-retired heat has no winner. A qualifying first-rank result must include at least three actual heat wins. Shared ranks split occupied purse positions equally; unassigned or indivisible remainder returns to the club. The points, minimum wins and payouts are explicit RRR proposals, not quoted PCR formulas.

The 60-credit award is gross prize income, not 60 credits of profit. All accounts, including the declared external counter-account, preserve the inherited total of 362.

The sponsor received a finite 1440-credit operating endowment; it is not player earnings. Prize reservations do not advance time. Each completed round costs 2 credits and 2 world turns plus actual care. The service covers the profile’s repeated preparation; individual reserve-tissue decay or automatic damage is not introduced. Cancellation of failed computation costs no round attendance. Voluntary withdrawal retains incurred costs, uses the edition and refunds unawarded sponsor money.

The first Local Open crown permits one claim of an existing Hopper or Glassfoot A/B pair. Hoppers supply high return for continued synchronization selection; Glassfoot stock supplies fast release for further purchase selection. The pair changes ownership and is protected. Capacity is checked. No gene or species becomes newly legal because the player won. Club custody of unclaimed specimens is a declared supported nursery rather than a complete simulated institutional budget.

Further actual probes completed Glassline, Fireworks, the owned-course invitational and the Regional Trial. The same improved starter build won the first three test configurations; it placed second on the longer Regional course. Fireworks produced both ram and claw contacts. These are actual scenario outcomes, not difficulty calibration or guarantees.

### Format

**W11.FORMAT.01** — Four entrants contest three round-robin rounds using the current two-racer engine.
Purpose: A roster of four is not falsely displayed as simultaneous four-roach physics.

**W11.FORMAT.02** — Every pairing has two standing-start heats with swapped sides.
Purpose: Neither entrant permanently receives one favorable starting position.

**W11.FORMAT.03** — Also reverse solver evaluation order in the second heat.
Purpose: The probe reduces order bias without claiming perfect physical symmetry.

**W11.FORMAT.04** — Use 3 points for a valid heat win, 1 each for a valid tied finish and 0 for a loss or no finish.
Purpose: Contact and damage do not score championship points.

**W11.FORMAT.05** — All-retired heats award no win and no tie point.
Purpose: Last-roach-standing does not replace completing the race.

**W11.FORMAT.06** — Rank by points, then number of valid finishes; otherwise share rank.
Purpose: Arbitrary IDs or summed times across different rival encounters do not break the tie.

**W11.FORMAT.07** — A qualifying crown requires first rank and at least three actual heat wins.
Purpose: An all-tied low-evidence table cannot automatically grant a championship invitation.

**W11.FORMAT.08** — Results use identical frozen course and build inputs and required gate completion.
Purpose: Client place labels cannot override finishing times.

**W11.FORMAT.09** — Four actual computed heats settle one round.
Purpose: NPC-versus-NPC results are simulated rather than inferred from labels.

## 9. Limits

**Boundary:** Runtime is based on the recovered Wave09 executable. Wave10 maker source and final archive were not recovered. Its written specification remains an explicit pending handoff.

**Boundary:** One local world; four-entry meets are sequences of two-roach races, not simultaneous four-roach physics.

**Boundary:** All tournament structure, costs, rates, thresholds, native profiles and inheritance values are authored normalized experiments, not exact PCR game data.

**Boundary:** Six fixed component-built NPCs; their prior breeding, purchases, training and supplier replenishment are not simulated.

**Boundary:** Two useful qualification stages only. Factional and World City events and powered flight remain unimplemented.

**Boundary:** Standing-start mirrored heats reduce one bias; path-space body contact, loading, airborne tactics and timing need further testing. No perfectly fair or calibrated tournament claim.

**Boundary:** Finite disclosed sponsorship and award nursery; club-owned care is bundled into supplied operating resources rather than a complete simulated institution.

**Boundary:** No globally imposed death, tissue-regrowth or storage policy. Earlier reusable-collar branch remains restricted.

**Boundary:** Snapshots and local backups are consistency-checked, not anti-cheat. Replay frames are session-only.

**Boundary:** Browser tested through exact HTML set_content when native file navigation was blocked. No native Windows/macOS, physical touch or human usability session.

**Boundary:** No measured enjoyment, retention, national economy balance or world-scale performance.
