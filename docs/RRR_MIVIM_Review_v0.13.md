# MIVIM — Lucky 13 / Wildlands

**Objective:** Finding and preserving a particular living source becomes a playable, evidence-bound activity feeding the existing breeding, material preparation, personal kit, maker, trading and race systems.

**Comparator:** A route button returns anonymous part rolls; lure or capture upgrades secretly improve genes; an exploration minigame pays unrelated points; a new expansion discards prior inventory.

**Admission:** quality at declared scope AND novel-development adherence. No numeric weights or private MIVIM grammar is fabricated.

| Criterion | Finding | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | Spatial approach, line of sight, tool risk, case custody, persistence and actual-control field-to-race path checked. Desktop and narrow layouts inspected. | Human usability, native platforms, real touch and final art are open. |
| Novel-development adherence | A concrete project-specific connection makes existing living sources individually approachable, selectable, preservable and usable rather than adding a luck aura. | No claim of worldwide originality. |
| Effectiveness | Caught stock becomes the same sale, brood parent or prepared race component; existing home production advances on the same clock. | A successful test route is not a guaranteed win, breeding result or income. |
| Creativity | Purposeful anatomical fieldwork and a specimen-case interface connect population composition, uncertain observations and material-oriented kit choices. | Player-perceived distinctiveness and pleasure unmeasured. |
| Scaling potential | Declarative patches reuse existing forms, traits, preparation and operators; discrete state and canonical identity support later expansion. | One local world, bounded archive, small boards; no large-world benchmark. |

**Supporting work:** Schema checks, migration comparisons, old-module hashes, model cases and browser evidence protect the integrated feature. They do not establish addictive gameplay or professional final visual quality.

**Observed negative/limited results:** A highly synchronized catch was not the fastest complete racer in its tested build. Bulk collecting can lose cash and leave damaged care obligations. The field-screen model has deliberate uncertainty and cannot certify a maker order. Legacy quick-route and spatial populations remain separate authored sources.

**Carried ambition:** Better limb/race fidelity, wider ecological continuity, repeat production economics and human participation remain required. No quality gate is closed by adding more catalogue names or passing more parsers.
