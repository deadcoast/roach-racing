# RRR — Wave v0.17

## Editorial Amendments and Interpretation Changes

**Authority and scope.** The explicit author premise governs. W15’s participation and progression revisions remain in force; W14 supplies the common anatomy and W16 the first donor/material volume. W17 refines only the named ground-source, preparation, receiving and repeat-work questions. New organisms, populations, inherited tendencies, material procedures, customer terms and prices are authored working fiction. Research supports the stated reference function or narrow biological observation—not real graft viability, balanced prices, universal heredity or measured player engagement. No game application is changed.

These changes apply to the named current definitions only. Earlier .spec records, prototype coefficients, owners, genes and race results remain preserved. The new material classifications describe what an actual preparation includes; they do not independently charge its mass several times.

<a id="w17-amend-01"></a>

### Observed strike versus arm response

**Earlier references.** [W14.ACTION.GRASP](reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-action-grasp) · [W16.PREP.CATCHCLAWS](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-catchclaws)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A short or long observed strike can include different decision timing, extension and closure.

**Current definition.** Record these separately. Select useful anatomy with controlled observations while the host supplies actual sensing and control.

**Purpose.** Prevents hereditary arm ratings from absorbing the entire donor’s behavior.

<a id="w17-amend-02"></a>

### Horn geometry versus unconditional penalty

**Earlier references.** [W14.PART.RAM](reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-ram) · [W15.GRADES.06](reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-grades-06)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Large horns may be treated as either automatically powerful or automatically burdened with dramatic drag.

**Current definition.** Use actual contact shape, direction, mass, support and flow interaction. Preserve genuine improvements without inventing an unrelated penalty.

**Purpose.** Keeps the original exciting horn builds meaningful without lazy longer-is-better or bigger-is-always-worse rules.

<a id="w17-amend-03"></a>

### Counterbrace actuation and material class

**Earlier references.** [W16.PREP.COUNTERBRACES](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-counterbraces) · [W16.MATERIAL.ACTIVE](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-active)

**Earlier ambiguity.** The material index lists elastic and structural needs but does not say who deploys an active support.

**Current definition.** Add a deployment declaration: passive contact, host-driven or included powered anatomy. Only the included-powered branch adds active tissue requirements.

**Purpose.** No deleted attack is mistaken for free deployment power. This refines rather than discards the old material classes.

<a id="w17-amend-04"></a>

### Source pair versus working pair

**Earlier references.** [W16.MATERIAL.SENSORY](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-sensory) · [W16.MATERIAL.ELASTIC](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-elastic)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Matched can be read as necessarily same-donor or as guaranteed by common origin.

**Current definition.** Keep source identity and pair calibration separate. A mixed-source tested pair is legitimate and a same-donor damaged pair can fail.

**Purpose.** Creates real rematching work and correct provenance without species or family bans.

<a id="w17-amend-05"></a>

### Moth covering versus brake material

**Earlier references.** [W14.PART.FELTBRAKE](reference/W16/reference/W15/reference/RRR_Wave_14_Foundations.md#w14-part-feltbrake) · [W16.PREP.FELTBRAKE](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-feltbrake)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Real moth scales can be mistaken for proof that loose scales are a useful contact brake.

**Current definition.** Sandfelt has an explicitly fictional supported ventral adaptation. The preparation includes face, backing, root and geometry; acoustic research supplies no brake coefficient.

**Purpose.** Prevents an attractive biological analogy from masquerading as evidence.

<a id="w17-amend-06"></a>

### Stage and adult feeding by donor

**Earlier references.** [W16.DONOR.WAXMOTH](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-donor-waxmoth)

**Earlier ambiguity.** The nonfeeding adult Reservoir profile could spread to all moth donors.

**Current definition.** Sandfelt and Ashveil have separately authored adult feeding and developmental profiles. Neither inherits Reservoir tissue products or life timing by the word moth.

**Purpose.** Maintains actual source diversity.

<a id="w17-amend-07"></a>

### Load face and condition are product properties

**Earlier references.** [W16.PREP.RAILSHOES](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-railshoes) · [W16.MATERIAL.STRUCTURE](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-structure)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A shell’s shiny outside or water response may be treated as a sliding measurement.

**Current definition.** Name outside/backing, direction, support and actual rail-condition test. Preserve inspection limits.

**Purpose.** Makes surface selection and carrying requirements purposeful.

<a id="w17-amend-08"></a>

### Interface lists must state complete anatomy

**Earlier references.** [W16.PREP.LOWSTEP](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-lowstep) · [W16.PREP.LONGTHROW](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Material_Register.md#w16-prep-longthrow)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Material shorthand can omit rigid levers or included tips while whole preparations retain the work.

**Current definition.** W17 sheets add structural needs to Lowstep and contact needs to Longthrow where those elements are included; standalone alternatives use only real unallocated source.

**Purpose.** Avoids both missing material obligations and double product yields.

<a id="w17-amend-09"></a>

### Working condition does not erase a useful object

**Earlier references.** [W16.MATERIAL.03](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-03) · [W15.SURPLUS.04](reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-surplus-04)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A component can be treated as wholly worthless or still fully certified after a local defect.

**Current definition.** Distinguish ready, recoverable service, changed-use proposal, working rejection and retained history. New use or repair needs actual material and consent.

**Purpose.** Supports recovery, collection and honest trade without universal salvage buyers.

<a id="w17-amend-10"></a>

### Future budgets retain unsold stock

**Earlier references.** [W16.ECON.KITS](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-econ-kits) · [W16.ECON.FITTING](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-econ-fitting) · [W15.LIVELIHOOD.04](reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-livelihood-04)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A good one-period remainder can hide later storage, failed work or repeated capitalization.

**Current definition.** Carry exact opening balances, listed outlays, delivered receipts and actual stock across the next periods; report negative branches and paid operator labor.

**Purpose.** Extends rather than recalculates W16 or the historical W12 loss.

<a id="w17-amend-11"></a>

### Arrival is a new observation, not a second genetic draw

**Earlier references.** [W16.MATERIAL.12](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-12) · [W15.CUSTOMERS.07](reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-customers-07)

**Earlier ambiguity.** A receiving test can be framed as another quality roll or a race guarantee.

**Current definition.** It observes the same material in its current condition using the agreed method. It neither rerolls genes nor guarantees the customer wins.

**Purpose.** Preserves both useful uncertainty and stable identity.

<a id="w17-amend-12"></a>

### Price the useful service, not every confirmation

**Earlier references.** [W15.BENCH.01](reference/W16/reference/W15/RRR_Wave_15_Foundations.md#w15-bench-01) · [W16.MATERIAL.11](reference/W16/RRR_Wave_16_Foundations.md#w16-material-11)

**Earlier ambiguity.** Dispatch, receiving, fitting and observation could accumulate into compulsory separate appointments.

**Current definition.** A normal ready kit includes an intelligible receiving and first-use allowance. Only new work or changed scope adds a visible quote.

**Purpose.** The deeper source and transport system must not obstruct the initial build-and-race pleasure.
