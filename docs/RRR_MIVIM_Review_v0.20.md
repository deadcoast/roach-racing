**Scope:** researched compendium milestone, not executable development. No numerical weighted MIVIM grade is invented.

**Finding:** Current cases specify responses to ambiguous condition, repair, product, source and sporting questions.

**Limit:** No human comprehension, race benefit or profitable-life evidence is claimed.

**Limit:** No exhaustive novelty search or measured player-perceived distinctiveness.

### Scaling Potential

**Finding:** Current routes and shared identity/condition distinctions support additional donors and institutions without repeated contradictory definitions.

**Limit:** No large-agent retrieval benchmark, autonomous economy or runtime-scale test.

### Medium And Finish

**Finding:** Readable current desk edition, specific objects and accessible normal-service route; detailed anatomy remains available rather than removed.

**Limit:** No rendered in-game interface or final production artwork delivered.

## Editorial admission

The manuscript and structured reference were checked for exact prior references, unique current identifiers, local links, component coverage, product roles, arithmetic and unchanged baseline files. These necessary checks cannot establish quality of the eventual game. The source research altered the repair definition; none of its percentages or times was imported as a game coefficient.

The milestone’s substantive work is the joined current reference and the actual decision chain through a setback—not its word count, archive size or number of rules. Unresolved life-course choices remain explicitly unresolved.
