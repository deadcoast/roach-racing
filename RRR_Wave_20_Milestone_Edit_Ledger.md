These twenty edits distinguish a demonstrated inconsistency, an incompletely developed connection, and a risk prevented by the current definition. They are not twenty claims that earlier authors implemented a defect. W15–W19 already solved substantial parts of the problem; those solutions are retained and made operational in the current reading.
