## Research Register: Source Functions, Biological Findings and Design Inference

Research is used narrowly. RRR organisms, grafts, product requirements, locomotion rules and services are authored fiction. No source establishes transplanted flight, the imagined species’ coefficients, balanced prices or player retention. No outside figure is reproduced.

**Reading scope.** Primary texts and author abstracts were used. Where search provided an indexed abstract but a direct opening failed or returned no text, that limit is recorded. The click-beetle correction is included; the figure itself was not used. Linked videos and PDF tables were not treated as inspected evidence.

### Walker, Thomas & Taylor (2009) — Deformable wing kinematics in the desert locust

`W18.S06`

**Observed.** Tethered desert-locust observations describe changing camber and twist, stereotyped hindwing motion and more variable forewing motion.

**Design use.** Give Longwing separate forewing and fan-hindwing roles, working deformation and supported folding.

**Limit.** One studied species and tethered conditions do not prove RRR flight efficiency, transferable wings, or a universally optimal configuration.

**Read scope.** Originating university publication abstract, DOI 10.1098/rsif.2008.0435.
