## Purposeful fitting and race behavior

A prepared stalk converts a change along its adjustment slot into pad clearance; the inspector reports the resulting height. This is a declared linkage, not a free stat reward for dragging. Inherited material friction and compression are unchanged. Raised approach contact removes motion through friction. Airborne brake contact is absent. The same race reserve never receives energy from braking.

The passive ramp splits existing ground speed into forward and vertical components, with an authored height after an uphill-load term. It does not spend an active Hopper charge. Reaching a curve before landing retires the racer in this bounded path model; it cannot steer through that curve while airborne. Broader jump physics, retrieval and damage remain separate work.

Rail shoes have a friction coefficient, support capacity, mass and lateral extension. Contact loss depends on what the pair supports. Existing feet and muscles provide motion; guide material does not supply another source of force. Ride the rails requests an outer line on curves; natural outward slip can also cause wall contact. Away from a wall the friction value changes nothing.

## Executed comparisons
